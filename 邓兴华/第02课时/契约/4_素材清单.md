## 二、语法素材

### G04 指示代词矩阵

| | 近处 | 远处 |
|---|------|------|
| 单数 | this | that |
| 复数 | these | those |

### G04 问答句型

| 问句 | 答句 |
|------|------|
| Is this your father? | Yes, it is. / No, it isn't. |
| Is that your mother? | Yes, it is. / No, it isn't. |
| Are these your brothers? | Yes, they are. / No, they aren't. |
| Are those your sisters? | Yes, they are. / No, they aren't. |

### G05 公式矩阵

| 句型 | 公式 | 示例 |
|------|------|------|
| 肯定句 | 主语 + be + ... | This is my father. |
| 否定句 | 主语 + be + not + ... | This isn't my father. |
| 一般疑问句 | Be + 主语 + ...? | Is this your father? |
| 肯定回答 | Yes, 主语 + be. | Yes, it is. |
| 否定回答 | No, 主语 + be + not. | No, it isn't. |

### G06 句型矩阵

| 问句类型 | 示例问句 | 示例回答 |
|----------|----------|----------|
| Who is + 单数主语? | Who is that man? | He is my father. |
| Who are + 复数主语? | Who are those people? | They are my parents. |
| Who + be + 人名? | Who is Lily? | She is my sister. |

## 三、阅读素材

### A篇：My Family Photo Album（65词）

```
Hello! This is a photo of my family. This is my father. He is a teacher. That is my mother. She is kind. These are my brothers. They are tall. Those are my sisters. They are cute. I love my happy family.
```

### A篇题目

1. 细节题：Who is the father? (A. A teacher B. A doctor C. A student)
2. 细节题：How are the brothers? (A. Short B. Tall C. Cute)
3. 推断题：How many people are in the photo? (A. 5 B. 6 C. 7)
4. 主旨题：What is the passage about? (A. A family photo B. A school day C. A friend)
5. 词汇题："cute" means? (A. 高的 B. 可爱的 C. 年轻的)
