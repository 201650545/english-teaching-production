📐 G49 · 复合不定代词分类表（1/3）
口诀 ★ 重点记忆
"some 开头用于肯定，any 开头用于否定疑问；no 开头表示全否定，every 开头表示全都含"
类别指人指物指地点 some-（肯定）someonesomethingsomewhere any-（否定/疑问）anyoneanythinganywhere no-（全否定）no onenothingnowhere every-（全包含）everyoneeverything—（everywhere 未列入）
G49 · 核心公式
some- → 肯定句
any- → 否定句 / 疑问句
no- → 全否定（= not any）
every- → 全包含
G49 Someone is exploring the park.（肯定句，指人）
G49 Is anyone planning to go abroad?（疑问句，指人）
G49 Nothing is more fun than a good weekend.（全否定，指物）
G49 Everything is ready for the trip.（全包含，指物）

🔍 G49 · 语义辨析矩阵（2/3）
不定代词语义用法示例 someone某人肯定句Someone is calling you. anyone任何人否定/疑问Is anyone here? no one没有人全否定No one knows the answer. everyone每个人全包含Everyone is happy. something某事肯定句I want something to drink. anything任何事否定/疑问Do you need anything? nothing没有什么全否定Nothing is impossible. everything一切全包含Everything is fine.
💡 特殊用法
some- 也可用于疑问句：表示请求/邀请时，用 some- 而非 any-（螺旋自 G33）
Would you like something to drink?（请求，用 something ✅）
Can someone help me?（请求，用 someone ✅）
中考辨析 ○ 理解即可
no one = not anyone：No one knows. = I don't know anyone.
nothing = not anything：I know nothing. = I don't know anything.
注意 ○ 理解即可
some- 用于疑问句的特殊场景：请求/邀请时不用 any-
Would you like something to eat? ✅（邀请）
Do you need anything to eat? ✅（普通疑问）

A Weekend to Remember
约 183 词

Last Saturday started like any other weekend. I was bored and had nothing to do. No one in my family had any plans. Then something interesting happened.

My friend Lisa called. "Are you doing anything this afternoon?" she asked. "No, nothing," I said. "Let's explore the old town!" she said. I love exploring new places, so I said yes right away.

We walked everywhere. Lisa seemed to know every corner of the town. She showed me an old building and a beautiful garden. "I wonder who lived here," she said. It was amazing to think that someone built this place long ago.

We stopped at a small cafe and had something to eat. The food was enjoyable. Lisa wrote about our adventure in her diary. "This is the best weekend ever," she said. "I don't want to go anywhere else."

That evening, I thought about everything we did. Sometimes the best weekends are the ones you don't plan. Now I always keep my diary ready for the next adventure!
