L12 语法回顾 · 冠词·可数不可数·some/any

🔄
G31 · 冠词 a/an/the 口诀
a 用辅音前，an 用元音前，the 特指这和那
泛指可数单加 a/an，特指上文提过的加 the
零冠词：不可数名词、复数名词泛指时
G32 · 可数不可数 + 量词口诀
可数名词有单复数（an egg → two eggs）
不可数名词只有单数（water, beef, sugar）
量词搭配：a cup of tea / a bowl of soup / a glass of juice
G33 · some vs. any 口诀
肯定句用 some：I'd like some tea. ✅
否定句用 any：I don't want any sugar. ✅
疑问句用 any：Do you have any water? ✅
💡 礼貌邀请用 some：Would you like some tea? ✅
💡 衔接导入

G34 · would like to do sth. 与 would like sth.

📐
口诀
would like 表意愿，想要某物直接接
想要做事加 to do，礼貌提出最得体
句型公式示例
would like sth.主语 + would like + 名词/代词I'd like a pancake. / She'd like some tea.
would like to do sth.主语 + would like to + 动词原形 + ...I'd like to buy some snacks.
would like sb. to do sth.主语 + would like + sb. + to + 动词原形I'd like you to try this dessert.
否定句主语 + wouldn't like + ...I wouldn't like any sugar.
疑问句Would + 主语 + like + ...?Would you like some dumplings?
缩写I would = I'd / She would = She'dI'd like a pancake.
💡 would like = want 的礼貌版本
want：直接、口语化（I want a pancake.）
would like：礼貌、委婉（I'd like a pancake. ✅ 更礼貌）
中考倾向：在对话和书信中优先使用 would like
例句1 · would like + 名词
I'd like a pancake.
我想要一个煎饼。
例句2 · would like to + 动词原形
She'd like to buy some snacks.
她想要买些零食。
例句3 · 疑问句（礼貌邀请用 some）
Would you like some dessert?
你想要些甜点吗？
G15 (L5) like to do→G18 (L6) want to do→G34 (L13) would like to do✅
🔒 防越级约束
仅限 would like 基础结构，不引入 feel like doing / would rather do
📝
例句4 · would like sb. to do
I'd like you to try this dumpling.
我想让你尝尝这个饺子。
例句5 · 否定句
I wouldn't like any sugar in my tea.
我不想在茶里加任何糖。
❌ 易错点
❌ I'd like pancake. → ✅ I'd like a pancake.（可数单数需冠词，螺旋自 G31）
❌ I would like eating snacks. → ✅ I would like to eat snacks.（would like to + 原形，不是 doing）
❌ Would you like any dessert? → ✅ Would you like some dessert?（礼貌邀请用 some，螺旋自 G33）
❌ I'd like to buys a snack. → ✅ I'd like to buy a snack.（to 后动词原形）
💡 中考辨析
would like vs. want：would like 更礼貌委婉，want 更直接——中考对话题优先选 would like
Would you like some...?：虽然是疑问句，但用 some 不用 any（礼貌邀请，螺旋自 G33）
I'd like = I would like：缩写形式常考，I'd 后接 like，不是 I'd to like ❌
would like to do vs. feel like doing：两者意思相同但结构不同——feel like doing 留至后续课

G35 · how much vs. how many 划线提问

📐
口诀
how much 问多少，不可数和价格
how many 问几个，可数复数才用它
疑问词用法划线提问对象示例
how much问不可数名词数量不可数名词How much sugar do you want?
how much问价格价格How much is the pancake?
how many问可数名词数量可数名词复数How many dumplings do you want?
📋 划线提问转换规则
原句划线部分提问句
I want two pancakes.two（可数数量）How many pancakes do you want?
I want some sugar.some（不可数数量）How much sugar do you want?
The pancake is five yuan.five yuan（价格）How much is the pancake?
The dumplings cost ten dollars.ten dollars（价格）How much do the dumplings cost?
例句1 · 问价格
How much is the pancake?
煎饼多少钱？
例句2 · 问不可数数量
How much sugar do you need?
你需要多少糖？
例句3 · 问可数数量
How many dumplings would you like?
你想要多少饺子？
G08 (L3) Where→G14 (L5) What→G22 (L9) 六宝系统→G35 (L13) how much/many
🔒 防越级约束
仅限 how much/how many 基础提问，不引入 how much + 比较级

G35 · 易错点 + 中考辨析

📝
❌ 易错点
❌ How many sugar do you want? → ✅ How much sugar do you want?（sugar 不可数）
❌ How much dumplings do you want? → ✅ How many dumplings do you want?（dumplings 可数）
❌ How much it costs? → ✅ How much does it cost?（疑问句需助动词 does）
❌ How many pancake? → ✅ How many pancakes?（how many 后接复数）
💡 中考辨析
how much 问价格 vs. 问数量：两种用法都是中考高频——看上下文判断
How much is it? → 问价格（It's five yuan.）
How much water do you want? → 问数量（A cup.）
how many 后必须接可数名词复数：How many pancake ❌ → How many pancakes ✅
how much 后必须接不可数名词：How much dumplings ❌ → How many dumplings ✅
🧠 本课词汇可数/不可数分类
可数名词（用 how many）不可数名词（用 how much）
pancake 🥞sugar 🍚
dumpling 🥟salt 🧂
snack 🍿oil 🛢️
dollar 💵dessert 🍰
—service 🙋

G36 · 基数词与货币/价格表达

📐
口诀
基数词表数量，dollar 美元 yuan 人民币
price is + 价格，cost/spend 各有法
表达方式结构示例
主语 + is/are + 价格The pancake is five yuan.The dumplings are ten dollars.
主语 + costs/cost + 价格sth. costs + 价格The pancake costs three yuan.
人 + spend + 价格 + on sth.sb. spends money on sth.I spent fifty yuan on snacks.
人 + pay + 价格 + for sth.sb. pays money for sth.I'll pay ten yuan for the dessert.
How much is/are...?问价格How much is the pancake?
How much does/do...cost?问价格How much does it cost?
📋 四种"花费"动词辨析
动词主语结构示例
cost物（商品）sth. costs sb. moneyThe pancake costs me five yuan.
spend人sb. spends money on sth.I spent fifty yuan on snacks.
pay人sb. pays money for sth.I'll pay ten yuan for the dessert.
buy人sb. buys sth. for moneyI bought a snack for three yuan.
💡 货币单位对照
人民币：yuan（¥）—— yuan 单复同形（five yuan ✅ / five yuans ❌）
美元：dollar(s)（$）—— dollar 有复数（five dollars ✅ / five dollar ❌）

G36 · 易错点 + 中考辨析

📝
例句1 · be 动词 + 价格
The pancake is five yuan.
煎饼五元。
例句2 · cost + 价格
The dumplings cost ten dollars.
饺子十美元。
例句3 · spend + on
I spent fifty yuan on snacks.
我花了五十元买零食。
例句4 · pay + for
I'll pay twenty yuan for the dessert.
我将付二十元买甜点。
❌ 易错点
❌ The pancake costs me five yuans. → ✅ The pancake costs me five yuan.（yuan 单复同形）
❌ I spent 50 yuan for snacks. → ✅ I spent 50 yuan on snacks.（spend 搭配 on）
❌ I paid 20 yuan on the dessert. → ✅ I paid 20 yuan for the dessert.（pay 搭配 for）
❌ The snack cost five dollar. → ✅ The snack costs five dollars.（dollar 需复数）
💡 中考辨析速记
cost 主语是物：The book costs 20 yuan. ✅ / I cost 20 yuan. ❌
spend 主语是人：I spent 20 yuan. ✅ / The book spent 20 yuan. ❌
pay 搭配 for：pay for sth. ✅ / pay on sth. ❌
yuan 无复数：five yuan ✅ / five yuans ❌
dollar 有复数：five dollars ✅
🔒 防越级约束
仅限基础价格表达（is/costs/spend/pay），不引入过去时态变化；不引入小数价格表达（$5.99等）

Last Sunday, Lily went to the supermarket with her mother. They wanted to buy some food for the week. Lily's mother took a wallet and a shopping list. At the supermarket, they first went to the food section. Lily chose some fresh fruits and vegetables. Her mother picked up some dessert and snacks. "How much do these cost?" Lily's mother asked the seller. "The total price is forty-five yuan," the seller said. Lily's mother paid fifty yuan. The seller gave her five yuan change. "Thank you! The service here is very good," Lily's mother said with a smile. Lily was happy because she spent a great morning with her mother. She learned how to bargain and how to choose fresh food. It was a special day for her.

Smart Shopping Tips for Teens
说明文 | 约150词 | 主题：青少年聪明购物建议
Do you like shopping? Here are some tips for teens to shop smart. First, always make a shopping list before you go to the supermarket. This helps you save money. When you have a list, you will not spend money on things you don't need. Second, look at the price before you buy things. Compare prices at different stores. Some stores sell the same thing at lower prices. You can also look for discounts to save money. Third, check the quality of the products. Sometimes cheap things are not good. It is better to buy fewer good things than many bad things. Fourth, always check your change after you pay. Make sure the seller gives you the right amount of money back. Shopping can be fun, but you should be careful with your money. The total amount you spend should be within your budget. Be a smart shopper!

💡 价格表达四件套
be动词：The pancake is 5 yuan.（物+be+价格）
cost：The pancake costs 5 yuan.（物+costs+价格）
spend：I spent 5 yuan on the pancake.（人+spend+on）
pay：I paid 5 yuan for the pancake.（人+pay+for）
