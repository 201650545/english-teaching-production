比较级构成

G55 规则

语
比较级构成 · 规则矩阵
G55 规则
单音节加 -er
tall → taller；nice → nicer（以 e 结尾加 r）；easy → easier（辅音+y 变 ier）；big → bigger（重读闭音节双写）。
多音节 more + 原级
popular → more popular；expensive → more expensive。
比较级 + than
A is taller than B；than 后接代词用宾格（He is taller than me.）。

阅读 C 篇

比较的意义

读
阅读 C 篇 · Why Comparisons Help
说明文
说明文：比较促进进步（约 215 词）
Why do we compare ourselves with others? Comparisons can be very helpful. When we compare our grades with a friend's, we can see our differences and find what to improve. For example, if your friend is more careful than you, you can learn to check your work more slowly. If someone is more creative, you can try new ideas and practice more. Comparisons also help us set goals. Seeing a better example makes us want to be better. But we should not compare to feel bad. The goal is to grow, not to feel jealous. In short, comparisons are useful when they push us to work harder and become a better version of ourselves.
主旨
比较帮助我们看清差距、设定目标、取得进步。
比较的主要好处是？
答案：improve
文中 'set a goal' 的意思是？
比较时应该避免什么心态？
答案：jealous
作者建议如何利用比较？
答案：find what to improve
比较让我们看到差 (gap) 并找到要 ____ (improve) 的地方。
答案：improve
