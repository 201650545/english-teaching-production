📐 G43 · 频度副词规则表（1/3）
口诀 ★ 重点记忆
"频度副词有级别，always 100% 到 never 0%；be 动词后面站，实义动词前面站"
位置规则（be后实前）
主语 + be + 频度副词 + ... → She is always happy.
主语 + 频度副词 + 实义动词 + ... → I often play sports.
主语 + 助动词 + 频度副词 + 实义动词 → She doesn't often watch TV.
句型公式示例
be 动词后主语 + be + 频度副词 + ...She is always happy.
实义动词前主语 + 频度副词 + 实义动词 + ...I often play sports.
助动词后主语 + 助动词 + 频度副词 + 实义动词Do you usually get up early?
句首/句尾Sometimes + 主语 + 动词...Sometimes I read books.
be后 She is usually full of energy.（be 动词后）
实前 I always jog in the morning.（实义动词前）
助后 Do you often read news online?（助动词后）
句首 Sometimes I watch television.（句首）

中考辨析 ▲ 难点
hardly vs. hard：hardly = 几乎不（否定含义）；hard = 努力地/困难的（肯定含义）
sometimes vs. sometime vs. some times：sometimes = 有时（频度）；sometime = 某时（时间点）；some times = 几次（次数）
⚠️ 易错点
❌ She always is happy.✅ She is always happy.（be 后不前）
❌ I play often sports.✅ I often play sports.（实前不后）
❌ I never don't eat breakfast.✅ I never eat breakfast.（never 本身否定）

A Health Survey in Our School
约 161 词 · 应用文

Last week, our school did a health survey. The results are very interesting. Here are some findings:

Eighty percent of students exercise regularly. They usually play basketball, run, or swim after school. Fifteen percent of students sometimes exercise, but not every day. Five percent of students never exercise. They hardly ever go outside to play.

How often do students watch television? Sixty percent of students watch TV every day. They often watch TV for more than two hours. Thirty percent of students sometimes watch TV. Only ten percent never watch TV.

What about online activities? Seventy percent of students go online every day. They usually read news or watch videos online. Twenty percent go online twice a week.

The survey shows that most students have good exercise habits, but many students spend too much time on TV and the Internet. A healthy lifestyle needs balance. We should exercise more and spend less time on screens.

One day, my teacher said, "You should try jogging. It's good for your health." I decided to try. I started to jog in the park every morning. At first, I could only run for five minutes. But I didn't give up. I jogged daily and regularly. After one month, I could run for twenty minutes.

Running changed my life. If you want to be healthy, start exercising today. You don't have to run fast. Just run regularly, and you will see the results.
