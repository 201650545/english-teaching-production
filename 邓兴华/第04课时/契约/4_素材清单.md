## 二、语法素材

### G10 名词复数规则变化矩阵

| 规则 | 词尾条件 | 变化方式 | 示例 |
|------|----------|----------|------|
| 规则1 | 一般情况 | 直接加 -s | room→rooms, book→books |
| 规则2 | -s/x/sh/ch 结尾 | 加 -es | box→boxes, watch→watches |
| 规则3 | 辅音+y 结尾 | 去 y 加 -ies | family→families, dictionary→dictionaries |
| 规则4 | 元音+y 结尾 | 直接加 -s | boy→boys, key→keys |

### G10 读音规则

| 词尾 | 读音 | 示例 |
|------|------|------|
| 清辅音后 | /s/ | books /buːks/, desks /desks/ |
| 浊辅音/元音后 | /z/ | rooms /ruːmz/, boys /bɔɪz/ |
| s/x/sh/ch 后加 es | /ɪz/ | boxes /ˈbɒksɪz/, watches /ˈwɒtʃɪz/ |

### G11 形容词用法矩阵

| 用法 | 结构 | 位置 | 示例 |
|------|------|------|------|
| 作定语 | a/an + 形容词 + 名词 | 名词前 | a tidy room, a clean desk |
| 作表语 | 主语 + be + 形容词 | be 动词后 | The room is tidy. |

### G12 方位介词矩阵

| 介词 | 含义 | 结构 | 示例 |
|------|------|------|------|
| in | 在……里面 | in + the + 名词 | in the room, in the box |

| under | 在……下面 | under + the + 名词 | under the bed, under the chair |
| behind | 在……后面 | behind + the + 名词 | behind the door, behind the sofa |
| next to | 紧挨着 | next to + the + 名词 | next to the window, next to the desk |
| in front of | 在……前面 | in front of + the + 名词 | in front of the TV |
| between...and | 在……和……之间 | between A and B | between the desk and the bed |

## 三、阅读素材

### A篇：A Clean and Tidy Room（86词，应用文）

```
This is my room. It is clean and tidy. A bed is in the room. A desk is next to the bed. My books are on the desk. A bookcase is behind the door. My schoolbag is under the chair. I always keep my room clean. This is a good habit.
```

### B篇：My Messy Brother's Room（107词，记叙文）

```
My brother's room is very messy. His books are everywhere in his room. His clothes are on the bed. His shoes are under the desk. His schoolbag is behind the door. But my room is always tidy. My books are in the bookcase. My clothes are in the wardrobe. My shoes are under the bed. I clean my room every weekend. My brother says he wants to keep his room tidy, but he always forgets. I tell him: "A tidy room is a good habit."
```

### C篇：How to Organize Your Room（107词，说明文）

```
Do you want a tidy room? First, put your books in the bookcase. Books everywhere make your room messy. Second, put your clothes in the wardrobe. Third, put your shoes under the bed or next to the door. Fourth, clean your desk every day. A clean desk helps you study better. Fifth, keep your room clean every week. A good habit makes a tidy room. A tidy room helps you find things quickly. You can organize your room today!
```
