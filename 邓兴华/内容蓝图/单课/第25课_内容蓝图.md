## 二、语法详解（3 考点）

### 语法①：动名词 V-ing 作主语与宾语（G62）——首次

**口诀**：
> "动名词，V-ing，作主语谓语单数；enjoy/finish/mind 后，doing 一定跟着走"

**公式矩阵**：

| 结构 | 公式 | 示例 |
|---|---|---|
| 动名词作主语 | V-ing + 谓语(单数) | Reading is fun. |
| enjoy/finish/mind + doing | 动词 + V-ing | I enjoy reading. |
| avoid/suggest/practice + doing | 动词 + V-ing | Avoid making mistakes. |

**常用只能接 doing 的动词**：enjoy, finish, mind, practice, suggest, avoid, consider, imagine, keep

**易错点**：
- ❌ enjoy to read → ✅ enjoy reading（enjoy 后接 doing）
- ❌ Reading are fun → ✅ Reading is fun（动名词作主语谓语单数）

**防越级约束**：**不引入分词作状语**（如 Seeing from the hill, ...）。

**螺旋递进链**：
```
G47 (L18) V-ing 变化规则 → G62 (L25) 动名词作主语/宾语（本课首次）
```

### 语法②：不定式 to do 作宾语与目的状语（G63）——首次

**口诀**：
> "不定式，to do，want/decide/hope 后头；表目的表将来，to do 用法记心怀"

**公式矩阵**：

| 结构 | 公式 | 示例 |
|---|---|---|
| want/decide/hope + to do | 动词 + to do | I want to learn English. |
| to do 作目的状语 | 主句 + to do | He came to help me. |
| 常用后接 to do 的动词 | want, decide, hope, plan, agree, refuse, promise, manage, afford, offer, fail, need | I agreed to help. |

**常用只能接 to do 的动词**：want, decide, hope, plan, agree, refuse, promise, manage, afford, offer, fail, need

**易错点**：
- ❌ I decided doing → ✅ I decided to do（decide 后接 to do）
- 目的状语用 to do，不用 doing

**螺旋递进链（to do 四阶链终点）**：
```
G15 (L5) like to do → G18 (L6) want to do → G34 (L13) would like to do
→ G63 (L25) to do 系统归纳（本课终点）
```

### 语法③：stop doing vs. stop to do / remember doing vs. remember to do（G63 复现扩展）

**口诀**：
> "stop 后 doing 是停止某事，to do 是停下来去做；remember 后 doing 记做过，to do 是记得要做"

**公式矩阵**：

| 结构 | 含义 | 示例 |
|---|---|---|
| stop doing | 停止正在做的事 | He stopped talking. |
| stop to do | 停下来去做另一件事 | He stopped to have a rest. |
| remember doing | 记得做过 | I remember seeing him. |
| remember to do | 记得要去做 | Remember to lock the door. |

**易错点**：
- ❌ stop to talk 误用（stop talking 才是停止说话）
- ❌ remember to do 误用（remember to do 是记得将要做）
