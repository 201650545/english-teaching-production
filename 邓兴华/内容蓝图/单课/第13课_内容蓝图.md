## 二、语法详案（3 考点）

### 语法①：would like to do sth. 与 would like sth.（G34）

**口诀**：
> "would like 表意愿，想要某物直接接；想要做事加 to do，礼貌提出最得体"

**公式矩阵**：

| 句型 | 公式 | 示例 |
|---|---|---|
| would like sth. | 主语 + would like + 名词/代词 | I'd like a pancake. / She'd like some tea. |
| would like to do sth. | 主语 + would like to + 动词原形 + ... | I'd like to buy some snacks. |
| would like sb. to do sth. | 主语 + would like + sb. + to + 动词原形 | I'd like you to try this dessert. |
| 否定句 | 主语 + wouldn't like + ... | I wouldn't like any sugar. |
| 疑问句 | Would + 主语 + like + ...? | Would you like some dumplings? |
| 缩写 | I would = I'd / She would = She'd | I'd like a pancake. |

> **would like = want 的礼貌版本**：
> - want：直接、口语化（I want a pancake.）
> - would like：礼貌、委婉（I'd like a pancake. ✅ 更礼貌）
> - **中考倾向**：在对话和书信中优先使用 would like

**例句（全部使用本课词汇）**：
1. I'd like **a pancake**.（would like + 名词）
2. She'd like **to buy** some snacks.（would like to + 动词原形）
3. Would you like **some dessert**?（疑问句，礼貌邀请用 some）
4. I'd like you **to try** this dumpling.（would like sb. to do）
5. I wouldn't like **any sugar** in my tea.（否定句）

**中考辨析**：
- **would like vs. want**：would like 更礼貌委婉，want 更直接——中考对话题优先选 would like
- **Would you like some...?**：虽然是疑问句，但用 some 不用 any（礼貌邀请，螺旋自 G33）
- **I'd like = I would like**：缩写形式常考，I'd 后接 like，不是 I'd to like ❌
- **would like to do vs. feel like doing**：would like to do = want to do；feel like doing = want to do（两者意思相同但结构不同）——feel like doing 留至后续课

**易错点**：
- ❌ I'd like a pancake. → ✅ I'd like a pancake.（正确）→ ❌ I'd like pancake.（可数单数需冠词，螺旋自 G31）
- ❌ I would like eating snacks. → ✅ I would like to eat snacks.（would like to + 原形，不是 doing）
- ❌ Would you like any dessert? → ✅ Would you like some dessert?（礼貌邀请用 some，螺旋自 G33）
- ❌ I'd like to buys a snack. → ✅ I'd like to buy a snack.（to 后动词原形）

**防越级约束**：仅限 would like 基础结构，不引入 feel like doing（留至后续课），不引入 would rather do（留至综合课）

**螺旋递进链（动词不定式 to do 三阶螺旋）**：
```
G15 (L5) like to do            ──首次接触──→
G18 (L6) want to do            ──螺旋复现──→
G34 (L13) would like to do     ──螺旋终点──→  三阶完成！
```

### 语法②：how much vs. how many 划线提问（G35）

**口诀**：
> "how much 问多少，不可数和价格；how many 问几个，可数复数才用它"

**公式矩阵**：

| 疑问词 | 用法 | 划线提问对象 | 示例 |
|---|---|---|---|
| **how much** | 问不可数名词数量 | 不可数名词 | How much sugar do you want? |
| **how much** | 问价格 | 价格 | How much is the pancake? / How much does it cost? |
| **how many** | 问可数名词数量 | 可数名词复数 | How many dumplings do you want? |
| **how much + 不可数名词** | How much + 不可数名词 + 一般疑问句? | — | How much water do you need? |
| **how many + 可数复数** | How many + 可数复数 + 一般疑问句? | — | How many eggs do you have? |

**划线提问转换规则**：

| 原句 | 划线部分 | 提问句 |
|---|---|---|
| I want **two** pancakes. | two（数量，可数） | How many pancakes do you want? |
| I want **some** sugar. | some（数量，不可数） | How much sugar do you want? |
| The pancake is **five yuan**. | five yuan（价格） | How much is the pancake? |
| The dumplings cost **ten dollars**. | ten dollars（价格） | How much do the dumplings cost? |

**例句（全部使用本课词汇）**：
1. **How much** is the pancake?（问价格）
2. **How much** sugar do you need?（问不可数数量）
3. **How many** dumplings would you like?（问可数数量）
4. **How much** does the snack cost?（问价格，cost 动词）
5. **How many** pancakes did you buy?（问可数数量）

**中考辨析**：
- **how much 问价格 vs. 问数量**：两种用法都是中考高频——看上下文判断
  - How much is it? → 问价格（It's five yuan.）
  - How much water do you want? → 问数量（A cup.）
- **how many 后必须接可数名词复数**：How many pancake ❌ → How many pancakes ✅
- **how much 后必须接不可数名词**：How much dumplings ❌ → How many dumplings ✅

**易错点**：
- ❌ How many sugar do you want? → ✅ How much sugar do you want?（sugar 不可数）
- ❌ How much dumplings do you want? → ✅ How many dumplings do you want?（dumplings 可数）
- ❌ How much it costs? → ✅ How much does it cost?（疑问句需助动词 does）
- ❌ How many pancake? → ✅ How many pancakes?（how many 后接复数）

**防越级约束**：仅限 how much/how many 基础提问，不引入 how much + 比较级（留至 L22）

**螺旋递进链（疑问词螺旋）**：
```
G08 (L3) Where 提问           ──首次接触──→
G14 (L5) What 提问             ──螺旋复现──→
G22 (L9) 疑问词六宝系统        ──螺旋递进──→
G35 (L13) how much/how many    ──螺旋终点──→
```

### 语法③：基数词与货币/价格表达（G36）

**口诀**：
> "基数词表数量，dollar 美元 yuan 人民币；price is + 价格，cost/spend 各有法"

**公式矩阵**：

| 表达方式 | 结构 | 示例 |
|---|---|---|
| 主语 + is/are + 价格 | The pancake is five yuan. / The dumplings are ten dollars. | 直接用 be 动词 |
| 主语 + costs/cost + 价格 | The pancake costs three yuan. / The snacks cost five dollars. | cost 物作主语 |
| 人 + spend + 价格 + on sth. | I spent fifty yuan on snacks. | spend 人作主语 |
| 人 + pay + 价格 + for sth. | I'll pay ten yuan for the dessert. | pay 人作主语 |
| How much is/are...? | How much is the pancake? / How much are the dumplings? | 问价格 |
| How much does/do...cost? | How much does it cost? / How much do they cost? | 问价格 |

**四种"花费"动词辨析**：

| 动词 | 主语 | 结构 | 示例 |
|---|---|---|---|
| **cost** | 物（商品） | sth. costs sb. money | The pancake costs me five yuan. |
| **spend** | 人 | sb. spends money on sth. / sb. spends time doing | I spent fifty yuan on snacks. |
| **pay** | 人 | sb. pays money for sth. | I'll pay ten yuan for the dessert. |
| **buy** | 人 | sb. buys sth. for money | I bought a snack for three yuan. |

> **中考高频辨析：cost vs. spend vs. pay**
> - **cost**：物作主语，不用被动语态——The book costs 20 yuan. ✅
> - **spend**：人作主语，搭配 on 或 doing——I spent 20 yuan on the book. ✅
> - **pay**：人作主语，搭配 for——I paid 20 yuan for the book. ✅

**货币单位对照**：

| 货币 | 单位 | 符号 | 示例 |
|---|---|---|---|
| 人民币 | yuan | ¥ | five yuan / ¥5 |
| 美元 | dollar(s) | $ | five dollars / $5 |

> **注意**：yuan 单复同形（five yuan ✅ / five yuans ❌）；dollar 有复数（five dollars ✅）

**例句（全部使用本课词汇）**：
1. The pancake is **five yuan**.（be 动词 + 价格）
2. The dumplings **cost** ten dollars.（cost + 价格）
3. I **spent** fifty yuan **on** snacks.（spend + on）
4. I'll **pay** twenty yuan **for** the dessert.（pay + for）
5. The total **is** thirty yuan.（total + be + 价格）

**中考辨析**：
- cost 主语是物：The book costs 20 yuan. ✅ / I cost 20 yuan. ❌
- spend 主语是人：I spent 20 yuan. ✅ / The book spent 20 yuan. ❌
- pay 搭配 for：pay for sth. ✅ / pay on sth. ❌
- yuan 无复数：five yuan ✅ / five yuans ❌
- dollar 有复数：five dollars ✅

**易错点**：
- ❌ The pancake costs me five yuans. → ✅ The pancake costs me five yuan.（yuan 单复同形）
- ❌ I spent 50 yuan for snacks. → ✅ I spent 50 yuan on snacks.（spend 搭配 on，不搭配 for）
- ❌ I paid 20 yuan on the dessert. → ✅ I paid 20 yuan for the dessert.（pay 搭配 for，不搭配 on）
- ❌ The snack cost five dollar. → ✅ The snack costs five dollars.（dollar 需复数，且三单 cost→costs 已学 G25）

**防越级约束**：仅限基础价格表达（is/costs/spend/pay），不引入过去时态的 cost/spend/pay 变化（cost→cost 不规则变化留至后续课）；不引入小数价格表达（$5.99 等）

**螺旋递进链（价格表达螺旋）**：
```
G36 (L13) 基数词+货币/价格表达    ──首次接触──→
后续: 比较级+价格对比              ──螺旋递进──→
G55+: 价格在阅读理解中的综合运用   ──螺旋终点──→
```

### 5.5 阅读指导五步法

2. **精读划关键词**：圈出价格数字、食物名词、动词（buy/spend/pay/cost/sell）
