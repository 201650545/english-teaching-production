## 二、语法详解（3 考点）

### 语法①：频度副词 always/usually/often/sometimes/hardly/never 百分比与位置（G43）

**口诀**：
> "频度副词有级别，always 100% 到 never 0%；be 动词后面站，实义动词前面站"

**百分比阶梯**：

| 频度副词 | 百分比 | 中文 | 缩写记忆 |
|---|---|---|---|
| always | 100% | 总是 | all ways = 所有方式→总是 |
| usually | 90% | 通常 | usual + ly = 通常地 |
| often | 60% | 经常 | of ten = 十次里六次→经常 |
| sometimes | 30% | 有时 | some times = 某些时候 |
| hardly (ever) | 5% | 几乎不 | hard + ly = 努力也难→几乎不 |
| never | 0% | 从不 | n + ever = 不曾经→从不 |

**位置规则（be后实前）**：

| 句型 | 公式 | 示例 |
|---|---|---|
| be 动词后 | 主语 + be + 频度副词 + ... | She is always happy. / I am usually busy. |
| 实义动词前 | 主语 + 频度副词 + 实义动词 + ... | I often play sports. / He never eats junk food. |
| 助动词后 | 主语 + 助动词 + 频度副词 + 实义动词 | She doesn't often watch TV. / Do you usually get up early? |
| 句首/句尾（sometimes） | Sometimes + 主语 + 动词... / 主语 + 动词... + sometimes | Sometimes I read books. / I read books sometimes. |

**例句（全部使用本课词汇）**：
1. I **always** jog in the morning.（实义动词前）
2. She is **usually** full of energy.（be 动词后）
3. We **often** read news online.（实义动词前）
4. **Sometimes** I watch television.（句首）
5. He **hardly ever** eats fast food.（实义动词前，hardly ever 连用）
6. I **never** stay up late.（实义动词前）

**中考辨析**：
- **hardly vs. hard**：hardly = 几乎不（否定含义）；hard = 努力地/困难的（肯定含义）——中考高频陷阱
- **sometimes vs. sometime vs. some times**：sometimes = 有时（频度）；sometime = 某时（时间点）；some times = 几次（次数）
- **频度副词位置**：be 后实前是核心规则，中考常考位置选择题

**易错点**：
- ❌ She always is happy. → ✅ She is always happy.（be 动词后，不前）
- ❌ I play often sports. → ✅ I often play sports.（实义动词前，不后）
- ❌ He hardly eats junk food. → ✅ He hardly ever eats junk food.（hardly 后常加 ever）
- ❌ I never don't eat breakfast. → ✅ I never eat breakfast.（never 本身否定，不加 don't）

**防越级约束**：仅限频度副词在一般现在时中的用法，不引入过去时中的频度副词（留至 L20 G54 跨时态对照）

**螺旋递进链（频度副词螺旋）**：
```
G16 (L6) 一般现在时非三单    ──首次接触──→
G25 (L10) 一般现在时三单      ──螺旋递进──→
G43 (L17) 频度副词             ──螺旋终点──→  频度副词完成！
```

### 语法②：How often 引导的特殊疑问句与回答（G44）

**口诀**：
> "How often 问频率，回答用 always 到 never；问次数用 once/twice/three times a week"

**公式矩阵**：

| 疑问词 | 用法 | 示例回答 |
|---|---|---|
| How often + 一般疑问句? | 问频率 | I always/usually/often/sometimes/never... |
| How often + do/does + 主语 + 动词? | 问具体频率 | I exercise twice a week. |
| 回答：频度副词 | 直接用频度副词 | — Always. / Sometimes. |
| 回答：次数表达 | once/twice/three times a week | I read three times a week. |

**次数表达规则**：

| 次数 | 表达 | 示例 |
|---|---|---|
| 1 次 | once | once a week（一周一次） |
| 2 次 | twice | twice a day（一天两次） |
| 3 次 | three times | three times a month（一月三次） |
| 4+ 次 | 数字 + times | four times a year（一年四次） |

> ⚠️ **once 和 twice 是特殊形式**，不能说 one time / two time ❌

**例句**：
1. — **How often** do you exercise? — I exercise **twice** a week.
2. — **How often** does she watch television? — She **usually** watches TV.
3. — **How often** do you go online? — I go online **every day**.
4. — **How often** do you jog? — I jog **three times a week**.

**中考辨析**：
- **How often vs. How long vs. How far**：How often 问频率；How long 问时长；How far 问距离——中考高频辨析
- **once/twice 不可拆分**：once a week ✅ / one time a week ❌

**易错点**：
- ❌ How often do you exercise? — One time a week. → ✅ Once a week.
- ❌ How often you exercise? → ✅ How often do you exercise?（需助动词）
- ❌ He exercise twice a week. → ✅ He exercises twice a week.（三单 -es，螺旋自 G25）

**防越级约束**：仅限 how often 基础提问与回答，不引入 how soon（多久以后，留至后续课）

**螺旋递进链（疑问词螺旋）**：
```
G06 (L2) Who            ──首次──→
G09 (L3) Where          ──→
G14 (L5) What           ──→
G22 (L9) 六宝系统        ──→
G35 (L13) how much/many ──→
G44 (L17) how often     ──螺旋终点──→  六疑问词全部完成！
```

### 语法③：百分比与生活习惯动词短语（G45）

**口诀**：
> "百分比 percent 不加 s，of 后面跟名词；健康习惯用一般现在时，exercise/sleep/read 都可加"

**公式矩阵**：

| 表达方式 | 结构 | 示例 |
|---|---|---|
| 百分比 + of + 名词 | 数字 + percent of + 名词 | Eighty percent of students like sports. |
| 主语 + 百分比 | 主语 + 动词 + 百分比 | I got 90 percent on the test. |
| 健康习惯短语 | exercise regularly / sleep 8 hours / read online | I exercise regularly and sleep 8 hours. |

**健康习惯核心动词短语**：

| 短语 | 中文 | 搭配 |
|---|---|---|
| exercise regularly | 规律运动 | I exercise regularly. |
| jog in the park | 在公园慢跑 | She jogs in the park every morning. |
| sleep 8 hours | 睡8小时 | He sleeps 8 hours every night. |
| read online | 在线阅读 | They read news online. |
| watch television | 看电视 | Don't watch television too often. |
| eat healthily | 健康饮食 | We should eat healthily. |

**例句**：
1. **Eighty percent** of students exercise **regularly**.
2. **Fifty percent** of people watch **television** every day.
3. I got 90 **percent** on my health **survey**.
4. A healthy **lifestyle** means you **hardly ever** eat junk food.

**中考辨析**：
- **percent 单复数**：percent 不加 s（80% = eighty percent ✅ / eighty percents ❌）
- **percent of + 复数名词 → 复数谓语**：Eighty percent of students are... ✅
- **percent of + 不可数名词 → 单数谓语**：Fifty percent of water is... ✅

**易错点**：
- ❌ 80 percents of students → ✅ 80 percent of students（percent 无复数）
- ❌ 80 percent of student → ✅ 80 percent of students（of 后用复数）
- ❌ I exercise sometime → ✅ I exercise sometimes（sometimes 有 s）

**防越级约束**：仅限基础百分比表达与健康习惯短语，不引入分数表达（1/3 等）

**螺旋递进链**：
```
G08 (L3) 基数词 1-100          ──首次──→
G36 (L13) 基数词与货币/价格    ──螺旋──→
G45 (L17) 百分比与生活习惯      ──螺旋终点──→
```
