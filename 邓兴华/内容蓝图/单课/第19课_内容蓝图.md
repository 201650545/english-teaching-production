## 二、语法详解（3 考点）

### 语法①：复合不定代词分类与语义（G49）

**口诀**：
> "some 开头用于肯定，any 开头用于否定疑问；no 开头表示全否定，every 开头表示全都含"

**复合不定代词全景表**：

| 类别 | 指人 | 指物 | 指地点 |
|---|---|---|---|
| some-（肯定） | someone | something | somewhere |
| any-（否定/疑问） | anyone | anything | anywhere |
| no-（全否定） | no one | nothing | nowhere |
| every-（全包含） | everyone | everything | everywhere |

**语义辨析矩阵**：

| 不定代词 | 语义 | 用法 | 示例 |
|---|---|---|---|
| someone | 某人（肯定句） | 肯定句中用 | Someone is calling you. |
| anyone | 任何人（否定/疑问） | 否定句/疑问句 | Is anyone here? / I don't know anyone. |
| no one | 没有人（全否定） | 等同 nobody | No one knows the answer. |
| everyone | 每个人（全包含） | 等同 everybody | Everyone is happy. |
| something | 某事（肯定句） | 肯定句中用 | I want something to drink. |
| anything | 任何事（否定/疑问） | 否定句/疑问句 | Do you need anything? / I don't need anything. |
| nothing | 没有什么（全否定） | 等同 not anything | Nothing is impossible. |
| everything | 一切（全包含） | 等同 all things | Everything is fine. |

> ⚠️ **some- 也可用于疑问句**：表示请求/邀请时，用 some- 而非 any-（螺旋自 G33）
> - Would you like something to drink?（请求，用 something ✅）
> - Can someone help me?（请求，用 someone ✅）

**例句（全部使用本课词汇）**：
1. **Someone** is exploring the park.（肯定句，指人）
2. Is **anyone** planning to go **abroad**?（疑问句，指人）
3. **No one** was **bored** at the **event**.（全否定，指人）
4. **Everyone** seems **happy** today.（全包含，指人）
5. I want **something** **enjoyable** to do.（肯定句，指物）
6. Do you need **anything** for the weekend?（疑问句，指物）
7. **Nothing** is more fun than a good weekend.（全否定，指物）
8. **Everything** is **ready** for the trip.（全包含，指物）
9. Let's go **somewhere** fun.（肯定句，指地点）
10. I can't find my diary **anywhere**.（否定句，指地点）

**中考辨析**：
- **someone vs. anyone**：someone 用于肯定句，anyone 用于否定/疑问句——与 some/any 规则一致（螺旋自 G33）
- **no one = not anyone**：No one knows. = I don't know anyone.（同义转换）
- **nothing = not anything**：I know nothing. = I don't know anything.（同义转换）
- **everyone 作主语 → 单数谓语**：Everyone is here. ✅ / Everyone are here. ❌

**易错点**：
- ❌ I don't know someone. → ✅ I don't know anyone.（否定句用 anyone）
- ❌ Do you need something?（一般疑问句应用 anything）→ ✅ Do you need anything?（但表邀请时可用 something）
- ❌ No one are here. → ✅ No one is here.（复合不定代词谓语用单数）
- ❌ Everyone have a plan. → ✅ Everyone has a plan.（谓语用单数）

**防越级约束**：仅限基础复合不定代词（someone/anyone/no one/everyone/something/anything/nothing/everything/somewhere/anywhere/nowhere），不引入 everywhere（本课未列入词汇表，留至后续课复现）

**螺旋递进链（不定代词螺旋）**：
```
G33 (L12) some/any 基础辨析  ──首次──→
G49 (L19) 复合不定代词         ──升级──→  完成！
```

### 语法②：形容词修饰复合不定代词强制后置规则（G50）

**口诀**：
> "形容词修饰不定代词，必须放在后面站；something new 不是 new something"

**公式矩阵**：

| 结构 | 公式 | 示例 |
|---|---|---|
| 形容词后置 | 复合不定代词 + 形容词 + ... | something new, someone interesting, nothing important |
| 不定式后置 | 复合不定代词 + to do | something to eat, nothing to do, someone to talk to |

**例句**：
1. I want to read **something interesting**.（形容词后置）
2. She met **someone new** at the event.（形容词后置）
3. There is **nothing important** in the diary.（形容词后置）
4. I need **something to do** this weekend.（不定式后置）
5. Let's find **somewhere nice** to go.（形容词后置，副词同规则）

**中考辨析**：
- **something new ✅ vs. new something ❌**：复合不定代词的形容词必须后置
- **anything else ✅**：else 也后置，与形容词同位
- **nothing much ✅**：much 也后置

**易错点**：
- ❌ I have new something. → ✅ I have something new.
- ❌ She is looking for important someone. → ✅ She is looking for someone important.
- ❌ There is to eat nothing. → ✅ There is nothing to eat.

**防越级约束**：仅限形容词后置与不定式后置，不引入定语从句修饰不定代词（留至 L33 G66）

### 语法③：复合不定代词作主语，谓语动词一律用单数（G51）

**口诀**：
> "复合不定代词作主语，谓语动词用单数；everyone is 不是 are，nothing is 不是 are"

**公式矩阵**：

| 主语 | 谓语 | 示例 |
|---|---|---|
| someone | is / does / has | Someone is calling you. / Someone has a question. |
| anyone | is / does / has | Is anyone there? / Does anyone know? |
| no one | is / does / has | No one is bored. / No one has a plan. |
| everyone | is / does / has | Everyone is happy. / Everyone knows the answer. |
| something | is / does / has | Something is wrong. / Something has happened. |
| anything | is / does / has | Is anything wrong? / Does anything matter? |
| nothing | is / does / has | Nothing is impossible. / Nothing seems boring. |
| everything | is / does / has | Everything is fine. / Everything seems enjoyable. |

**例句**：
1. **Everyone is** planning **something** enjoyable.（主语 everyone → is）
2. **Nothing seems** boring when you have a good **diary**.（主语 nothing → seems）
3. **Someone has** left a **message**.（主语 someone → has）
4. **No one knows** the answer.（主语 no one → knows，三单形式 G25）
5. **Everything is** ready for the **event**.（主语 everything → is）

**中考辨析**：
- **everyone is ✅ vs. everyone are ❌**：复合不定代词作主语，谓语永远单数
- **they each vs. everyone**：they each → 复数谓语；everyone → 单数谓语——中考易混点
- **none vs. no one**：none 可指人或物，可接单/复数谓语；no one 仅指人，接单数谓语

**易错点**：
- ❌ Everyone are happy. → ✅ Everyone is happy.（谓语用单数）
- ❌ Nothing are wrong. → ✅ Nothing is wrong.（谓语用单数）
- ❌ Someone have a plan. → ✅ Someone has a plan.（谓语用三单）

**防越级约束**：仅限复合不定代词主谓一致，不引入 none/neither 等高级不定代词（留至后续课）

**螺旋递进链（主谓一致螺旋）**：
```
G03 (L1) be 动词与主格搭配         ──首次──→
G25 (L10) 三单 -s/-es 变化         ──螺旋──→
G51 (L19) 复合不定代词主谓一致     ──升级──→
```
