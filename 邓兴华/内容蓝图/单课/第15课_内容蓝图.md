## 二、语法详案（3 考点）

### 语法①：天气表达句型（G40）

**口诀**：
> "How's the weather 问天气，What's the weather like 也可以；It's sunny/rainy 来回答，温度用 degrees"

**公式矩阵**：

| 句型 | 公式 | 示例 |
|---|---|---|
| 问天气① | How's the weather + in + 地点/时间? | How's the weather in Beijing? |
| 问天气② | What's the weather like + in + 地点/时间? | What's the weather like in summer? |
| 回答 | It's + 天气形容词 | It's sunny. / It's rainy. / It's cloudy. |
| 回答（名词） | It's + a + 天气名词 + day | It's a sunny day. / It's a rainy day. |
| 温度表达 | The temperature is + 数字 + degrees. | The temperature is 25 degrees. |

> **两种问法完全等价**：
> - How's the weather? = What's the weather like?（两种问法意思相同）
> - 回答都用 It's + 形容词
> - **注意**：What's the weather **like**? 中的 like 不可省略——What's the weather? ❌

**天气形容词与名词对照表**：

| 名词 | 形容词 | 示例 |
|---|---|---|
| sun | sunny | It's sunny. |
| rain | rainy | It's rainy. / It's raining.（进行时也可表天气） |
| cloud | cloudy | It's cloudy. |
| wind | windy | It's windy. |
| snow | snowy | It's snowy. / It's snowing. |

**例句（全部使用本课词汇）**：
1. **How's the weather** in summer? — **It's hot**.（问天气+回答）
2. **What's the weather like** in winter? — **It's cold and snowy**.（另一种问法）
3. In spring, the weather is **warm**.（描述季节天气）
4. The temperature is 20 **degrees**.（温度表达）
5. It's a **rainy** day. We can't go on a **trip**.（天气影响计划）

**中考辨析**：
- **How's the weather? = What's the weather like?**：两种问法完全等价——中考常考句型转换
- **It's raining vs. It's rainy**：raining 强调"正在下雨"（进行时），rainy 强调"是雨天"（形容词）——都可回答天气问题
- **in summer vs. in the summer**：表季节用 in summer（零冠词，螺旋自 G31 零冠词规则）
- **weather 是不可数名词**：How's the weather? ✅ / How's the weathers? ❌

**易错点**：
- ❌ What's the weather? → ✅ What's the weather like?（like 不可省略）
- ❌ It is sun. → ✅ It is sunny.（回答用形容词不用名词）
- ❌ How is weather? → ✅ How is the weather?（weather 前需 the）
- ❌ The weather is 25 degrees. → ✅ The temperature is 25 degrees.（温度用 temperature 不用 weather）

**防越级约束**：仅限基础天气句型，不引入天气进行时与一般现在时的辨析（raining vs. rainy 仅提及，不深入）；不引入天气预报高级词汇

**螺旋递进链（天气表达螺旋）**：
```
G30 (L11) 时间介词 in/on/at     ──首次接触──→
G33 (L12) some/any（Would you like some?）──螺旋复现──→
G40 (L15) 天气表达句型           ──螺旋递进──→
G42 (L15) 非人称 It              ──螺旋终点──→
```

### 语法②：Could/Would you please do sth.? 礼貌请求（G41）

**口诀**：
> "Could you please 礼貌求，Would you please 委婉问；please 后接动词原形，回答 Sure/Certainly 最相称"

**公式矩阵**：

| 句型 | 公式 | 示例 |
|---|---|---|
| Could you please + do sth.? | Could + 主语 + please + 动词原形 + ...? | Could you please open the window? |
| Would you please + do sth.? | Would + 主语 + please + 动词原形 + ...? | Would you please help me? |
| Could I + do sth.?（请求许可） | Could + I + 动词原形 + ...? | Could I use your phone? |
| Would you like + sth.?（邀请） | Would + 主语 + like + 名词? | Would you like some tea?（螺旋自 G34） |

> **Could/Would you please 用法辨析**：
> - **Could you please...?**：请求别人做某事（Could you please close the door?）
> - **Would you please...?**：语气更委婉，请求别人做某事（Would you please pass the salt?）
> - **Could I...?**：请求自己被允许做某事（Could I borrow your book?）
> - **Would you like...?**：邀请/招待（Would you like some coffee?）

**肯定/否定回答对照表**：

| 请求类型 | 肯定回答 | 否定回答 |
|---|---|---|
| Could/Would you please...? | Sure. / Certainly. / Of course. / No problem. | Sorry, I can't. / I'm afraid not. |
| Could I...? | Sure, go ahead. / Of course. | Sorry, you can't. / I'm afraid not. |

**例句（全部使用本课词汇）**：
1. **Could you please** tell me the weather tomorrow?（请求别人）
2. **Would you please** close the window? It's **cold**.（请求别人+天气）
3. **Could I** go on a **trip** this **vacation**?（请求许可）
4. **Would you like** to go on a **trip** in **summer**?（邀请，螺旋自 G34）
5. **Could you please** bring a jacket? It's **windy** outside.（请求+天气）

**中考辨析**：
- **Could vs. Would（礼貌请求）**：两者都表礼貌请求，Could 更常用，Would 更委婉——中考两者均可
- **Could you please vs. Can you**：Could you please 更礼貌，Can you 更随意——正式场合用 Could
- **please 的位置**：Could you **please** open the door? = Could you open the door, **please**?（please 可放句中或句末）
- **Could I...? vs. Can I...?**：Could I 更礼貌，Can I 更口语化

**易错点**：
- ❌ Could you please to open the window? → ✅ Could you please open the window?（please 后接动词原形）
- ❌ Would you please opening the door? → ✅ Would you please open the door?（用原形不用 -ing）
- ❌ Could you please helps me? → ✅ Could you please help me?（please 后动词原形，不加 -s）
- ❌ What's the weather? → ✅ What's the weather like?（螺旋自 G40）

**防越级约束**：仅限 Could/Would you please do sth.? 基础礼貌请求，不引入 Would rather do（留至综合课），不引入 Might/May I...?（留至后续课）

**螺旋递进链（礼貌请求螺旋）**：
```
G28 (L11) can/can't, must/have to     ──首次接触──→
G33 (L12) Would you like some...?      ──螺旋复现──→
G34 (L13) would like to do             ──螺旋递进──→
G41 (L15) Could/Would you please do?   ──螺旋终点──→
```

### 语法③：非人称代词 It 指代天气、时间、距离（G42）

**口诀**：
> "It 不只是代词，天气时间距离它；It's sunny 说天气，It's seven 说时间；It's far 说距离"

**公式矩阵**：

| 用法 | 公式 | 示例 |
|---|---|---|
| It 指代天气 | It's + 天气形容词 | It's sunny today. / It's cold in winter. |
| It 指代时间 | It's + 时间 | It's seven o'clock. / It's Monday. / It's time for lunch. |
| It 指代距离 | It's + 距离 (from A to B) | It's far from here. / It's two kilometers from school. |
| It's time to do sth. | It's time + to + 动词原形 | It's time to go on a trip. |
| It's time for sth. | It's time + for + 名词 | It's time for vacation. |

> **非人称 It 的核心特征**：
> - It 在这些句型中**不指代特定的人或物**，而是作为形式主语
> - 天气：It's sunny.（It 不指代"太阳"，而是"天气状况"）
> - 时间：It's seven.（It 不指代"钟"，而是"时间"）
> - 距离：It's far.（It 不指代"路"，而是"距离"）

**例句（全部使用本课词汇）**：
1. **It's** sunny and **warm** in spring.（It 指天气）
2. **It's** seven o'clock. Let's go on a **trip**.（It 指时间）
3. **It's** far from here to the **supermarket**.（It 指距离，螺旋自 L13 词汇）
4. **It's** time to go on **vacation**.（It's time to do sth.）
5. **It's** time for **summer vacation**.（It's time for sth.）

**中考辨析**：
- **It's vs. Its**：It's = It is（缩写），Its = 它的（物主代词）——中考高频陷阱
  - ✅ It's sunny.（It is sunny）
  - ✅ Its weather is nice.（它的天气很好）
- **It's time to do vs. It's time for**：
  - ✅ It's time to go.（to + 动词原形）
  - ✅ It's time for lunch.（for + 名词）
  - ❌ It's time for go. / ❌ It's time to lunch.
- **非人称 It vs. 人称 It**：
  - 非人称：It's sunny.（无具体指代对象）
  - 人称：Where is my book? — It's on the desk.（It 指代 book）

**易错点**：
- ❌ Its sunny today. → ✅ It's sunny today.（It's = It is，不是 Its）
- ❌ It's time to lunch. → ✅ It's time for lunch. / It's time to have lunch.（to + 动词，for + 名词）
- ❌ The weather is sunny. → ✅ It's sunny.（天气句型用 It，不用 The weather is...）
- ❌ It is far to school. → ✅ It is far from here to school.（距离需用 from...to... 结构）

**防越级约束**：仅限非人称 It 指天气/时间/距离的基础用法，不引入 It 作形式主语的其他用法（It's said that... / It seems that... 等留至后续课）

**螺旋递进链（非人称 It 螺旋）**：
```
G03 (L3) It 作为人称代词          ──首次接触──→
G40 (L15) It 指代天气              ──螺旋复现──→
G42 (L15) 非人称 It（天气/时间/距离）──螺旋终点──→
```

### 4.1 本课音素（Stage 3 拼读 L15）

| 字母组合 | 核心音素 | 发音要点 |
|---|---|---|
| oo | /uː/ | 双唇收圆，舌后部抬起，发长元音 /uː/（如 food, moon） |
| oo（短） | /ʊ/ | 双唇收圆，短促发音 /ʊ/（如 book, good）——同一组合两种发音 |

| 字母组合 | 朗读词汇（每组合 4 个，补充 2 个共 6 个） |
|---|---|
| oo /uː/ | food, moon, room, soon, cool, school |
| oo /ʊ/ | book, good, look, foot, stood, wood |
| ew /juː/ | new, few, knew, news, dew, hew |
| ew /uː/ | flew, grew, drew, chew, blew, threw |

> **补充说明**：本课 2 组音素（/uː/ 和 /juː/），oo 和 ew 各有两种发音需分别练习。共 16 词，充分满足训练量。其中 cool 为本课新词，foot 为 L11 不规则复数词（foot→feet），可形成跨课联动记忆。
