❓ G22 · 特殊疑问句"六宝"（1/4）
口诀 ★ 重点记忆
"六宝疑问词开头，be/助/情态跟着走；主语谓语随后到，句末问号不能丢"
What
事物/动作
What is this?
Where
地点/方位
Where did you go?
When
时间/日期
When did you travel?
Why
原因/理由
Why are you happy?
Who
人/身份
Who is the guide?
How
方式/程度
How did you travel?
疑问句结构公式
疑问词 + be + 主语 + ...? → What is this?
疑问词 + 助动词 + 主语 + 动词原形? → What did you do?
疑问词 + 情态动词 + 主语 + 动词? → How can I help you?
疑问词作主语 → Who is there?
构成公式 ★ 重点记忆
What→事物 Where→地点 When→时间 Why→原因 Who→人 How→方式
CORE · 课堂必做
🧭 G22 · 六宝三步判断法（2/4）
用法 ★ 重点记忆
第一步：判断提问对象
事物→What · 地点→Where · 时间→When · 原因→Why · 人→Who · 方式→How
第二步：判断疑问词角色
作主语：疑问词 + 谓语（Who is there?）
不作主语：疑问词 + be/助/情态 + 主语 + 谓语（What did you do?）
第三步：注意时态
过去时用 did（疑问词 + did + 主语 + 原形），现在时用 do/does
例1 What did you see at the museum?（问事物，过去时用 did）
例2 Where is the art gallery?（问地点，用 is）
例3 When did you travel to Beijing?（问时间，过去时用 did）
例4 Why was the traffic heavy?（问原因，过去时 be 用 was）
例5 Who is your favorite guide?（问人，用 is）
例6 How did you go to the station?（问方式，过去时用 did）
易错 ▲ 难点
❌ What you do?
✅ What do you do?
❌ Where you went?
✅ Where did you go?
❌ Who are there?
✅ Who is there?

❌ Where you went?

🔀 G23 · 选择疑问句 or 结构（1/3）
口诀 ★ 重点记忆
"or 连接选一个，Yes/No 不能答；直接选 A 或选 B，完整回答最稳当"
A
or
B
句型结构示例回答 be动词选择Be + 主语 + A or B?Are you a teacher or a student?I'm a student. 实义动词选择Do/Does/Did + 主语 + V + A or B?Do you like tea or coffee?I like tea. 情态动词选择情态 + 主语 + V + A or B?Can you swim or run?I can swim. or连接地点疑问词 + ... A or B?Did you go by bus or by train?By bus.
用法 ★ 重点记忆
三步判断法：
第一步：找 or——句中出现 or 连接两个选项 → 选择疑问句
第二步：禁用 Yes/No——选择疑问句绝不能用 Yes/No 回答
第三步：直接选答——从 A 或 B 中选一个（完整回答 I like tea. 或简短回答 Tea.）

🚌 G24 · 交通方位介词（1/3）
口诀 ★ 重点记忆
"by 加交通工具，on foot 步行走；turn left/right 转方向，go straight 直走不回头"
🚌
by bus
by + 交通工具（无冠词）
类别搭配规则示例 交通方式by + 交通工具by 后无冠词，直接加名词by bus / by car / by bike / by train 步行on foot固定搭配，foot 不变复数I go to school on foot. 转向turn left / turn rightturn + 方向词，直接连用Turn left at the station. 直走go straightgo + straight aheadGo straight and turn right. 方位on the left / on the righton + the + 方向The museum is on your right.
用法 ★ 重点记忆
三步判断法：
第一步：交通方式——by + 工具（by bus），on foot（步行）
第二步：方向——turn left/right（转向），go straight（直走）
第三步：方位——on the left/right（在左/右边）
关键陷阱：by 后不加 a/the（by bus ✅ / by a bus ❌）；on foot 的 foot 不变复数（on foot ✅ / on feet ❌）

A Guide to the National Science Museum
107 words · 应用文

At the National Science Museum

Last Saturday, my class went on a special trip to the National Science Museum. We traveled by bus and arrived at nine in the morning. The museum was big and beautiful.

First, our guide showed us the machine display on the first floor. We saw many old machines. Some of them were still working! It was amazing to see how they worked. Then, we went to the second floor to learn about traffic. There was a special train from 1804. It was one of the oldest trains in the world.

After lunch, we visited the art gallery on the third floor. The shining paintings were beautiful. My friend and I shared our favorite paintings with each other. I chose a picture of a fresh morning in the park.

Before we left, we bought some souvenirs at the museum shop. It was a wonderful day. I learned a lot about science, history, and culture. I want to go back again in the future.

How Museums Change Our Lives
129 words · 说明文

Museums are special places. They are not just buildings with old things. They help us understand the world and our place in it.

When we visit a science museum, we can learn about the history of machines and the future of technology. We can see how traffic changed from horses to cars and trains. This helps us understand how people lived in the past.

Museums also show culture and art. When we look at a painting in a gallery, we can feel what the artist felt. We can share the experience with our friends and family. This makes our lives richer.

Today, museums are using new ways to display their collections. Some have interactive displays. Some have special guides. These new ways make learning fun and fresh.

In the future, museums can help us make good choices for our world. They show us what is possible.

六宝 What/Where/When/Why/Who/How
结构 疑问词 + be/助/情态 + 主语 + 谓语
