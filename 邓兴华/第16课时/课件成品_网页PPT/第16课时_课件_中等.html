📝语法错题精讲 · 代词系统
G01-G06 主格/宾格 + 物主代词 + 指示代词 + be动词 + Who疑问
G01-G03主格·宾格·物主代词
主格做主语（I/you/he/she/it/we/they）
宾格做宾语（me/you/him/her/it/us/them）
物主代词表"谁的"（my/your/his/her/its/our/their）
主格 + V → 宾格放在V/介词后 → 形容词性物主 + 名词
✅ 正确 She gave me her notebook. （她把她的笔记本给了我）
❌ 错误 Her gave my she notebook.（格混用）
G04-G05指示代词 + be动词搭配
this/these（近）→ that/those（远）
be动词口诀：
I→am, you→are, he/she/it→is, 复数→are
✅ This is my dictionary. / These are their textbooks.
❌ This are my dictionary.（单复数不匹配）
G06Who引导的疑问句
Who做主语：
Who + V(三单) + ...?
Who做宾语：
Who + do/does + 主语 + V + ...?
✅ Who is your English teacher? / Who do you play with after school?
🎓 口诀
主格打头做主人，宾格跟在动介后；物主带名词不忘记，is/am/are看主语。
1选择正确答案：___ gave the assignment to ___ teacher?
A. Her / my
B. She / my
C. She / her
D. Her / her

3选择正确答案：— ______ is that girl over there? — She is my classmate, Lily.
A. What
B. Where
C. Who
D. How

G19-G24 was/were + did + 时间状语 + 六疑问词 + 选择疑问 + 交通介词
G19-G20was/were + 一般过去时
错误率42% · 全卷最高！
was/were规则：
I/he/she/it → was | you/we/they → were
一般过去时：动词加-ed，不规则需记
play→played, go→went, have→had, do→did, spend→spent

📝语法错题精讲 · 冠词与代词
G31-G33 a/an/the + 量词 + some/any
G31冠词 a / an / the
a + 辅音音素开头：a book, a university（u发/juː/）
an + 元音音素开头：an apple, an hour（h不发音）
the = 特指（双方都知道的、前文提过的）
a/an = 泛指（首次提到） → the = 特指（再次提到）
✅ I read an article yesterday. The article was about a survey.
❌ I read a article.（article以元音开头→an article）
G32-G33量词 + some/any
可数：a few（几个, +复数）/ few（很少, 几乎没有）
不可数：a little（一点）/ little（很少, 几乎没有）
some/any规则：
some → 肯定句 / any → 否定句&疑问句
注意：Would you like some...?（委婉请求用some）
✅ There is some milk in the fridge. / Is there any milk? / There isn't any milk.

G34-G39 would like + how much/many + 价格 + be vs have + why/because + 描述形容词
G34-G36would like + how much/many + 价格表达
would like = 想要（委婉）
主语 + would like + 名词/to do
how much + 不可数名词（多少钱/多少）

📝语法错题精讲 · 天气与礼貌
G40-G42 天气句型 + Could/Would礼貌请求 + It指代
G40天气表达句型
错误率30% · 高频错题第7名
天气句型：
It's + adj (描述天气) / It + V (天气动作)
✅ It's sunny today. / It's cloudy and windy.
❌ The weather is sun. / Today is rain.（中英结构混淆）
常见天气词：sunny ☀️ / cloudy ☁️ / rainy 🌧️ / windy 💨 / snowy ❄️ / foggy 🌫️
G41-G42Could/Would礼貌请求 + It指代
Could委婉请求：
Could you + V原形? / Could I + V原形?
Would委婉提议：
Would you like + 名词/to do?
It指代：天气、时间、距离等无生命事物用It做主语
✅ Could you help me with the survey? / Would you like some coffee?
It's five kilometers from here.（指距离）
🎓 口诀
天气用It不用The，Could Would最礼貌；It指时间与天气，还能表示远和近。
1选择正确答案：— How's the weather? — ______ sunny.

🔍诊断题 #48 · 五选四句际逻辑（错误率35%）
Our Group Survey
Last week, our class did a survey about favorite foods. ① ___ First, we made a list of questions. Then we asked our classmates. ② ___ After that, we counted the answers. ③ ___ The results were very interesting! ④ ___ Next time, we want to survey about hobbies.
A. Finally, we wrote a report about the results.
B. We worked in groups of four.
C. Most students like dumplings best.
D. It was a great experience!
解题思路：① 前句"did a survey"后应说明方式→选B（分组工作）
② 前句"asked classmates"后应是数据结果→选C（大多数喜欢饺子）
③ 前句"counted answers"后接总结→选A（写报告）
④ 结尾感受→选D（很棒的体验）
正确答案：①B ②C ③A ④D
