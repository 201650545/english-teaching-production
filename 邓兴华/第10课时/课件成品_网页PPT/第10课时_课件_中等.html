📐 G26 · does/doesn't 否定句与疑问句
G26 口诀 ★ 必背
does/doesn't帮三单，动词一定还原形！
句型结构例句
肯定句He/She/It + 动词-sHe studies math.
否定句He/She/It + doesn't + 动词原形He doesn't study math.
疑问句Does + he/she/it + 动词原形?Does he study math?
肯定回答Yes, he/she does.Yes, he does.
否定回答No, he/she doesn't.No, he doesn't.
G26 核心公式
肯定：He studies math.
否定：He doesn't study math.
疑问：Does he study math?
✗ 最常见错误
He doesn't studies math.He doesn't study math.
Does he studies math?Does he study math?

📊 综合对比 · 三单 vs 非三单

📖 阅读 A篇 · My Busy School Day
My Busy School Day
应用文 · 107词

My name is Tom. I am a middle school student. I have a busy school day every day.

My first class begins at 8:00 a.m. It is Chinese. I like Chinese because it is interesting. Then I have math at 9:00. Math is a little hard for me, but I study hard.

At 10:00, I have geography. My geography teacher is very kind. She makes the class fun. After geography, I have biology. We study plants and animals in biology class. It is my favorite subject.

In the afternoon, I have physics. My classmate Jack says physics is difficult, but I think it is simple. I finish my homework at school and go home at 5:00 p.m.

I love my school day!

1. What time does Tom's first class begin?
解析：第一段 "My first class begins at 8:00 a.m."。时间细节，原文直接提取。
2. What is Tom's favorite subject?
解析：第三段末尾 "It is my favorite subject." 指代 biology（简单指代）。
3. Who says physics is difficult?
解析：第四段 "My classmate Jack says physics is difficult"。人物细节，原文直接提取。
4. How does Tom feel about his school day?
解析：结尾 "I love my school day!" 直接表达喜爱。态度推断开放度高，故用选项。
5. What is the passage mainly about?
解析：全文按时间顺序介绍 Tom 一天的课程安排。主旨概括开放度高，故用选项。

A Day in the Library
记叙文 · 129词

It is Thursday. Lily goes to the school library after lunch. She likes reading books there because the library is quiet and comfortable.

Lily walks to a shelf and picks up a book about biology. She wants to understand how plants grow. She sits down at a table and begins to read. The book is very interesting.

After thirty minutes, her classmate Mike comes in. He needs to finish his math exercise. He sits next to Lily and starts to work. They do not talk because the library is a quiet place.

At three o'clock, Lily finishes reading. She puts the book back on the shelf and says goodbye to Mike. She has a physics lesson at three thirty. She hurries to the classroom.

Lily loves her school library. It is a good place to study and read.

1. When does Lily go to the library?
解析：第一段 "Lily goes to the school library after lunch"。时间细节，原文直接提取。
2. What book does Lily read?
解析：第二段 "picks up a book about biology"。细节，原文直接提取。
3. Why don't Lily and Mike talk?
解析：第三段 "They do not talk because the library is a quiet place."。明确因果，原文摘录即可。
4. What does "hurries" mean in the passage?
解析：第四段 "She hurries to the classroom."（3:30 有物理课，时间紧）。开放性词义判断，故用选项。
5. What is the passage mainly about?
解析：全文按时间顺序讲 Lily 在图书馆的一个下午。主旨概括开放度高，故用选项。
