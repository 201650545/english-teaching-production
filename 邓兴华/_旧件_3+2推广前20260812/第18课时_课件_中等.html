📐 G46 · 现在进行时结构规则表
📋be + V-ing = 现在进行时
口诀 ★ 重点记忆
现在进行 be + ing，am/is/are 要分清；肯定加 ing，否定 be 后 not 加
句型公式示例
肯定句主语 + am/is/are + V-ing + ...She is washing the dishes.
否定句主语 + am/is/are + not + V-ing + ...She is not (isn't) washing the dishes.
缩写否定isn't / aren't / 'm notHe isn't sleeping. / We aren't chatting.
be 动词选择规则
I → am   He/She/It → is   You/We/They → are
肯定 She is cooking dinner in the kitchen.
否定 He isn't sleeping right now.
肯定 They are chatting online.
否定 I am not watching television at the moment.
CORE · 课堂必做
🔧 G46 · be + V-ing 结构拆解
🧩三要素缺一不可：be 动词 + 主语一致 + V-ing
👤
I
am + V-ing
👨
He/She/It
is + V-ing
👥
You/We/They
are + V-ing
❌
be 漏掉
She cooking. ❌
❌ 常见错误
She cooking dinner. → She is cooking dinner.（be 动词不能漏）
He is sleep. → He is sleeping.（必须加 -ing）
They are talk. → They are talking.（必须加 -ing）
I am not cook. → I am not cooking.（必须加 -ing）
💡 中考辨析
现在进行时 vs. 一般现在时
She is cooking now.（此刻正在做饭）
She cooks every day.（每天做饭的习惯）

🌅 A Busy Saturday Morning
约 183 词 · 记叙文

Last Saturday morning, our house was very noisy. Everyone was busy doing different things.

At eight o'clock, Mom was cooking breakfast in the kitchen. The smell was wonderful. Dad was sweeping the floor in the living room. He is always tidy on weekends. My sister was dancing to music in her room. She dances every morning. My little brother was drawing a picture at the table. He was not writing or reading. He just loves drawing.

I was washing the dishes from last night. The water was cold, but I didn't mind. Our grandmother was chatting with our neighbor at the door. They were talking about the weather. Our cat was sleeping on the windowsill. It was not noticing anything.

Suddenly, the phone rang. It was my aunt. She was calling to invite us for lunch. Mom spoke to her and said yes. We were all happy to hear that.

At that moment, I noticed something: everyone in our family was doing something useful. We were not resting, but we were not complaining either. It was a busy but happy morning.

/l/
词尾 le 发 /l/，前接辅音形成成音节。舌尖抵上齿龈，气流从舌两侧通过。

-le
/əl/

le 前的辅音与 /l/ 连读，形成成音节。先读前面的重读音节，再轻轻带出 /l/ 音。
CORE · 课堂必做
👂 le 听音选词 + 看词归音
🎯判断单词是否含 le 词尾
1. 下列哪个单词词尾含 le？
解析：bottle 词尾为 -tle，含 le。water 和 paper 不含 le。
2. 下列哪个单词不含 le 词尾？
解析：orange 不含 le 词尾。apple 和 uncle 都含 le。
3. "table" 中 le 前的辅音是？
解析：ta-ble，le 前是字母 b，发 /b/。
4. "little" 中 le 前的辅音是？
解析：lit-tle，le 前是字母 t，发 /t/。

1. "table" 和 "tab" 的区别是什么？
解析：table = tab + le /l/，词尾多一个 /l/ 音。

解析：table 和 chair 完全不同，不是最小对立对。
