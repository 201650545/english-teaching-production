🔍 L19 错题回顾 · 不定代词易错点
⚠️ L19 高频错误
❌ Everyone are happy. → ✅ Everyone is happy.（不定代词用单数谓语）
❌ I saw interesting something. → ✅ I saw something interesting.（形容词后置）
❌ Nobody don't know. → ✅ Nobody knows.（否定代词不再加 don't）
易错 ▲ 难点
不定代词三铁律：
1. 主语用单数谓语（is/was）
2. 形容词必须后置（something + adj.）
3. 否定代词不再否定（Nobody knows. ≠ Nobody doesn't know.）
🤔 本课导入思考
如果这些事发生在过去，句子会怎么变？
→ Everyone was happy. （is → was）
→ I saw something interesting. （see → saw）
这就是本课核心：一般过去时全套综合！
导入检测：Yesterday everyone ____ tired after the trip.
解析：Everyone 用单数谓语，过去时 → was。G52 + G49 综合考点。

📐 G52 · 一般过去时全套综合规则表（1/3）
口诀 ★ 重点记忆
"was 单数 were 复数，did 否定加 not；过去时间作信号，is/am/are 变 was/were"
句型公式示例 be 过去肯定主语 + was/were + ...The building was tall. / They were hungry. be 过去否定主语 + was/were + not + ...It wasn't foggy. / We weren't tired. be 过去疑问Was/Were + 主语 + ...?Was it foggy? / Were they hungry? 实义动词过去肯定主语 + V-ed/d + ...We climbed the hill. / He traveled abroad. 实义动词过去否定主语 + didn't + V(原形) + ...He didn't go to the hill. 实义动词过去疑问Did + 主语 + V(原形) + ...?Did you ride a bicycle?
was/were 选择规则 ★ 重点记忆
I → was
He/She/It → was
You/We/They → were

🛡️ G52 · was/were/did 混淆项排除（2/3）
混淆排除清单 ▲ 高频陷阱
❌ He was go.
✅ He went.（过去时用 V-ed/不规则变化，不是 was + V）
❌ He didn't went.
✅ He didn't go.（didn't 后用动词原形）
❌ Did he went?
✅ Did he go?（Did 后用动词原形）
❌ I were hungry.
✅ I was hungry.（I 搭配 was）
❌ They was tired.
✅ They were tired.（复数主语搭配 were）
🔑 三大混淆排除法则
1. was/were 不与实义动词原形连用：was go ❌ → went ✅
2. didn't + V(原形)：didn't went ❌ → didn't go ✅
3. Did + 主语 + V(原形)?：Did went ❌ → Did go ✅
中考辨析 ○ 理解即可
was/were vs. did：be 动词过去时用 was/were；实义动词过去时用 did/didn't + V-ed
didn't + V(原形)：否定句中 didn't 后必须用动词原形——中考高频陷阱
Did + 主语 + V(原形)?：疑问句中 Did 后必须用动词原形
🔒 防越级约束
仅限一般过去时综合运用，不引入过去进行时（was/were + V-ing）；不引入过去完成时（had done）

From the visitor center, you can walk up the hill. The distance from the bottom to the top is about two kilometers. At the top, you can see the whole city below you. It is a wonderful view!

If you visit on a foggy day, please take an umbrella. The weather can change quickly. You can also ride a bicycle around the hill. We have enough bicycles for rent near the entrance.

Your journey to Green Hill Park will be a great experience. The park is open from 8:00 a.m. to 6:00 p.m. every day. Tickets are free for children under 12. Come and enjoy nature!

Last summer, I went on a journey with my family. The entire trip was wonderful! We started early in the morning. The weather was foggy, so my father took an umbrella. My mother was a trader, and she brought some food for us.

We climbed a hill near our city. The distance was not far, but the road was difficult. When we reached the top, we were all hungry. We sat below a big tree and ate lunch. My mother made roast duck. It was delicious!

After lunch, we rode bicycles around the hill. I realized that I didn't dislike the rain at all. The foggy weather made the journey more exciting. I could imagine I was an explorer.

My father remained silent most of the time, but I knew he was happy. The journey left a beautiful memory in my heart. I want to write a sentence about it: "The best journeys are the ones we share with family."

🔊 拼读 · tion → /ʃən/ 音素学习（1/2）
tion
/ʃən/
t 发 /ʃ/，ion 发 /ən/
整体如"什恩"
station, action, nation, motion, attention, section
ture
/tʃə/
t 发 /tʃ/，ure 发 /ə/
整体如"吃额"
future, picture, nature, culture, adventure, lecture
发音要点 ★ 重点记忆
tion → /ʃən/：嘴唇微撅，发出"什"的音，加上轻柔的"恩"
ture → /tʃə/：舌尖抵上齿龈，发出"吃"的音，加上轻柔的"额"
对比：tion 的 /ʃ/ 是清辅音（不送气），ture 的 /tʃ/ 是塞擦音（有爆破）
tion sta·tion /ˈsteɪ·ʃən/ — 车站
tion ac·tion /ˈæk·ʃən/ — 行动
ture fu·ture /ˈfjuː·tʃə/ — 未来
ture pic·ture /ˈpɪk·tʃə/ — 图片
