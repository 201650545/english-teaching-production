📐 G28 · 情态动词 can/can't/must/have to（1/3）
口诀 ★ 重点记忆
"can表能够，can't表不能；must是必须，have to被迫行；mustn't禁止做，don't have to不必行"
情态动词含义用法例句 can能够表能力或许可You can ask questions. can't不能表禁止或不能You can't run in the hallway. must必须表主观必须You must wear your uniform. mustn't禁止表强烈禁止You mustn't fight in school. have to不得不表客观被迫I have to arrive at 7:00. don't have to不必表不需要You don't have to wear a tie.
用法 ★ 重点记忆
must vs have to：
must = 主观认为必须（内在要求）
have to = 客观被迫必须（外在压力）
mustn't vs don't have to：
mustn't = 禁止做（绝对不能）
don't have to = 不必做（可以做但非必须）
can You can use your pencil to write.
must Students must follow the school rules.
mustn't You mustn't break the rules.
CORE · 课堂必做
🔄 G28 · 情态动词转换器（2/3）
转换公式 ★ 重点记忆
肯定句 → 否定句：
can → can't（直接加not）
must → mustn't（直接加not，表示禁止）
have to → don't have to（加don't/doesn't，表示不必）
疑问句：
Can + 主语 + 动词原形?
Must + 主语 + 动词原形?
Do/Does + 主语 + have to + 动词原形?
转换1 You can talk. → You can't talk.
转换2 You must listen. → You mustn't talk.（禁止）
转换3 I have to go. → I don't have to go.（不必）
疑问 Can I go to the bathroom? / Must I wear this? / Do you have to leave?
易错 ▲ 难点
❌ You don't must run.
✅ You mustn't run.（must否定是mustn't）
❌ You mustn't to go.
✅ You mustn't go.（情态动词后不加to）
❌ You don't have to = You mustn't
✅ don't have to = 不必（可以但不强制）; mustn't = 禁止

📊 G29 · 规则与不规则对照（2/3）
对比 ★ 重点记忆
规则复数（加-s/-es）：
rule → rules · uniform → uniforms · hallway → hallways
不规则复数（本课9词）：
man → men · woman → women · child → children
foot → feet · tooth → teeth · mouse → mice
sheep → sheep · deer → deer · fish → fish
易错 ▲ 难点
❌ two mans
✅ two men（man→men，不是mans）
❌ three childs
✅ three children（child→children，不是childs）
❌ five sheeps
✅ five sheep（sheep单复数同形）
❌ four mouses
✅ four mice（mouse→mice）
🔒 防越级约束
仅限9个不规则变化词：man/woman/child/foot/tooth/mouse/sheep/deer/fish
不引入 goose→geese, ox→oxen, person→people 等
对比 One woman, three women are in the classroom.
对比 One tooth hurts. I need to brush all my teeth.

🕐 G30 · 时间介词 in/on/at（1/2）
口诀 ★ 重点记忆
"in年月季，on具体日，at时刻记分明"
介词用法适用时间例句 in大范围年、月、季节in 2024 / in May / in summer on具体日星期、日期、某天on Monday / on May 5th at精确时刻钟点、时刻at 8:00 / at noon / at night
用法 ★ 重点记忆
三步判断法：
第一步：看时间范围大小——年/月/季用 in
第二步：看是否具体某天——星期/日期用 on
第三步：看是否精确时刻——钟点用 at
特殊搭配：
in the morning/afternoon/evening（一天中的时段）
on Monday morning（具体某天的时段用on）
at night（夜间固定搭配）
in School starts in September.
on We have English class on Monday.
at Class begins at 8:00 a.m.

Our Class Rules
107 words · 应用文

Welcome to our class! We have some important rules that everyone must follow.

First, you must arrive at school before 8:00 a.m. Don't be late! Second, you must wear your school uniform every day. Third, you must keep quiet in the hallway. No noise, please!

Fourth, you can't fight with your classmates. If you break this rule, the teacher will give you a warning. Fifth, you must listen to the teacher carefully in class. Don't forget to do your homework!

Remember: rules are not for punishing you. They are for keeping order and saving time. Good behavior makes our class better!

📝 阅读理解 A 篇

1. What time must students arrive at school?
解析：第二段 "arrive at school before 8:00 a.m."。
2. What must students wear every day?
解析：第二段 "wear your school uniform every day"。
3. What happens if you fight with classmates?
解析：第三段 "the teacher will give you a warning"。
4. According to the passage, rules are for ____.
解析：最后一段 "keeping order and saving time"。
5. What is the main idea of the passage?
解析：主旨题。全文讲班级规则及其意义。

📖 阅读 B 篇 · A New Student's First Week
A New Student's First Week
129 words · 记叙文

My name is Tom, and I am a new student at Green Hill School. On my first day, I was very nervous. I didn't know the rules.

In the morning, I arrived at school at 8:15. The teacher said, "You must arrive before 8:00!" I was late. Then I realized I wasn't wearing my uniform. I had to go home and change. What a bad morning!

On the second day, I wore my uniform and arrived at 7:50. Much better! But in the hallway, I made a lot of noise. A teacher stopped me and said, "You must keep quiet in the hallway." I remembered this rule.

By Friday, I knew all the rules. I didn't fight, I didn't break any rules, and my behavior was good. The teacher said, "Well done, Tom!" I was so happy. Rules help me be a better student.

📝 阅读理解 B 篇

1. Why was Tom nervous on his first day?
解析：第一段 "I didn't know the rules"。
2. What time did Tom arrive on his first day?
解析：第二段 "I arrived at school at 8:15"。
3. What did Tom do wrong in the hallway?

Rules Make Us Better
129 words · 说明文

Rules are everywhere in our lives. At school, we have class rules. At home, we have family rules.

Some students think rules are bad because they can't do everything they want.

However, rules are actually very helpful.

Without rules, some students might fight or make too much noise.

Good rules also save time. When everyone follows the rules, things go smoothly.

So, what should we do?

Rules don't punish us — they make us better.
