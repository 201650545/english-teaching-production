📐 G31 · 冠词 a/an/the 规则表
口诀 ★ 重点记忆
"a用辅音前，an用元音前，the特指这和那；泛指可数单加a/an，特指上文提过的加the"
冠词用法例词例句
a辅音音素开头，泛指单数可数a book, a car, a bowlI'd like a bowl of rice.
an元音音素开头，泛指单数可数an egg, an apple, an onionShe eats an egg every day.
the特指（上文提过/双方都知道）the menu, the soup, the customerThe soup is delicious.
零冠词不可数名词/复数名词泛指water, beef, noodlesI like noodles and soup.
G31 · 冠词选择三步法
第一步：是可数单数吗？→ 不是 → 多用零冠词
第二步：可数单数 → 听开头音素 → 辅音用 a，元音用 an
第三步：是特指吗？（上文提过/这/那）→ 用 the

❌ a egg
✅ an egg（元音音素开头）

✅ I like noodles.（泛指不用the）

📊 G32 · 可数与不可数名词规则
口诀 ★ 重点记忆
"可数能数有复数，不可数不能加s；量词搭着名词用，a cup of一杯茶"
特征可数名词不可数名词
能否加 -s✅ 能（egg→eggs, bowl→bowls）❌ 不能（water, beef, pork）
加冠词a/an + 单数（an egg, a bowl）不能直接加 a/an
量词表达直接数（two eggs）量词+of+名词（a cup of water）
本课例子egg, carrot, onion, bowl, menuyogurt, water, juice, tea, coffee, beef, mutton, pork, soup
G32 · 量词结构公式
数字 + 量词 + of + 名词
a cup of tea（一杯茶）
a glass of water（一杯水）
a bowl of noodles（一碗面）
a piece of beef（一块牛肉）
two cups of coffee（两杯咖啡）
必背 ★ 量词公式
数字 + 量词(单/复) + of + 不可数名词 → a cup of tea / two cups of coffee
易错 ▲ 难点

✅ two pieces of beef
❌ a water
✅ a glass of water
❌ three soup
✅ three bowls of soup

⚖️ G33 · some vs. any 规则矩阵
口诀 ★ 重点记忆
"some用在肯定句，any用在否定疑问句；Would you like用some，礼貌请求记分明"
词用法句型例句
some肯定句主语 + have + some + 名词I have some tea.
any否定句主语 + don't + any + 名词I don't have any coffee.
any疑问句Is/Are there + any + 名词?Is there any soup?
some礼貌请求（例外）Would you like + some + 名词?Would you like some juice?
G33 · some/any 判断三步法
第一步：看句型 → 肯定句 → some
第二步：看句型 → 否定句/疑问句 → any
第三步：Would you like...? → 用 some（礼貌请求例外）
定义 ★ 概念
some / any 均表示"一些"，既可接可数名词复数，也可接不可数名词。
警示 ▲ 易混
疑问句并非都用 any：Would you like some...? / Can I have some...? 是礼貌请求，期待肯定回答，用 some。
some I'd like some beef noodles.
any Is there any cabbage in the soup?
any There isn't any water on the table.
some Would you like some tea?（礼貌请求）

💡 G33 · some/any 例句与例外详解
🍽️ 餐厅场景例句
肯定 The customer wants some soup.
否定 The customer doesn't want any pork.
疑问 Do you have any fresh fish?
礼貌 Would you like some yogurt?
⚠️ Would you like some...? 例外
Would you like...? 是礼貌请求，虽然是一般疑问句形式，但用 some 不用 any！
因为这是邀请/提议，期待对方说 Yes。

❌ Would you like any coffee?

Ordering Lunch at the Noodle House
记叙文 · 129词

It was lunchtime. Tom and his sister Lily walked into a small noodle house. The restaurant was not big, but it was clean and warm. A friendly waiter brought them a menu.

"What would you like, Tom?" Lily asked. "I'd like a large bowl of beef noodles. What about you?" Tom said. "I'll have a bowl of mutton noodles and a glass of juice," Lily said.

The waiter wrote down their order. "Would you like some soup?" he asked. "Yes, a bowl of chicken soup, please," Tom said. "And can we have some tea too?" Lily added.

The food came quickly. Tom said the beef noodles were delicious. Lily enjoyed her mutton noodles too. They drank the soup and tea happily. After the meal, Tom left a tip on the table. "What a great lunch!" Lily said with a smile.

解析："I'd like a large bowl of beef noodles."
3. What did Lily drink?
解析：Lily点了"a glass of juice"和"some tea"。
4. What did Tom do after the meal?
解析："Tom left a tip on the table."
5. How did Lily feel about the lunch?
解析："What a great lunch!" Lily said with a smile.

How to Eat a Balanced Meal
说明文 · 129词

A balanced meal has three parts: grains, vegetables and fruit, and protein.

Grains give us energy. Rice, noodles and bread are grains. We can have a bowl of rice or a bowl of noodles. Vegetables and fruit give us vitamins. Carrots, cabbage and onions are good choices. Protein helps our body grow. Eggs, fish, beef and mutton are protein food. We can eat an egg or some fish for protein.

How much should we eat? Eat a little of everything. Do not eat too much meat. Drink water or soup with the meal. A glass of water is a good choice. Do not drink too much juice, because it has a lot of sugar.

A balanced meal helps us stay healthy and strong. Try to eat well every day!

📖 阅读理解 C篇（5题）

1. What are grains in the passage?
解析：第二段"Rice, noodles and bread are grains."
2. Which food gives us vitamins?
解析：第二段"Vegetables and fruit give us vitamins. Carrots, cabbage and onions are good choices."
3. What should we drink with the meal?
解析：第三段"Drink water or soup with the meal."
4. Which of the following is TRUE?
解析：第三段"Eat a little of everything."——均衡饮食讲究适量多样，据此推断。
5. What is the passage mainly about?
解析：标题与全文都在讲均衡饮食的组成与吃法。
