# 第1课 · 题型练习：阅读理解 · 细节理解题

> 学生：刘洋（八升九）｜ 本课语法：冠词 a/an/the｜ 限时建议：20 分钟

**技巧提示——定位法**：① 先读题干，抓关键词（人名 / 时间 / 地点 / 数字）→ ② 回原文找到对应句子 → ③ 用"同义替换"判断选项，不选原文没有的。

## 一、语篇与题目

### 语篇 A（Lost and Found）

Lost and Found in the School Library

We found these things in the school library this week. Come and see if any are yours.

A blue pencil box with two pens and a ruler. Found Monday.
A white eraser with a cat picture. Found Tuesday.
An English dictionary with a red cover. Found Wednesday.

Call us at 8866-5533 or come to the library office. You must describe your thing before taking it.

**题目 1–4（细节题）**

1. Where were these things found?
   A. In the classroom　B. In the school library　C. On the playground　D. At the school gate

2. What is in the blue pencil box?
   A. Two rulers and a pen　B. Two pens and a ruler　C. An eraser and a pen　D. A dictionary and a pen

3. On which day was the white eraser found?
   A. Monday　B. Tuesday　C. Wednesday　D. Friday

4. What must you do before taking your thing?
   A. Pay some money　B. Write your name　C. Describe your thing　D. Show your student card

### 语篇 B（Lily 的健康饮食）

Hello! I am Lily. I have good eating habits every day. For breakfast, I have milk and bread at home. For lunch at school, I eat rice, vegetables and chicken with my friends. For dinner, my family eat apples, bananas and oranges together. I like healthy food very much. I do not like fast food because it is not good. I drink water every day after meals. Fruit and milk help me grow tall and strong. I feel happy and healthy all the time!

**题目 5–8（细节题）**

5. What does Lily have for breakfast?
   A. Rice and chicken　B. Milk and bread　C. Apples and oranges　D. Vegetables and meat

6. Where does Lily eat her lunch?
   A. At home　B. At school　C. In the library　D. In a restaurant

7. What does Lily's family eat for dinner?
   A. Rice, vegetables and chicken　B. Fast food　C. Apples, bananas and oranges　D. Milk and bread

8. What does Lily drink every day after meals?
   A. Cola　B. Juice　C. Milk　D. Water

## 二、答案与解析

| 题 | 答案 | 定位 | 关键依据 |
|----|------|------|---------|
| 1 | B | 原文"in the school library" | 直接定位 |
| 2 | B | "two pens and a ruler" | 蓝铅笔盒里的内容 |
| 3 | B | "Found Tuesday" | 白橡皮是周二捡到的 |
| 4 | C | 末句"describe your thing" | 取走前要描述物品 |
| 5 | B | "I have milk and bread at home" | 早餐吃牛奶面包 |
| 6 | B | "For lunch at school" | 在学校吃午饭 |
| 7 | C | "eat apples, bananas and oranges" | 晚餐吃水果 |
| 8 | D | "I drink water every day" | 饭后喝水 |
