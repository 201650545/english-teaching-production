# 第2课 · 题型练习：阅读理解 · 主旨大意题

> 学生：刘洋（八升九）｜ 本课语法：现在完成时｜ 限时建议：20 分钟

**技巧提示——主旨归纳法**：① 看**首尾段/首尾句**（主题句常在开头或结尾）；② 找**反复出现的高频词**（本篇主角）；③ 标题 = "主角 + 核心事件/评价"，不选只覆盖一小节的片面选项。

---

## 一、语篇与题目

### 语篇 A

Come and see our family photo album! It is big and nice. In the first picture, you can see my grandpa and grandma. They are kind. In the next picture are my father, my mother and me. Look at the little boy! That is my cute cousin. We are a happy family. Welcome to our class show!

**题目 1–2（主旨 / 标题）**

1. What is the passage mainly about?
   A. A big album　B. A happy family　C. A cute boy　D. A class show

2. What is the best title for this passage?
   A. My Kind Grandpa　B. Our Happy Family　C. A Little Boy　D. School Life

### 语篇 B

My favorite teacher is Mr. Lee. He is a tall and thin man of medium height. He has short black hair and a round face. His eyes are big and bright. He always wears a pair of glasses in class.

Mr. Lee is a good teacher and a kind person. He is very popular because he is patient and funny. We all like his lessons. He often describes difficult ideas with easy examples so that every student can understand them. In my eyes, he is a handsome teacher in our school. I want to be a teacher like him when I grow up.

**题目 3–4（主旨 / 标题）**

3. What is the passage mainly about?
   A. A school in China　B. How to be a teacher　C. A kind and popular teacher　D. A pair of glasses

4. What is the best title for this passage?
   A. My Favorite Teacher　B. A Tall Man　C. Easy Examples　D. My School Life

---

## 二、答案与解析

| 题 | 答案 | 解析 |
|----|------|------|
| 1 | B | 全文围绕"家人 + happy family"展开，末句"a happy family"点题 |
| 2 | B | 标题 = 主角（family）+ 评价（happy）；A/C 只覆盖局部 |
| 3 | C | 首句"My favorite teacher"总领全文，后文反复讲 Mr. Lee 的优点 |
| 4 | A | 首句即主题句，直接作为标题最准确 |
