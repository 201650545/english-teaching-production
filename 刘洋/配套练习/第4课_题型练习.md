## 一、语篇与题目

### 语篇（Last Saturday at the Market）

Last Saturday, my mom and I went to the market. She gave me a list and a budget of fifty yuan. First, we looked for vegetables. We picked some tomatoes and carrots. They were fresh and ___1___. Then we went to the meat section. Mom chose a kilo of chicken. It was a little ___2___, but good for dinner. Next, we needed some ___3___ for breakfast. Mom let me choose ___4___. I picked my ___5___ brand of pancakes. They were not ___6___, only eight yuan a bag. Mom also checked the ___7___ on the package. At the ___8___, the cashier told us the ___9___ was forty-two yuan. Mom paid with a fifty-yuan note and got eight yuan in ___10___. I was glad we had money left.

**题目 1–10**

1. A. heavy　B. cheap　C. expensive
2. A. cheap　B. heavy　C. expensive
3. A. pancakes　B. bread　C. milk
4. A. some　B. one　C. two
5. A. new　B. only　C. favorite
6. A. fresh　B. cheap　C. expensive
7. A. date　B. price　C. name
8. A. checkout　B. window　C. door
9. A. price　B. bill　C. total
10. A. change　B. money　C. coins

---

## 二、答案与解析

| 题 | 答案 | 解析（四法归类） |
|----|------|-----------------|
| 1 | B. cheap | 词义辨析：fresh and cheap（又新鲜又便宜） |
| 2 | C. expensive | 上下文逻辑：but 转折，"有点贵但晚餐合适" |
| 3 | A. pancakes | 下文线索：后文提到 brand of pancakes |
| 4 | B. one | 固定搭配：let me choose one（选一种） |
| 5 | C. favorite | 词义辨析：my favorite brand（我最喜欢的牌子） |
| 6 | C. expensive | 上下文逻辑：only eight yuan 说明"不贵" |
| 7 | A. date | 词义辨析：check the date on the package（看包装日期） |
| 8 | A. checkout | 场景词：at the checkout（在收银台） |
| 9 | C. total | 上下文逻辑：the total was forty-two yuan（总额） |
| 10 | A. change | 固定搭配：got eight yuan in change（找回八元零钱） |
