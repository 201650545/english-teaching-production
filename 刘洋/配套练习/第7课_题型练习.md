# 第7课 · 题型练习：阅读回答问题

> 学生：刘洋（八升九）｜ 本课语法：非谓语动词｜ 限时建议：15 分钟

**技巧提示——阅读表达五步**：① **答完整句**（主语 + 谓语）；② 细节题**回原文定位**原词/同义替换；③ Why 题用 **Because + 句子**；④ 翻译题**逐字对应**，不丢关键信息；⑤ 不超纲、不自由发挥。

---

## 一、语篇与题目

阅读下面的短文，根据短文内容回答问题。

Lost: My School ID Card

I lost my school ID card in the library on Tuesday morning. My name is Jack Brown. The card is white and blue. It has my photo and name on it. This card is important to me. I need it to borrow books. I must find it before Friday. If you find it, call me at 139-8877-6655, or take it to the lost and found office.

**题目 1–5**

1. Where did Jack Brown lose his school ID card?

2. What color is the school ID card?

3. What should you do if you find the card?

4. Why is the card important to Jack?

5. 把文中划线句子翻译成中文：I need it to borrow books.

---

## 二、答案与解析

| 题 | 答案（完整句） | 解析 |
|----|--------------|------|
| 1 | He lost it in the library (on Tuesday morning). | 细节题，定位第一句 |
| 2 | It is white and blue. | 细节题，定位 "white and blue" |
| 3 | I should call him at 139-8877-6655 or take it to the lost and found office. | 细节题，定位末句，用完整句作答 |
| 4 | Because he needs it to borrow books. | Why 题，用 Because + 句子 |
| 5 | 我需要它（学生卡）来借书。 | 翻译题，it 指代"学生卡"，to borrow 表目的 |
