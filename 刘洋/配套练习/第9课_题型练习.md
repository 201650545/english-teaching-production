## 一、完形填空（10 空）

My best friend is Linda. She is a girl of medium ___1___. She ___2___ long straight black hair and big bright eyes. Her face is ___3___ and round. Linda is not very tall, ___4___ she looks neat and energetic.

Linda is a good student. She is also a good ___5___. She sings beautifully. ___6___ do so many students like her? ___7___ she is kind and helpful.

When someone feels sad, Linda always gives a warm smile. Her smile makes people feel ___8___. She is a ___9___ girl, ___10___ we all like her.

1. A. height　B. weight　C. color
2. A. is　B. has　C. have
3. A. small　B. long　C. round
4. A. but　B. so　C. because
5. A. singer　B. actor　C. artist
6. A. What　B. Why　C. How
7. A. So　B. Because　C. For
8. A. warm　B. cold　C. sad
9. A. big　B. tall　C. kind
10. A. and　B. but　C. or

## 二、选词填空（6 空）

用方框内所给词的正确形式填空，有两个词多余。

| 方框 | | | | |
|------|---|---|---|---|
| protect | environment | careful | help | green | plant |
| we | happy | pollute | clean | | |

We should ___1___ the environment. First, we can ___2___ more trees. Second, we should ___3___ sort (分类) our rubbish. What's more, we must stop ___4___ the rivers. If everyone does his part, our city will become ___5___ and more beautiful. Let's work together to make a ___6___ world.

## 三、五选四（4 空）

根据短文内容，从方框中选择适当句子还原到文中，有一项多余。

| 方框 | |
|------|---|
| A. First, we went there by a yellow school bus. | B. There were many interesting things to see. |
| C. The stories about robots made us laugh. | D. We learned a lot from the visit. |
| E. We played games in the garden that day. | |

We went on a school trip last month and it was a lot of fun for the whole class. A good plan before the day helps a lot to make the trip easy. ___1___ We visited the science museum and saw old and strange exhibits there with our eyes for the first time. ___2___ Our guide told us funny stories about small robots and stars in the sky. ___3___ We took many photos by the big door together to remember the day with joy. ___4___ Everyone enjoyed the trip and we went home happy and tired at night.

## 四、答案与解析

### 完形填空
1. A（of medium height 中等身高）；2. B（she has...她有着……）；3. C（round face 圆脸）；4. A（but 转折）；5. A（sings beautifully → singer 歌手）；6. B（Why 问原因）；7. B（Because 答原因）；8. A（warm smile 暖心的微笑）；9. C（kind girl 善良的女孩）；10. A（and 顺承）

### 选词填空
1. protect（protect the environment 保护环境）；2. plant（plant trees 种树）；3. carefully（careful 变副词，修饰 sort）；4. polluting（stop polluting the rivers 停止污染河流）；5. cleaner（become cleaner 比较级）；6. greener（a greener world）

### 五选四
1. A（"先去……"交代出行方式）；2. B（"有很多有趣的东西看"承接 museum）；3. C（"机器人的故事让我们笑"承接 guide stories）；4. D（"从参观中学到很多"承接拍照、过渡总结）；E 为干扰项
