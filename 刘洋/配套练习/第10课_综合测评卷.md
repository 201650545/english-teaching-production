## 第一部分 语法选择（10 题 × 2 分 = 20 分）

1. She is ___ honest girl and everyone likes her.
   A. a　B. an　C. the　D. /

2. I ___ my keys. I can't find them now.
   A. lose　B. lost　C. have lost　D. am losing

3. The new library ___ last year, and it ___ by many students since then.
   A. built; used　B. was built; has been used　C. is built; used　D. has built; was used

4. — Could you tell me ___? — Sure. Go along this street.
   A. where is the bank　B. where the bank is　C. where was the bank　D. the bank is where

5. Please do your homework ___.
   A. careful　B. carefully　C. care　D. caring

6. There are ___ floors in the building, and we live on the ___ floor.
   A. nine; nine　B. ninth; nine　C. nine; ninth　D. ninth; ninth

7. Neither of the two boys ___ from Beijing.
   A. is　B. are　C. were　D. am

8. There isn't ___ milk in the glass. Could you give me some?
   A. many　B. much　C. few　D. a few

9. The workers stopped ___ a short rest and then went on working.
   A. have　B. having　C. to have　D. had

10. ___ it was raining heavily, the farmers kept working in the field.
    A. Because　B. So　C. If　D. Although

## 第四部分 阅读回答问题（5 题 × 4 分 = 20 分）

Lily is a middle school student. Her bedroom is very beautiful, clean and tidy. In her room, a warm bed is near the window. Next to the bed, there is a desk. A pink lamp is on the desk. Her clothes are in the big wardrobe. Under the chair, there is a blue soccer ball. Lily likes to read books at her desk every evening. She thinks a clean and tidy room always makes her feel very happy every single day of her life.

1. Where is the bed in Lily's room?
2. What is on the desk?
3. Where is the blue soccer ball?
4. Why does Lily like a clean and tidy room?
5. 把文中划线句子翻译成中文：She thinks a clean and tidy room always makes her feel very happy.

## 第五部分 作文（30 分）

请以 "My Best Friend"（我最好的朋友）为题，写一篇 80 词左右的短文。
要点：1. 他/她的外貌与性格；2. 你们一起常做的事；3. 你喜欢他/她的原因。

### 第四部分 阅读回答

2. A pink lamp is on the desk.
3. It is under the chair.

### 第五部分 作文评分维度（30 分）

- **语言（10 分）**：语法（时态、主谓一致）正确、用词准确；
- **结构（10 分）**：有开头、主体、结尾，连接词清晰（First/Second/because）。
