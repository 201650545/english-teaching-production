# 第6课 · 题型练习：五选四（篇章衔接）

> 学生：刘洋（八升九）｜ 本课语法：主谓一致 + 可数/不可数｜ 限时建议：15 分钟

**技巧提示——五选四四法**：① **衔接词**（and/but/because/so）；② **代词指代**（it/they/this 指前面哪个名词）；③ **总分结构**（先总起再分述）；④ **逻辑顺序**（时间/因果递进）。先看空缺前后句，找"call back"线索。

---

## 一、语篇与题目

根据短文内容，从方框中选择恰当的句子还原到文中，使短文完整通顺。有一项多余。

| 方框 | |
|------|---|
| A. Running is the only way to stay healthy. | B. Fruit is also an important part of a healthy diet. |
| C. Vegetables are good for our body, too. | D. Drinking milk is a good habit every morning. |
| E. Do not forget to drink enough water every day. | |

Eating healthy food is important for every student at school. Good food is the fuel for your body and your brain.

A good breakfast helps you listen and learn in class with a clear mind. ___1___ We should eat fruit every day after our three meals because fruit gives us vitamins. ___2___ Fresh vegetables help our body grow tall and stay strong, so eat them often. ___3___ Drink milk for strong bones and white teeth every morning before school. ___4___ A good diet keeps us happy and full of energy for the whole day.

Eat well, drink water often and move every day for a healthy life that you will love!

---

## 二、答案与解析

| 空 | 答案 | 解析 |
|----|------|------|
| 1 | B. Fruit is also... | also 承接上文"早餐"，引出"水果也是健康饮食的一部分" |
| 2 | C. Vegetables are good...too | too 与上一句 fruit 并列，从"水果"过渡到"蔬菜" |
| 3 | D. Drinking milk... | 后句"Drink milk"直接呼应，D 是总起句 |
| 4 | E. Do not forget to drink enough water | 承上启下，最后补充"别忘了喝水"，衔接总结句 |

> 干扰项 A：Running is the only way...（"唯一"太绝对，且本文只讲饮食，没提跑步）
