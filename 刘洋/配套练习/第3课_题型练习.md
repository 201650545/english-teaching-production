## 一、语篇与题目

### 语篇 A（Supermarket Sale）

GREEN SUPERMARKET — BIG WEEKEND SALE! Come and shop with us from Friday to Sunday, June 6 to June 8. We have great deals for the whole family.

Fresh fruit and vegetables: twenty percent off every morning before nine o'clock. Milk, bread and eggs: buy two, get one free. Cooking oil and sugar: twenty percent off all week long.

Special deal: spend over fifty yuan and get a free package of salt. New members can save an extra five yuan on their very first bill. We are open every day from eight in the morning to ten at night. Visit us at 28 Market Road, or call 555-0199.

**题目 1–3（推理）**

1. We can infer (推断) from the passage that the sale ______.
   A. lasts for three days　B. is only for students　C. happens every week

2. If you spend 60 yuan, you can ______.
   A. get a free package of salt　B. get everything free　C. close the shop

3. The passage is most probably a(n) ______.
   A. story　B. advertisement (广告)　C. diary

### 语篇 B（Habits Survey）

We are doing a survey about teenagers' daily habits. Our results show that sixty percent of students exercise every day. Twenty percent exercise twice a week. But twenty percent hardly ever exercise.

Eighty percent of students usually eat breakfast. However, forty percent sometimes eat junk food. Most students sleep about seven hours each night. We also find that fifty percent of students read books every day. Thirty percent read online sometimes. Twenty percent never read books after school. A healthy lifestyle is important for your body and mind.

**题目 4–5（词义猜测 / 推理）**

4. The word "hardly" in the passage means ______.
   A. almost not　B. often　C. always

5. What can we infer from the survey?
   A. Most students have healthy habits.　B. Nobody eats breakfast.　C. All students read books every day.

---

## 二、答案与解析

| 题 | 答案 | 解析 |
|----|------|------|
| 1 | A | "from Friday to Sunday, June 6 to June 8" = 共三天 |
| 2 | A | "spend over fifty yuan and get a free package of salt"，60>50 可领盐 |
| 3 | B | 有促销信息、地址电话，是典型广告 |
| 4 | A | hardly ever = 几乎从不（频率副词，表"几乎不"） |
| 5 | A | 60% 每天锻炼 + 80% 吃早餐，说明大多数习惯健康 |
