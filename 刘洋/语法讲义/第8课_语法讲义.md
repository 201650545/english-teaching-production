## 一、语法概念

### 1. 规则与构成

- **因果**：because（因为）/ so（所以）——**because 与 so 不能连用**
- **转折**：although / though（虽然）——**although 与 but 不能连用**
- **条件**：if（如果）——"主将从现"：If it rains, we **will stay** at home.
- **时间**：when / while（当……时）、until（直到）
- **目的**：so that（以便，为了）

### 2. 典例（6 句）

1. He didn't come **because** he was ill.
2. **Although** it was raining, the farmers kept working.（不再加 but）
3. **If** it rains tomorrow, we will stay at home.
4. I will wait **until** you come back.
5. He got up early **so that** he could catch the first bus.
6. **When** I arrived, he was reading a book.

### 3. 高频易错（5 条）

1. ❌ Because he was ill, so he didn't come. → ✅ 删掉 so（because 与 so 二选一）
2. ❌ Although it rained, but we went out. → ✅ 删掉 but（although 与 but 二选一）
3. ❌ If it will rain tomorrow, we stay home. → ✅ If it **rains** tomorrow, we **will stay** home.（主将从现）
4. ❌ I won't leave until you will come. → ✅ ...until you **come**.（until 从句用现在表将来）
5. ❌ 表"虽然下雨却继续干活"选了 If → ✅ 用 **Although**（转折关系）

### 4. 判诀

> **because / so 不连用，although / but 不连用；**
> **if / when / until 从句用现在时表将来（主将从现）。**

## 二、当堂练习（2 : 6 : 2）

### 语法选择（2 题）

1. ___ it was raining heavily, the farmers kept working in the field.
   A. Because　B. So　C. Although　D. If

2. If it ___ tomorrow, we ___ at home.
   A. rains; will stay　B. will rain; stay　C. rains; stay　D. will rain; will stay

### 选词填空（6 题，选项词：because / so / although / if / until / so that）

1. ___ he is young, he can do the work well.
2. He didn't go to school ___ he was ill.
3. He was ill, ___ he didn't go to school.
4. Please wait here ___ I come back.
5. ___ you work hard, you will pass the exam.
6. She got up early ___ she could catch the train.

### 造句（2 题）

1. 用 **although** 造一句（不连用 but）。
2. 用 **if + 主将从现** 造一句。

---

## 三、答案与解析

**语法选择**：
1. C（"下雨"与"继续干活"是转折，用 Although）
2. A（if 从句用一般现在 rains，主句用 will stay）

**选词填空**：
1. Although（"年纪小"与"做得好"转折）
2. because（没上学是因为生病，because 接原因）
3. so（生病是原因，结果是没上学，so 接结果）
4. until（"在这里等，直到我回来"，until 表"直到"）
5. If（"如果努力"条件）
6. so that（"以便赶火车"目的）

**造句参考答案**：
1. **Although** he is tired, he keeps working.
2. **If** it is sunny tomorrow, we **will go** hiking.
