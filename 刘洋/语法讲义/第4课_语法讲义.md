## 一、语法概念

### 1. 规则与构成

- **什么是宾语从句**：一个句子作主句动词的宾语
  - I know **that he is right**.（我知道他是对的）
  - Can you tell me **where the bank is**?（你能告诉我银行在哪吗）
- **三大引导词**：
  1. **that**（陈述句，无实义，常可省略）：I think (that) he is kind.
  2. **if / whether**（一般疑问句，意为"是否"）：I wonder if he will come.
  3. **疑问词**（特殊疑问句，what/where/when/why/how 等）：Tell me where you live.
- **黄金规则——陈述语序**（主语 + 谓语，不倒装）：
  - ❌ where **is** the bank → ✅ where the bank **is**
  - ❌ what **is** he doing → ✅ what he **is** doing
- **时态呼应**：
  - 主句现在时 → 从句用其所需时态
  - 主句过去时 → 从句用相应过去时态（will → would；is → was）
  - 客观真理永远用一般现在：The teacher said the earth **goes** around the sun.

### 2. 典例（6 句）

1. I think **that he is right**.
2. I want to know **where the bank is**.
3. Can you tell me **what time the train leaves**?
4. I don't know **whether/if she will come**.
5. She asked me **where I lived**.
6. The teacher said **the earth goes around the sun**.

### 3. 高频易错（5 条）

1. ❌ Can you tell me where is the bank? → ✅ ...where the bank **is**.（陈述语序）
2. ❌ I don't know when will he come. → ✅ ...when he **will** come.（will 放主语后）
3. ❌ He asked me where do I live. → ✅ He asked me where I **lived**.（主句过去 + 从句过去）
4. ❌ I think he won't come. → ✅ 若表否定应说 I **don't** think he will come.（think 的否定前移）
5. ❌ She said that the sun rose in the east. → ✅ ...the sun **rises** in the east.（真理用一般现在）

### 4. 判诀

> **宾语从句三要素：引导词、陈述语序、时态呼应；**
> **疑问词引导不倒装：where the bank is，不是 where is the bank。**

## 二、当堂练习（2 : 6 : 2）

### 语法选择（2 题）

1. — Could you tell me ___? — Sure. Go along this street and turn left.
   A. where is the nearest bank　B. where the nearest bank is　C. where was the bank　D. the bank is where

2. She asked me ___ I had been to Beijing.
   A. that　B. whether　C. what　D. where

### 选词填空（6 题，选项词：that / whether / where / what / when / who）

1. I think ___ he will pass the exam.
2. Do you know ___ the meeting will begin?
3. Can you tell me ___ the post office is?
4. I don't know ___ she likes the gift or not.
5. Please tell me ___ you did last weekend.
6. She wants to know ___ is calling her.

### 造句（2 题）

1. 用"宾语从句 + 陈述语序"表达："请告诉我火车什么时候出发"。
2. 用 if/whether 造一句，表达"我不知道他明天是否来"。

---

## 三、答案与解析

**语法选择**：1. B（疑问词引导宾语从句用陈述语序：where the nearest bank is）；2. B（"是否去过北京"用 whether/if）

**选词填空**：
1. that（陈述句 I think that...）
2. when（问"什么时候开始"）
3. where（问"邮局在哪儿"）
4. whether（与 or not 连用，用 whether）
5. what（问"做了什么"，what 作宾语）
6. who（问"谁在打电话"，who 作主语，本身是陈述语序）

**造句参考答案**：
1. Please tell me **when the train leaves**.
2. I don't know **whether/if he will come tomorrow**.
