## 一、8 大时态总表

| 时态 | 构成 | 常用标志词 | 例句 |
|------|------|-----------|------|
| 一般现在 | do / does | always, often, every day | I **go** to school every day. |
| 一般过去 | did / was / were | yesterday, last..., ...ago | I **went** to Beijing last year. |
| 一般将来 | will do / be going to | tomorrow, next... | I **will go** tomorrow. |
| 现在进行 | am/is/are doing | now, Look!, Listen! | I **am reading** now. |
| 过去进行 | was/were doing | at that time, while | I **was reading** when he came. |
| 现在完成 | have/has done | already, yet, for, since | I **have finished** it. |
| 过去完成 | had done | by..., before... | He **had left** before I arrived. |
| 过去将来(启蒙) | would do | 主句用过去时 | He said he **would come**. |

## 二、三大易混时态辨析

### 1. 现在完成 vs 一般过去
- **现在完成**：动作对现在有影响，不点出具体过去时间
- **一般过去**：只讲过去发生，常有 yesterday / last... / ...ago
- ❌ I have lost my key **yesterday**. → ✅ I **lost** my key yesterday.

### 2. 过去进行 vs 一般过去（when 打断）
- **过去进行**：过去某个时刻**正在做**（长动作）
- **一般过去**：突然发生的**打断动作**（短动作）
- I **was reading** (长) when he **came in** (短).

### 3. 现在完成 for / since
- **for + 时间段**（for five years）；**since + 时间点**（since 2020 / since he was seven）

## 四、精选练习（10 题）

1. She ___ an honest girl. A. is a　B. is an　C. are a　D. are an
2. I ___ my pen. I can't find it anywhere. A. lose　B. lost　C. have lost　D. am losing
3. The library ___ last year. A. built　B. was built　C. is built　D. has built
4. Can you tell me ___? A. where is the bank　B. where the bank is　C. where was the bank　D. the bank is where
5. Please listen ___ in class. A. careful　B. carefully　C. care　D. caring
6. Today is her ___ birthday. A. nine　B. nineth　C. ninth　D. ninths
7. Neither of the boys ___ from Beijing. A. is　B. are　C. were　D. am
8. There isn't ___ milk in the glass. A. many　B. much　C. few　D. a few
9. They stopped ___ a rest because they were tired. A. have　B. having　C. to have　D. had
10. ___ it was raining, he still went out. A. Because　B. So　C. If　D. Although

---

## 五、答案与解析

1. B（honest 元音开头用 an）
2. C（结果影响现在"现在找不到"，用现在完成 have lost）
3. B（last year 过去 + 被动 was built）
4. B（宾语从句陈述语序 where the bank is）
5. B（listen 用副词 carefully）
6. C（nine 去 e 加 th → ninth）
7. A（neither of + 复数 → 单数 is）
8. B（milk 不可数用 much）
9. C（累 → 停下来去休息 stop to have）
10. D（"下雨"与"还是出去"转折 Although）
