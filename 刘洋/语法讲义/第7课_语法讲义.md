> 学生：刘洋（八升九）｜ 对应教学计划 L7 ｜ 检测错点：stop to have / working 双空全反

---

## 一、语法概念

### 1. 规则与构成

- **动词不定式 to do**
  - 作宾语：want / decide / hope / plan **to do** sth.
  - 作目的状语："为了……"：She went to the library **to borrow** books.
- **stop doing / stop to do（重点辨析）**
  - **stop doing sth.** = 停止**正在做的**事：The workers stopped **working** the machines.（停下操作机器）
  - **stop to do sth.** = 停下来**去做**另一件事：The workers stopped **to have** a rest.（停下（工作）去休息）
- **make 复合宾语**
  - make sb **do** sth.（省 to 不定式）：The teacher made him **stay** after class.
  - make sth / sb + adj.：The good news made us **happy**.

### 2. 典例（6 句）

1. I want **to be** a teacher when I grow up.
2. She went to the library **to borrow** some books.（目的状语）
3. The workers were tired, so they stopped **to have** a rest.
4. They stopped **working** the machines and went home.
5. The good news made us **happy**.
6. The teacher made him **stay** after class.

### 3. 高频易错（5 条）

1. ❌ They stopped working the machines to have a rest.（语序反了）→ ✅ They stopped **to have** a rest.（累 → 停下来去休息）
2. ❌ stop to work 表"停止工作" → ✅ stop **working**（停止正在做的事用 doing）
3. ❌ The teacher made him to stay. → ✅ ...made him **stay**.（make 后接动词原形，省 to）
4. ❌ I want be a teacher. → ✅ I want **to be** a teacher.（want 后接 to do）
5. ❌ He decided going to Beijing. → ✅ He decided **to go** to Beijing.（decide 后接 to do）

### 4. 判诀

> **stop doing 停正在做，stop to do 停下来做；**
> **make 后接动词原形（省 to）；**
> **want / decide / hope / plan 后接 to do。**

## 二、当堂练习（2 : 6 : 2）

### 语法选择（2 题）

1. The workers were tired, so they stopped ___ a short rest, and then they stopped ___ the machines.
   A. to have; to work　B. to have; working　C. having; to work　D. having; working

2. The teacher made the noisy boy ___ outside the classroom.
   A. stand　B. to stand　C. standing　D. stood

### 选词填空（6 题，选项词：to have / working / to buy / to work / run / to run）

1. They stopped ___ a rest because they were tired.
2. The workers stopped ___ the machines at noon.
3. She went to the shop ___ some milk.
4. He decided ___ hard this term.
5. The coach made us ___ five laps (圈) every morning.
6. He promised ___ with us after school.

### 造句（2 题）

1. 用 "stop to do" 造一句（停下来去做某事）。
2. 用 "make sb do" 造一句。

---

## 三、答案与解析

**语法选择**：
1. B（累 → 停下来去休息 stop to have；休息后再停止操作机器 stop working）
2. A（make sb do，省 to，用动词原形 stand）

**选词填空**：
1. to have（累 → 停下来去休息，stop to have a rest）
2. working（stop working the machines 停止操作机器，用 doing 表"停止正在做的动作"）
3. to buy（去商店买牛奶，to buy 表目的）
4. to work（decide to work hard，decide 后接 to do）
5. run（make us run，make 后省 to，动词原形）
6. to run（promise to run，promise 后接 to do）

**造句参考答案**：
1. I stopped **to have** a rest because I was tired.
2. The bad weather made us **stay** at home.
