## 一、语法概念

### 1. 规则与构成

- **关系代词**：在从句中作主语/宾语
  - **who**（先行词是人）：The boy **who is standing** under the tree is my cousin.
  - **whom**（先行词是人，作宾语，可省）：The man (whom) you met is my uncle.
  - **which**（先行词是物）：I like the book **which** you gave me.
  - **that**（人/物都可）：The book **that** you gave me is interesting.
  - **whose**（表"某人的"，后接名词）：The girl **whose father** is a doctor is my classmate.
- **关系副词**：when（时间）、where（地点）
  - This is the house **where** I lived.（where = in which）
- **只用 that 的情形**（重点）：
  1. 先行词是**不定代词**（everything, nothing, something）
  2. 先行词被**最高级 / 序数词**修饰
  3. 先行词**既有人又有物**

### 2. 典例（6 句）

1. The boy **who** is standing under the tree is my cousin.
2. I like the book **which / that** you gave me.
3. She is the teacher **who** teaches us English.
4. This is the house **where** I lived.
5. I'll never forget the day **when** we met.
6. The girl **whose** father is a doctor is my classmate.

### 3. 高频易错（5 条）

1. ❌ The boy which is standing... → ✅ The boy **who**...（人用 who，不用 which）
2. ❌ The book who you gave me → ✅ The book **which/that**...（物用 which/that）
3. ❌ the house where he lives in → ✅ the house **where** he lives（where 已含"in"，不再加 in）
4. ❌ He is the best student which I know. → ✅ ...**that** I know.（最高级修饰，只用 that）
5. ❌ The girl who father is a doctor → ✅ The girl **whose** father...（表所属用 whose）

### 4. 判诀

> **人用 who/whose，物用 which/that；**
> **不定代词、最高级、序数词修饰、人+物 → 只用 that；**
> **where 表地点已含介词，when 表时间。**

## 二、当堂练习（2 : 6 : 2）

### 语法选择（2 题）

1. The boy ___ is standing under the tree is my cousin.
   A. which　B. who　C. where　D. whose

2. Everything ___ we saw at the museum was interesting.
   A. which　B. who　C. that　D. where

### 选词填空（6 题，选项词：who / which / that / whose / where / when）

1. The girl ___ is singing is my sister.
2. The book ___ is on the desk is mine.
3. Everything ___ he said is true.
4. The man ___ car is red is Mr. Green.
5. This is the school ___ I studied three years ago.
6. I'll never forget the day ___ I met you.

### 造句（2 题）

1. 用 **who** 造一句定语从句。
2. 用 **that**（先行词是物）造一句定语从句。

## 三、答案与解析

**语法选择**：
1. B（先行词 the boy 是人，作主语用 who）
2. C（先行词 everything 是不定代词，只用 that）

**选词填空**：
1. who（the girl 是人，作主语）
2. which（the book 是物）
3. that（everything 不定代词，只用 that）
4. whose（car 的车主"谁的"，表所属）
5. where（school 是地点，study 是不及物，用 where）
6. when（the day 是时间）

**造句参考答案**：
1. The man **who** is talking with my father is my teacher.
2. I like the movie **that** we watched last night.
