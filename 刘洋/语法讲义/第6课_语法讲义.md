## 一、语法概念

### 1. 规则与构成

**（一）主谓一致**
- **neither of / either of / each of / one of + 复数名词 → 谓语用单数**
  - Neither of the two boys **is** from Beijing.（两个男孩都不来自北京）
  - Each of us **has** a book.
- **there be 就近原则**：be 动词与最近的名词一致
  - There **is** a book and two pens on the desk.（a book 是单数，紧跟 there 后）

**（二）可数 / 不可数 + many/much/few/little**
- many / few + **可数复数**：many books、few apples
- much / little + **不可数**：much milk、little water
- a few / a little 表肯定（有一些）；few / little 表否定（几乎没有）
- 常见不可数：milk, water, bread, rice, tea, news, information

### 2. 典例（6 句）

1. Neither of the two boys **is** from Beijing.
2. Each of us **has** a dream.
3. There **is** a book and two pens on the desk.
4. There **isn't** much **milk** in the glass.
5. I have **many** books but **few** friends in the new class.（few 否定）
6. She has **a little** money, so she can buy the pen.（a little 肯定）

### 3. 高频易错（5 条）

1. ❌ Neither of the boys are here. → ✅ Neither of the boys **is** here.（neither of + 复数 → 单数谓语）
2. ❌ Each of them have a map. → ✅ Each of them **has** a map.（each of → 单数）
3. ❌ There are a book and two pens. → ✅ There **is** a book and two pens.（就近原则，靠近 be 的是 a book）
4. ❌ There isn't many milk. → ✅ There isn't **much** milk.（milk 不可数，用 much）
5. ❌ I have few water. → ✅ I have **little** water.（water 不可数，用 little）

### 4. 判诀

> **neither/each/either/one of + 复数，谓语一定用单数；**
> **there be 就近看第一个；**
> **many/few 配可数，much/little 配不可数。**

## 二、当堂练习（2 : 6 : 2）

### 语法选择（2 题）

1. Neither of the two boys ___ from Beijing, but both of them can speak Chinese.
   A. are　B. is　C. were　D. am

2. There isn't ___ milk in the glass. Could you give me ___ water?
   A. many; some　B. much; some　C. many; many　D. much; many

### 选词填空（6 题，选项词：is / are / has / have / much / many / few / a little）

1. Each of the students ___ a dictionary.
2. Neither of the answers ___ correct.
3. There ___ two apples and a banana on the plate.
4. How ___ money do you need?
5. I have ___ friends here, so I feel lonely.（几乎没有）
6. There is ___ water in the bottle. You can drink it.（有一点）

### 造句（2 题）

1. 用"neither of + 复数名词 + 单数谓语"造一句。
2. 用"much + 不可数名词"造一句。

---

## 三、答案与解析

**语法选择**：1. B（neither of + 复数 → 单数 is）；2. B（milk 不可数用 much；water 不可数，some 表"一些"用于请求/肯定）

**选词填空**：
1. has（each of → 单数，用 has）
2. is（neither of → 单数，用 is）
3. are（就近原则，two apples 是复数，紧跟 there 后）
4. much（money 不可数，用 how much）
5. few（few 表否定"几乎没有"，与 lonely 相符）
6. a little（a little 表肯定，water 不可数）

**造句参考答案**：
1. **Neither of the two answers is** right.
2. I don't have **much** time today.
