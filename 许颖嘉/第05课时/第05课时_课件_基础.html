新词速记

分组 · 搭配 · 词族

词新词速记 · 记忆地图
分组记 + 高频搭配，结构化成卡
三餐组
一日三餐 breakfast / lunch / dinner，搭配动词 have：have breakfast。
水果组
apple / banana / orange / fruit，泛指水果用 fruit（不可数）。
饮品组
water / milk / juice，量词搭配：a glass of milk / a cup of tea。
主食组
rice / bread / egg，不可数用 a piece of bread；可数用 two eggs。
状态词
hungry 饿（想吃饭）／ thirsty 渴（想喝水），搭配 be：I am hungry.
核心动词
eat 吃（固体）／ drink 喝（液体）／ like 喜欢，本课高频使用。
记忆策略
① 按三餐/水果/饮品/主食分组记；② 用搭配短语带动单词；③ 每词造一句。

近义 / 形近辨析

成对记忆 · 避免混淆

词近义词 / 形近词辨析
food / fruit
food 泛指食物；fruit 特指水果。healthy food 健康食品。
drink / eat
drink 喝（液体）；eat 吃（固体）。eat rice / drink water。
hungry / thirsty
hungry 饿；thirsty 渴。饿了想eat，渴了想drink。
breakfast / lunch / dinner
早/午/晚餐，都搭配 have：have breakfast。for + 餐名 表某一餐：for breakfast。
rice / noodles
rice 米饭（不可数）；noodles 面条（可数复数）。均为主食 staple food。
water / juice
water 水（最健康）；juice 果汁。a glass of water / a cup of juice。
辨析口诀
形近看首尾字母，词义靠搭配；food/fruit、drink/eat 最易混，多写三遍。
辨析不是死记，而是见词想搭档：breakfast→have，milk→a glass of，hungry→be hungry。

What特殊疑问句

法考点2 · What特殊疑问句
一 · 构成与用法
What 单独提问泛指"什么东西"；What + 名词提问限定类别（What sports 限定运动类）。疑问句中动词用原形，借助 do/does 提前。

What + 助动词(do/does) + 主语 + 动词原形 + …?

命令/建议。肯定 动词原形开头，否定 Don't + 原形，提议 Let's + 原形。
What 问句
对事物提问。公式 What + 助动词 + 主语 + 动词原形 + …?，助动词随主语（三单 does）。
like 用法
喜好表达。like + 名词 喜欢某物；like to do 喜欢做；三单 likes。
易混速记
Don't 后接原形不接 to；What 后助动词随主语变。

阅读 A · 篇章＋理解题

左文右题 · 边读边选

读阅读 A · Healthy Eating Habits（左文右题）
CORE · 课堂必做课堂精读 · 三篇都读，但只有 A 是课上必须完成

Lily is a student.

She has good eating habits.

Every morning, she has milk and bread for breakfast.

"What do you like for breakfast?" her mother asks.

"I like milk and bread," Lily answers.

For lunch, she has rice, vegetables and chicken at school.

In the evening, her family eat apples, bananas and oranges after dinner.

Lily drinks water every day.

She says, "Drink water, not cola! It is good for you.

" She does not like fast food.

"Don't eat too much fast food," she often says.

Fruit and milk help her grow tall and strong.

Lily is healthy and happy.

中文大意
莉莉每天有良好的饮食习惯：早餐在家喝牛奶吃面包，午餐在学校和同学吃米饭、蔬菜和鸡肉，晚餐和家人吃水果。她喜欢健康食物，不喜欢快餐，每天喝水，水果和牛奶帮助她健康成长。
长难句拆解
① She has good eating habits. —— have 表有（三单 has），eating habits 饮食习惯。
② Fruit and milk help her grow tall and strong. —— help sb. do sth. 帮助某人做某事。
📝 理解题 · 边读边选
1. What does Lily have for breakfast?
2. Where does Lily have lunch?
3. What fruit does her family eat for dinner?
4. Which food does Lily NOT like?
5. What is the main idea?
答案解析
题1 细节定位 For breakfast, I have milk and bread；题2 For lunch at school；题3 my family eat apples, bananas and oranges；题4 I do not like fast food；题5 主旨为健康饮食习惯。

阅读 B · 篇章＋理解题

左文右题 · 边读边选

读阅读 B · A Happy Sunday（左文右题）
EXTEND · 时间充足时做时间不够可跳过，不影响核心闭环

Sam gets up at seven on Sunday.

He is hungry.

He wants a big breakfast.

"What do you have for breakfast?" his mother asks.

"I like eggs and bread," Sam says.

Mother says, "Please wash your hands first.

" Sam washes his hands.

Then he eats two eggs and some bread.

He also drinks a glass of milk.

After breakfast, his mother says, "Don't forget to brush your teeth.

" Sam brushes his teeth.

Now Sam is full.

"What do you like for lunch?" his mother asks.

Sam smiles.

"I like rice and chicken.

And I like oranges after lunch!" he answers.

What a happy Sunday!.

中文大意
周日早晨山姆饿了，想要一顿丰盛早餐。他喜欢鸡蛋和面包，还喝了一杯牛奶。妈妈先让他洗手，饭后让他刷牙。午餐他想吃米饭、鸡肉和橘子。真是个快乐的周日。
长难句拆解
① Please wash your hands first. —— first 首先；祈使句表请求。
② Don't forget to brush your teeth. —— forget to do 忘记做某事。
📝 理解题 · 边读边选
1. When does Sam get up?
2. What does Sam like for breakfast?
3. What does his mother ask Sam to do first?
4. What does Sam drink?
5. What does Sam like for lunch?
答案解析
题1 He gets up at seven；题2 He likes eggs and bread；题3 Mother asks him to wash hands first；题4 He drinks a glass of milk；题5 He likes rice and chicken for lunch。
