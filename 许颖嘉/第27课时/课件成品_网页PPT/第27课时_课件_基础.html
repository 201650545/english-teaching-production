The Little Gardener

Last spring, ten-year-old Lily noticed that the empty lot (空地) near her house was full of weeds (杂草). She wanted to make it beautiful, so she decided to plant flowers.

First, Lily asked her parents for help. They bought some flower seeds and tools for her. Then, every weekend, Lily went to the lot with her mom. They cleaned the weeds, dug the soil (土壤), and planted the seeds together.

Weeks passed, and little green shoots (嫩芽) began to appear. Lily watered them every day. Slowly, colorful flowers started to bloom. Neighbors walked by and smiled. Some even stopped to pick up litter (垃圾) around the lot.

Six months later, the empty lot became a small garden with roses, tulips, and sunflowers. People called it "Lily\'s Garden". The community (社区) held a small party to celebrate.

Lily learned that one person\'s small action can make a big difference. She said, "I just love flowers, and I\'m happy that others can enjoy them too."

Now, Lily plans to plant more flowers next spring. She wants to make the whole street beautiful.

Why Do We Dream?

Dreams are one of the biggest mysteries in science. Most people dream several times every night, but no one knows exactly why we dream or what dreams mean.

Some scientists believe that dreams help us process (处理) our daily experiences. When we sleep, our brain sorts through (梳理) everything that happened during the day. It keeps important memories and throws away unimportant information.

Other scientists think dreams are like a safety practice. They believe that dreaming helps us prepare for difficult situations. For example, if you dream about being chased by a dog, your brain is practicing how to run away or fight back.

A recent study showed that students who dreamed about a task performed better the next day. This supports the idea that dreams help us learn and remember.

But some researchers disagree. They say dreams are just random (随机的) brain activity with no special meaning. Like a computer cleaning its hard drive, the brain just activates (激活) random thoughts during sleep.

Whatever the truth is, one thing is clear: dreams are a normal and important part of healthy sleep. Everyone dreams, even if they do not remember them.

How to Stay Focused in Class

Staying focused in class is important for every student. Here are some tips to help you pay attention and learn better. First, sit in the front row if possible. (1) _______ This helps you see the board clearly and hear the teacher better. It also makes it harder to sleep or look at your phone. Second, take notes by hand. ___2___ _______ Writing things down helps your brain remember the information. You do not need to write every word—just the key points. Third, ask questions when you do not understand. ___3___ _______ Teachers usually appreciate (欣赏) students who are interested in the lesson. Asking questions also helps you stay engaged. Fourth, avoid multitasking. ___4___ _______ Your brain can only focus on one thing at a time. If you check messages during class, you will miss important information. Finally, get enough sleep. ___5___ _______ A tired brain cannot focus well. Try to sleep at least eight hours every night.

C. It shows the teacher that you are paying attention and want to learn.
D. Doing two things at once means you do neither well.
E. Sleep is when your brain processes and stores everything you learned that day.

正确答案

📝 详细解析

C - The sentence is about asking questions, and this option explains how teachers feel about interested students.

E - The sentence is about getting enough sleep, and this option explains why sleep matters for learning.
