## 二、语法硬契约

### G07：名词所有格（'s / of）修饰法则

**口诀**：有生命加 's（Tom's book），无生命用 of（the leg of the table）

**公式**：
- 有生命单数：名词 + 's → Tom's pen
- 以s结尾复数：名词 + ' → the students' books
- 不以s结尾复数：名词 + 's → children's books
- 无生命：A of B（B的A） → the key of the box
- 并列各自拥有：A's and B's + 复数 → Tom's and Jack's pens
- 并列共同拥有：A and B's + 单数 → Tom and Jack's room

**例句**：
1. This is Tom's pen.（有生命加 's）
2. The legs of the table are short.（无生命用 of，倒装翻译）
3. The students' schoolbags are in the library.（复数s结尾只加撇号）

**防越级约束**：仅限 's 和 of 两种所有格，不引入双重所有格（a friend of mine 留至后续综合）

---

### G08：基数词 1-100 拼写与数词修饰名词

**口诀**：1-12 各不同，13-19 都加 teen，20-90 整十加 ty，几十几用连字符

**分段拼写表**：

| 范围 | 规律 | 示例 |
|:---|:---|:---|
| 1-12 | 各自独立 | one, two, three... twelve |
| 13-19 | 基础词 + teen | thirteen, fourteen... nineteen |
| 20-90 | 基础词 + ty | twenty, thirty... ninety |
| 几十几 | 整十-连字符-个位 | twenty-one, thirty-five |
| 100 | hundred | one hundred |

**特殊拼写易错**：fifteen（非fiveteen）、forty（非fourty）、eighty（非eightty）、thirty（非threety）、fifty（非fivety）

**数词修饰名词**：one + 单数名词；数词(>1) + 复数名词

**例句**：
1. I have two pens and three rulers.
2. There are twenty students in the library.
3. Call me at one three eight, zero zero zero, zero zero zero.

**防越级约束**：仅限基数词 1-100，不引入序数词（first/second/third 留至后续综合）

---

### G09：Where 引导疑问句与方位介词 in/on/under + There is/are 存在句

**口诀**：Where 问"在哪里"，in/on/under 答方位；There is 接单数，There are 接复数

**Where 疑问句**：
- Where is + 单数主语? → Where is my pen? → It is in the box.
- Where are + 复数主语? → Where are my keys? → They are on the table.

**方位介词**：
- in = 在……里面（The pen is in the box.）
- on = 在……上面（The cup is on the table.）
- under = 在……下面（The cat is under the chair.）

**There be 存在句**：
- 肯定单数：There is a pen on the desk.
- 肯定复数：There are two books in the box.
- 否定单数：There isn't a pen on the desk.
- 疑问单数：Is there a pen on the desk?
- 就近原则：There is a pen and two books on the desk.

**例句**：
1. Where is my eraser? — It is under the book.
2. There is a dictionary on the desk.
3. There are three pens in my pencil box.

**防越级约束**：仅限 in/on/under，不引入 behind/next to/in front of（留至 L4 G12）
