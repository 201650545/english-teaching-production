#### T1 代词系统（终极复习）｜G01, G02, G04, G33, G42

| 对比维度 | 人称代词主格/宾格 (G01) | 物主代词 (G02) | 指示代词 (G04) | some/any (G33) | 非人称 It (G42) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **功能** | 作主语(主格)/宾语(宾格) | 表示"谁的" | 指示远近单复 | 表示"一些" | 指代天气/时间/距离 |
| **关键形式** | I/me, he/him, she/her, we/us | my/mine, your/yours | this/that(单) these/those(复) | some(肯定) any(否定/疑问) | It is sunny. It is 3 o'clock. |
| **句中位置** | 主格在句首，宾格在动词/介词后 | 形容词性在名词前，名词性独立使用 | 句首或句中 | 名词前或独立使用 | 句首主语 |

- **记忆口诀**：主格作主语、宾格作宾语；my 后跟名词、mine 独立行；this 近 that 远、these 多 those 远；some 肯 any 否疑、It 指天时距。
- **典型例句**：He gave me his book. / This is my pen, that is yours. / I have some water. Do you have any? / It is raining now.
- **高频易错点**：① 主格宾格混用（×He gave she a book → ✓He gave her a book）；② my/mine 混淆（×This is my → ✓This is mine）；③ some 用于疑问句表请求时属例外（Would you like some tea? ✓）；④ It 非人称用法遗漏（×Weather is sunny → ✓It is sunny）。

#### T2 be 动词系统（终极复习）｜G03, G05, G19

| 对比维度 | be 现在时 (G03) | be 否定/疑问 (G05) | be 过去时 (G19) |
|---|---|---|---|
| **肯定句** | I am / You are / He is / They are | — | I was / You were / He was / They were |
| **否定句** | — | I am not / He isn't / They aren't | I wasn't / They weren't |
| **疑问句** | — | Are you...? Is he...? | Were you...? Was he...? |
| **搭配口诀** | 我用am你用are，is连着他她它，单数is复数are | be提前变疑问，not加后变否定 | was配单、were配复，过去时态要记住 |

- **记忆口诀**：现在 am/is/are，过去 was/were；be 提前变疑问，加 not 变否定。
- **典型例句**：I am a student. / Are you from China? / She was happy yesterday. / They weren't at home last night.
- **高频易错点**：① was/were 与主语不匹配（×He were → ✓He was）；② be 否定缩写遗漏（×are not → aren't）；③ 现在时与过去时 be 混用（时间状语 yesterday 却用 is）。

#### T3 疑问句系统（终极复习）｜G06, G09, G14, G22, G23, G35, G44

| 疑问词 | 考点 | 提问对象 | 回答结构 | 典型例句 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Who | G06 | 人（主语/宾语） | 人名/身份 | Who is he? — He is my teacher. |
| Where | G09 | 地点 | 介词+地点 | Where is the book? — It's on the desk. |
| What | G14 | 事物/职业 | 名词/名词短语 | What do you like? — I like basketball. |
| 六宝归纳 | G22 | What/Where/When/Why/Who/How | 系统归纳 | 特殊疑问词 + 一般疑问句语序 |
| 选择疑问 | G23 | or 选择 | 禁用 Yes/No，直接选 | Do you like tea or coffee? — Coffee. |
| how much/many | G35 | 不可数/价格 vs. 可数复数 | 数量/价格 | How much water? How many apples? |
| how often | G44 | 频率 | 频度副词/次数 | How often do you exercise? — Twice a week. |

- **记忆口诀**：疑问词放句首，后面跟着一般疑问句语序；how much 问不可数和价格，how many 问可数复数，how often 问频率；选择疑问 or 连接，回答不用 Yes/No。
- **高频易错点**：① how much vs how many 混淆（×How much apples → ✓How many apples）；② 选择疑问句回答用 Yes/No（×Yes, I like tea → ✓Tea）；③ how often 回答不用频度副词而用时间（×Every day is OK，但更标准用 once a day）。

#### T4 名词系统（终极复习）｜G07, G10, G17, G29, G32

| 对比维度 | 所有格 (G07) | 规则复数 (G10) | 可数/不可数 (G17) | 不规则复数 (G29) | 量词表达 (G32) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **规则** | 's（有生命）/ of（无生命） | -s / -es / -ies | 可数有复数、不可数无复数 | 特殊记忆 | 数词 + 量词 + of + 名词 |
| **示例** | Tom's book / the door of the room | book→books, box→boxes, baby→babies | apple(可数) / water(不可数) | man→men, child→children, foot→feet, tooth→teeth, sheep→sheep | a cup of tea, two bowls of rice |

- **记忆口诀**：有生命加 's，无生命用 of；规则复数 s/es/ies（辅音字母+y 变 ies）；不规则特殊记（man-men, child-children, foot-feet, tooth-teeth, sheep-sheep）；不可数名词加量词。
- **典型例句**：This is my sister's room. / There are three boxes on the table. / I'd like two bowls of soup.
- **高频易错点**：① 's 与 of 混用（×the room's door → ✓the door of the room）；② -ies 变化遗漏（×babys → ✓babies）；③ 不可数名词加 s（×waters → ✓water / two glasses of water）；④ sheep 单复数同形（×sheeps → ✓sheep）。

#### T5 介词系统（终极复习）｜G09, G12, G24, G30

|---|---|---|---|
| 方位基础 (G09) | in/on/under | in the box, on the desk, under the chair |
| 方位扩展 (G12) | behind/next to/in front of/between...and | behind the door, between A and B |
| 交通方向 (G24) | by/on/turn | by bus, on foot, turn left/right |
| 时间/规则 (G30) | in/on/at | in the morning, on Monday, at 3 o'clock, on time |

- **记忆口诀**：方位 in/on/under 打基础，behind/next to/between 来扩展；交通 by + 工具（on foot 例外）；时间 in 大段 on 具体天 at 精确点。
- **高频易错点**：① by bus vs on foot（by+交通工具，on+foot）；② between...and... 遗漏 and；③ in/on/at 时间介词混淆（×in Monday → ✓on Monday）。

#### T6 形容词系统（终极复习）｜G11, G39

- **防越级提醒**：**严禁比较级（-er/more）和最高级（-est/most）**，本课范围仅原级。
- **高频易错点**：① 形容词位置错误（×a boy tall → ✓a tall boy）；② 比较级越级（出现 taller/more interesting 属超纲）。

#### T7 动词与句型（终极复习）｜G13, G15, G16, G18, G34, G37

> 本主题含 **S1 螺旋链（to do 三阶）**，是动词搭配系统的核心。

| 对比维度 | 祈使句 (G13) | like+复数/to do (G15) | 一般现在时非三单 (G16) | want to do (G18) | would like to do (G34) | be vs have 外貌 (G37) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **结构** | Do/Don't + 动词原形 | like + 名词复数 / like to do | 主语(I/you/we/they) + V原形 | want + to do | would like + to do/sth. | be + 形容词 / have + 名词 |
| **否定** | Don't + V原形 | don't like | don't + V原形 | don't want to do | wouldn't like | — |
| **示例** | Open the door. / Don't run. | I like apples. I like to read. | I play soccer. They don't know. | I want to go home. | I would like some tea. | She is tall. She has long hair. |

- **to do 三阶螺旋（S1）**：G15 like to do（L5 首次接触）→ G18 want to do（L6 螺旋复现）→ G34 would like to do（L13 螺旋终点）。
- **记忆口诀**：祈使句 Do/Don't 开头；like 喜欢接复数或 to do；want 想要 to do；would like 礼貌想；外貌 be 描述高矮胖瘦，have 描述头发五官。
- **典型例句**：Don't be late. / I like playing basketball. / She wants to be a doctor. / Would you like to join us? / He is tall and has short hair.
- **高频易错点**：① want to do 遗漏 to（×want go → ✓want to go）；② would like to do 与 would like sth. 混淆；③ be vs have 外貌描述搭配错误（×She is long hair → ✓She has long hair）。

#### T8 数词系统（终极复习）｜G08, G36

| **范围** | 1–100 拼写 | yuan/dollar/total/pay/cost/spend |

| **示例** | I am twelve years old. There are twenty-five students. | It costs 5 dollars. I spent 20 yuan on the book. |

#### T9 时态系统（终极复习·重点）｜G16, G25, G26, G19, G20, G21, G46, G47, G48, G52, G54

> 本主题是 L21 的**核心对比区域**，涵盖全部三种时态的跨时态综合对比。含 **S2（一般现在时三阶）**和 **S6（时态综合链）**两条螺旋链。

**三态终极对照表**：

| 对比维度 | 一般现在时 | 一般过去时 | 现在进行时 |
|---|---|---|---|
| **考点** | G16(非三单) / G25(三单-s) / G26(does否定疑问) | G19(be过去) / G20(实义过去) / G21(时间状语) / G52(综合) | G46(be+V-ing) / G47(V-ing规则) / G48(标志词) |
| **结构** | 主语 + V原形 / V-s | 主语 + V-ed / was/were | 主语 + be + V-ing |
| **否定** | don't/doesn't + V原形 | didn't + V原形 / wasn't/weren't | isn't/aren't/am not + V-ing |
| **疑问** | Do/Does + 主语 + V原形? | Did + 主语 + V原形? / Was/Were + 主语? | Is/Are/Am + 主语 + V-ing? |
| **标志词** | always, usually, often, every day | yesterday, ago, last week | now, look!, listen!, at the moment |
| **用途** | 习惯/事实/规律 | 过去发生的事 | 此时此刻正在发生 |

- **三单变化规则 (G25)**：一般加 -s（plays）；s/x/ch/sh/o 结尾加 -es（watches, goes）；辅音+y 变 -ies（studies）。
- **V-ed 变化规则 (G20)**：一般加 -ed；e 结尾加 -d；辅音+y 变 -ied；重读闭音节双写加 -ed（stopped）。
- **V-ing 变化规则 (G47)**：一般加 -ing；e 结尾去 e 加 -ing（making）；重读闭音节双写加 -ing（running）。
- **记忆口诀**：现在时看三单（he/she/it 加 s/es/ies），过去时加 ed（规则变化要记牢），进行时 be + ing（now/look/listen 是信号）。do/does/did 后动词必还原！
- **跨时态对照 (G54)**：频度副词（always/usually）搭配一般现在时表习惯，搭配过去时表过去的习惯。
- **高频易错点**：① 三单动词变化遗漏（×He play → ✓He plays）；② does/did 后动词未还原（×Does he likes → ✓Does he like）；③ 进行时 be 动词遗漏（×He running → ✓He is running）；④ V-ing 双写遗漏（×runing → ✓running）；⑤ was/were 与主语不匹配。

#### T10 情态与冠词（终极复习）｜G28, G31, G41

| 对比维度 | can/must/have to (G28) | 冠词 a/an/the (G31) | Could/Would 礼貌请求 (G41) |
|---|---|---|---|
| **功能** | 能力(can) / 义务(must/have to) | 泛指(a/an) / 特指(the) | 礼貌请求/邀请 |
| **结构** | 情态动词 + V原形 | a+辅音 / an+元音 / the+特指 | Could/Would you please + V原形? |
| **示例** | I can swim. You must wear a uniform. | a book, an apple, the red car | Could you please help me? |

- **记忆口诀**：can 表能力，must 表必须，have to 表不得不；a 接辅音 an 接元音，the 表特指防裸奔；Could/Would you please 礼貌请求，动词用原形。
- **防裸奔规则 (G31)**：单数可数名词不能单独出现，必须加 a/an/the 或物主代词。
- **高频易错点**：① a/an 误用（×a apple → ✓an apple，看发音不是拼写）；② 情态动词后加 s（×He cans → ✓He can）；③ 单数可数名词裸奔（×I have book → ✓I have a book）。

#### T11 连词与句型（终极复习）｜G38, G40

| 对比维度 | why/because 因果 (G38) | 天气句型 (G40) |
|---|---|---|
| **结构** | Why + 疑问句? — Because + 陈述句 | How's the weather? / What's the weather like? — It's + 形容词 |
| **示例** | Why are you late? — Because I missed the bus. | How's the weather today? — It's sunny and warm. |

- **记忆口诀**：Why 提问 Because 答，因果关系要配对；天气两句型记牢——How's the weather 或 What's the weather like。
- **高频易错点**：① why 提问不用 because 回答（×Because I was tired. 作为 why 问句的回答是正确的，但作为 what 问句的回答是错误的）；② 天气句型遗漏 It（×Is sunny → ✓It is sunny）。

#### T12 交通与规则介词（终极复习）｜G24, G30

#### T13 频度副词与进行时（新增详讲）｜G43, G44, G45, G46, G47, G48

> **本主题为 L21 新增内容，需详细展开教学。**涵盖频度副词系统（G43–G45）与现在进行时系统（G46–G48）两大板块。

##### G43 频度副词（always, usually, often, sometimes, hardly, never）

**频度副词百分比与位置对照表**：

| 频度副词 | 百分比 | 中文 | 位置规则 | 例句 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| always | 100% | 总是 | be 后 / 实义动词前 | He is always late. / I always get up at 6. |
| usually | 80% | 通常 | be 后 / 实义动词前 | She is usually happy. / We usually play after school. |
| often | 60% | 经常 | be 后 / 实义动词前 | They are often busy. / He often reads books. |
| sometimes | 40% | 有时 | 句首 / be 后 / 实义动词前 | Sometimes I walk to school. / I sometimes go by bus. |
| hardly (ever) | 10% | 几乎不 | be 后 / 实义动词前 | I hardly ever eat fast food. |
| never | 0% | 从不 | be 后 / 实义动词前 | She is never angry. / He never tells lies. |

- **核心规则**：频度副词位置 = **"be 后实前"**（be 动词之后，实义动词之前）。
- **记忆口诀**：频度副词有六级，always→usually→often→sometimes→hardly→never，百分比从百到零；位置口诀"be 后实前"记心间。
- **高频易错点**：① 频度副词位置错误（×I get always up early → ✓I always get up early）；② hardly 与 hard 混淆（hardly = 几乎不，hard = 努力地/困难的）；③ never 本身表否定，不再加 not（×I do not never → ✓I never）。

##### G44 How often 引导的特殊疑问句与回答

| 提问结构 | 回答方式 | 示例 |
|---|---|---|
| How often + 一般疑问句语序? | 频度副词 / 次数表达 | How often do you exercise? — Twice a week. |
| — | always / usually / often... | — I usually exercise in the morning. |
| — | once a week / twice a month / three times a day | — I play tennis twice a week. |

- **次数表达公式**：once（一次）/ twice（两次）/ three times（三次及以上用 times）/ + a/an + 时间单位（a week, a month, a day）。
- **记忆口诀**：How often 问频率，回答用频度副词或"次数 + a + 时间"；once 是一次 twice 是两次，三次以上用 times。
- **高频易错点**：① How often 与 How long 混淆（How often 问频率，How long 问时长）；② one time/two times 替代 once/twice（应使用 once/twice，three times 及以上用 times）。

##### G45 百分比与生活习惯动词短语

| 习惯短语 | 中文 | 搭配场景 |
|---|---|---|
| exercise | 锻炼 | exercise every day / exercise three times a week |
| sleep 8 hours | 睡 8 小时 | I sleep eight hours every night. |
| read online | 在线阅读 | She often reads online news. |
| eat junk food | 吃垃圾食品 | He hardly ever eats junk food. |
| drink milk | 喝牛奶 | I always drink milk for breakfast. |

- **记忆口诀**：习惯短语配频度——exercise 锻炼、sleep 睡眠、read 阅读、eat 饮食，加频度副词描述生活习惯。
- **高频易错点**：exercise 名词/动词两用混淆（do exercise = 锻炼，exercise = 锻炼动词）。

##### G46 现在进行时结构（be + V-ing）

**现在进行时结构对照表**：

| 句型 | 结构 | 示例 |
|---|---|---|
| 肯定句 | 主语 + be(am/is/are) + V-ing | I am reading. / He is running. / They are playing. |
| 否定句 | 主语 + be + not + V-ing | I am not sleeping. / She isn't watching TV. |
| 疑问句 | Be + 主语 + V-ing? | Are you studying? / Is he cooking? |

- **核心规则**：现在进行时 = **be 动词 + 动词-ing**，be 动词随主语变化（am/is/are）。
- **记忆口诀**：进行时 be + ing，am/is/are 看主语；否定 be 后加 not，疑问 be 提前到句首。
- **高频易错点**：① be 动词遗漏（×He running → ✓He is running）；② be 动词与主语不匹配（×I is reading → ✓I am reading）；③ 否定句 not 位置错误（×He is running not → ✓He is not running）。

##### G47 V-ing 变化规则

**V-ing 三种变化规则对照表**：

| 规则类型 | 变化方法 | 适用条件 | 示例 |
|---|---|---|---|
| 直接加 -ing | V + ing | 大多数动词 | play→playing, read→reading, eat→eating |
| 去 e 加 -ing | V-e → V-ing | 以不发音 e 结尾 | make→making, write→writing, dance→dancing |
| 双写加 -ing | 双写尾字母 + ing | 重读闭音节（辅-元-辅结尾） | run→running, swim→swimming, sit→sitting |

- **记忆口诀**：直接加 ing 最常见，e 结尾去 e 再加 ing，重读闭音节双写尾字母加 ing。
- **双写判断口诀**：看末尾三个字母——"辅音+元音+辅音"且重读，就双写。
- **高频易错点**：① 去 e 遗漏（×makeing → ✓making）；② 双写遗漏（×runing → ✓running）；③ 不该双写的双写（×openning → ✓opening，因为 open 重音在第一个音节，末尾不双写）。

##### G48 现在进行时标志词识别

| 标志词 | 位置 | 示例 |
|---|---|---|
| now | 句末 | I am reading a book now. |
| Look! | 句首 | Look! The bus is coming. |
| Listen! | 句首 | Listen! Someone is singing. |
| at the moment | 句末 | She is cooking at the moment. |
| these days | 句末 | He is studying hard these days. |

- **记忆口诀**：看到 now、Look!、Listen!、at the moment，就要用进行时 be + V-ing。
- **高频易错点**：① 看到标志词却用一般现在时（×Look! He runs → ✓Look! He is running）；② Look!/Listen! 后逗号遗漏。

#### T14 复合不定代词与综合（新增详讲）｜G49, G50, G51, G52, G53, G54

> **本主题为 L21 新增内容，且包含语法授课的最高终点 G54。**涵盖复合不定代词系统（G49–G51）与时态综合运用（G52–G54）两大板块。

##### G49 复合不定代词分类与语义

**复合不定代词分类矩阵**：

| 类别 | some- 系列（肯定） | any- 系列（否定/疑问） | no- 系列（否定） | every- 系列（全部） |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 指人 | someone / somebody | anyone / anybody | no one / nobody | everyone / everybody |
| 指物 | something | anything | nothing | everything |
| 指地点 | somewhere | anywhere | nowhere | everywhere |

- **核心规则**：some- 系列用于肯定句；any- 系列用于否定句和疑问句；no- 系列本身表否定；every- 系列表全部。
- **例外**：some- 系列可用于表请求/邀请的疑问句（Would you like something to drink?）。
- **螺旋链 (S3)**：G33 some/any 基础辨析（L12 首次）→ G49 复合不定代词（L19 升级），二阶完成。
- **记忆口诀**：some 肯定 any 否疑，no 本身就是否定，every 全部都包含；some- any- no- every- 加 body/thing/where。
- **典型例句**：Someone is knocking at the door. / I didn't see anyone. / There is nothing in the box. / Everyone is here.
- **高频易错点**：① some-/any- 使用场景混淆（否定句用 someone → ✓anyone）；② no one 与 nobody 语义等价但拼写不同；③ everyone 与 every one 区别（everyone = 所有人，every one = 每一个[可接 of]）。

##### G50 形容词修饰复合不定代词强制后置

| 语序规则 | 错误语序 | 正确语序 |
|---|---|---|
| 形容词必须放在复合不定代词**之后** | ×something interesting → ✓something interesting（正确） | ✓something interesting |
| — | ×interesting something | ✓something interesting |
| — | ×anything new | ✓anything new |
| — | ×nothing special | ✓nothing special |

- **核心规则**：形容词修饰复合不定代词时，**形容词必须后置**（放在不定代词之后），与汉语语序相反。
- **记忆口诀**：复合不定代词 + 形容词，形容词往后站！something good, anything new, nothing special, everything possible。
- **典型例句**：I have something important to tell you. / Is there anything interesting in the newspaper? / There is nothing wrong with you.
- **高频易错点**：① 形容词前置（×important something → ✓something important）；② good/well 混淆（×something well → ✓something good）。

##### G51 复合不定代词作主语，谓语动词用单数

| 主语 | 谓语形式 | 示例 |
|---|---|---|
| someone / somebody | 单数（is/does/V-s） | Someone is waiting for you. |
| anyone / anybody | 单数（is/does/V-s） | Is anyone here? |
| no one / nobody | 单数（is/does/V-s） | Nobody knows the answer. |
| everyone / everybody | 单数（is/does/V-s） | Everyone is happy. |
| something / anything / nothing / everything | 单数（is/does/V-s） | Everything is ready. Nothing is impossible. |

- **核心规则**：复合不定代词作主语时，**谓语动词一律用单数**（第三人称单数形式）。
- **记忆口诀**：复合代词作主语，谓语动词用单数——is/was/has/does/V-s。
- **典型例句**：Everyone likes ice cream. / Something is wrong with my phone. / Nobody was at home yesterday.
- **高频易错点**：① 谓语用复数（×Everyone are happy → ✓Everyone is happy）；② everyone 语义为"所有人"却误用复数动词。

##### G52 一般过去时全套综合与混淆项排除

**过去时 be vs 实义动词终极对照表**：

| 对比维度 | be 动词过去时 (G19) | 实义动词过去时 (G20) | 综合混淆排除 (G52) |
|---|---|---|---|
| 肯定句 | He was happy. / They were tired. | He played soccer. / They went home. | 判断用 was/were 还是 V-ed：看动词类型 |
| 否定句 | was/were + not | didn't + V原形 | be 过去否定加 not，实义过去否定用 didn't + 还原 |
| 疑问句 | Was/Were + 主语? | Did + 主语 + V原形? | be 过去疑问直接提前，实义过去疑问用 Did + 还原 |
| 关键区分 | be 动词本身变形 | 借助 did，动词还原 | was/were 后接形容词/名词；did 后接动词原形 |

- **综合排除策略**：① 看时间状语→确认过去时；② 看动词类型→be 动词用 was/were，实义动词用 did；③ 否定/疑问→be 直接加 not/提前，实义用 didn't/Did + 动词还原。
- **记忆口诀**：过去时两套系统——be 动词变 was/were，实义动词借 did 帮忙；否定疑问 be 自变，实义 did 后必还原。
- **高频易错点**：① was/were 与 did 混用（×He didn't was happy → ✓He wasn't happy）；② didn't 后动词未还原（×He didn't went → ✓He didn't go）；③ Did 疑问句动词未还原（×Did he played → ✓Did he play）。

##### G53 复合不定代词与形容词后置在过去时中的综合

| 综合考点 | 结构 | 示例 |
|---|---|---|
| 复合不定代词 + 形容词后置 + 过去时 | 主语(someone...) + was/were + 形容词 + 后置形容词 | Someone important came yesterday. |
| 否定句综合 | nobody/nothing + wasn't/weren't 或 didn't + V原形 | Nothing special happened. / Nobody said anything. |
| 疑问句综合 | Did + anyone + V原形 + 形容词? | Did anyone see anything strange? |

- **核心规则**：将 G49（复合不定代词）+ G50（形容词后置）+ G51（单数谓语）+ G52（过去时）四考点综合运用。
- **记忆口诀**：复合代词单数谓语，形容词后置，过去时用 was/were 或 did，四合一综合考。
- **典型例句**：I bought something interesting yesterday. / Did anyone tell you anything important? / Nobody was hurt in the accident. / She found nothing valuable in the old box.
- **高频易错点**：① 形容词未后置（×I bought interesting something → ✓I bought something interesting）；② 单数谓语用错（×Everyone were → ✓Everyone was）；③ 过去时 did 后未还原（×Did anyone saw → ✓Did anyone see）。

##### G54 频度副词与过去时间的跨时态对照（语法授课最高终点）

**跨时态终极对照表**：

| 时态 | 频度副词位置 | 标志词 | 示例 |
|---|---|---|---|
| 一般现在时 + 频度副词 | be 后 / 实前 | always, usually, every day | He always gets up at 6. / She is usually happy. |
| 一般过去时 + 频度副词 | was/were 后 / did 前 | always, usually, yesterday, last week | He always walked to school last year. / She was usually late last month. |

- **核心规则**：频度副词在一般现在时和一般过去时中**位置规则相同**（be 后实前），但动词形式不同（现在时 V/V-s，过去时 V-ed/was/were）。
- **语法授课最高终点**：G54 是全部 54 个语法考点的最后一个，标志着语法授课体系的完成。L21–L40 绝不再教新语法。
- **记忆口诀**：频度副词位置不变——be 后实前，现在时配 V/V-s，过去时配 V-ed/was/were；跨时态对照是终点，54 考点到此全。
- **典型例句**：I usually eat breakfast at 7.（现在习惯）/ I usually ate breakfast at 7 when I was young.（过去习惯）/ She was always the best student last year.（过去时 + 频度）
- **高频易错点**：① 过去时中频度副词位置错误（×I walked always to school → ✓I always walked to school）；② 过去时动词形式错误（×I always walk yesterday → ✓I always walked yesterday）；③ was/were 与频度副词顺序（×always was → ✓was always）。
