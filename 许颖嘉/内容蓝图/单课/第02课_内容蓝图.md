## 二、语法详案（3 考点）

### 语法①：指示代词 this/that/these/those 单复数及问答（G04）

**口诀**：
> 近处单数 this，远处单数 that；近处复数 these，远处复数 those

**规律对照表（按远近×单复数排列，矩阵记忆）**：

| | 近处（这） | 远处（那） | 记忆规律 |
|---|---|---|---|
| **单数** | **this**（这个） | **that**（那个） | this→that，i 变 a |
| **复数** | **these**（这些） | **those**（那些） | this→these，that→those，加 s 变复 |

> **规律记忆法**：
> - 第一行记单数：this（近）→ that（远），字母 i 变 a
> - 第二行记复数：在单数后加 s，但 this→these（is 变 ese），that→those（at 变 ose）
> - **this/these 指近处**，**that/those 指远处**

**问答句型**：

| 问句 | 答句 | 说明 |
|---|---|---|
| Is this your father? | Yes, it is. / No, it isn't. | 单数用 Is，答用 it |
| Is that your mother? | Yes, it is. / No, it isn't. | 单数用 Is，答用 it |
| Are these your brothers? | Yes, they are. / No, they aren't. | 复数用 Are，答用 they |
| Are those your sisters? | Yes, they are. / No, they aren't. | 复数用 Are，答用 they |

**例句**：
1. This is my father.（近处单数）
2. That is my mother.（远处单数）
3. These are my brothers.（近处复数）
4. Those are my sisters.（远处复数）

**中考辨析**：
- 指示代词做主语时，单数用 is，复数用 are
- 回答 this/that 的疑问句时，答语用 it（不用 this/that）
- 回答 these/those 的疑问句时，答语用 they（不用 these/those）

**易错点**：
- ❌ This are my father. → ✅ This is my father.（this 单数用 is）
- ❌ Are these your brother? → ✅ Are these your brothers?（these 复数，名词也变复数）
- ❌ Is this your father? Yes, this is. → ✅ Yes, it is.（答语用 it）

**防越级约束**：仅限 this/that/these/those 四词指代，不引入 such 指示代词

### 语法②：be 动词否定句与一般疑问句提前法则（G05）

**口诀**：
> 否定加 not 紧跟 be，疑问把 be 提到主语前

**公式矩阵**：

| 句型 | 公式 | 示例 |
|---|---|---|
| 肯定句 | 主语 + be + ... | This is my father. |
| 否定句 | 主语 + be + not + ... | This is not my father. / This isn't my father. |
| 一般疑问句 | Be + 主语 + ...? | Is this your father? |
| 肯定回答 | Yes, 主语 + be. | Yes, it is. |
| 否定回答 | No, 主语 + be + not. | No, it isn't. |

**be 动词缩写表**：

| 完整形式 | 缩写形式 | 完整形式 | 缩写形式 |
|---|---|---|---|
| is not | isn't | are not | aren't |
| I am | I'm | is not → | isn't |
| is not | isn't | are not | aren't |

> **注意**：am not 没有缩写形式，只能写 I am not。

**例句**：
1. 肯定：This is my photo. → 否定：This isn't my photo.
2. 肯定：These are my parents. → 否定：These aren't my parents.
3. 肯定：That is my uncle. → 疑问：Is that your uncle? → 回答：Yes, it is. / No, it isn't.

**中考辨析**：
- 否定句中 not 必须放在 be 动词之后，实义动词之前
- 一般疑问句将 be 动词提到句首，首字母大写
- 否定回答不能用缩写：No, it isn't. ✅ / No, it's not. ✅ / No, it is not. ✅

**易错点**：
- ❌ This no is my father. → ✅ This is not my father.（否定用 not，不用 no）
- ❌ Is this your father? Yes, this is. → ✅ Yes, it is.（答语主语用 it）
- ❌ Are not these your books? → ✅ Aren't these your books?（缩写疑问句）

**防越级约束**：仅限现在时 be（am/is/are）的否定和疑问，不涉及过去时（was/were 留至 L8）

### 语法③：Who 引导的特殊疑问句与回答句型（G06）

**口诀**：
> Who 问"是谁"，回答用主格 + be + 身份/称呼

**句型矩阵**：

| 问句类型 | 句型 | 示例问句 | 示例回答 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Who is + 单数主语? | Who is + he/she/this/that? | Who is that man? | He is my father. |
| Who are + 复数主语? | Who are + they/these/those? | Who are those people? | They are my parents. |
| Who + be + 人名? | Who is + Tom/Lily? | Who is Lily? | She is my sister. |

**例句**：
1. Who is this? — This is my brother, Tom.
2. Who are they? — They are my grandparents.
3. Who is that girl? — She is my cousin, Lily.

**中考辨析**：
- Who 问人，What 问事物，Where 问地点
- Who is...? 的回答可以用人名或身份：He is Tom. / He is my father.
- Who is this/that? 回答用 He/She（不用 This/That）

**易错点**：
- ❌ Who is that? That is my father. → ✅ He is my father.（回答用 He/She）
- ❌ Who are they? They are teacher. → ✅ They are teachers.（复数主语配复数名词）
- ❌ Who is she? She name is Lily. → ✅ Her name is Lily.（用形容词性物主代词）

**防越级约束**：仅限 Who 引导的疑问句，不引入 Whose（谁的）和 Whom（宾格谁）
