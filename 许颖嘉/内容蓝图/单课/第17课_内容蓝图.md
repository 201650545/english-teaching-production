## 二、语法详案（3 考点）

### 语法①：频度副词百分比与位置（G43）

**口诀**：
> 频度副词六兄弟，always 到 never 百分降；be 后实前是位置，百分比来表达频

**频度副词百分比对照表**：

| 频度副词 | 百分比 | 中文 | 位置规则 | 示例 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| always | 100% | 总是 | be 后 / 实义动词前 | She **always** is happy. → She is **always** happy. |
| usually | 80% | 通常 | be 后 / 实义动词前 | I **usually** get up at six. → I get up **usually**... → I usually get up... |
| often | 60% | 经常 | be 后 / 实义动词前 | He **often** plays sports. |
| sometimes | 40% | 有时 | be 后 / 实义动词前 / 句首 | **Sometimes** I walk to school. |
| hardly (ever) | 10% | 几乎不 | be 后 / 实义动词前 | He **hardly ever** eats junk food. |
| never | 0% | 从不 | be 后 / 实义动词前 | I **never** drink coffee. |

> **规律记忆法（be 后实前规则）**：
> - **be 动词后**：She is **always** happy. / They are **never** late.
> - **实义动词前**：I **usually** get up at six. / He **often** reads books.
> - **助动词后**：I don't **often** go there. / She doesn't **usually** eat out.

**频度副词位置对比表**：

| 句型类型 | 频度副词位置 | 正确示例 | 错误示例 |
|---|---|---|---|
| be 动词句 | be 动词**之后** | She is **always** busy. | ❌ She always is busy. |
| 实义动词句 | 实义动词**之前** | He **often** plays basketball. | ❌ He plays often basketball. |
| 助动词句 | 助动词与实义动词**之间** | I don't **usually** eat out. | ❌ I usually don't eat out. |
| 情态动词句 | 情态动词与实义动词**之间** | You should **always** be careful. | ❌ You always should be careful. |

**例句**：
1. I usually get up at six o'clock.（实义动词前）
2. She is always happy and energetic.（be 动词后）
3. They hardly ever eat junk food.（实义动词前，hardly ever 连用）
4. We sometimes go shopping on weekends.（实义动词前）
5. He never drinks coffee or tea.（实义动词前）

**中考辨析**：
- always → usually → often → sometimes → hardly ever → never：频度从高到低排列
- hardly ever = almost never（几乎不），hardly 后常接 ever
- 频度副词放在 be 后、实义动词前是中考高频考点
- sometimes 可放句首：Sometimes I walk to school. ✅

**易错点**：
- ❌ She always is happy. → ✅ She is **always** happy.（be 动词后）
- ❌ He plays often basketball. → ✅ He **often** plays basketball.（实义动词前）
- ❌ I usually don't eat out. → ✅ I don't **usually** eat out.（助动词后）
- ❌ He hardly eats junk food. → ✅ He **hardly ever** eats junk food.（hardly 后加 ever）

**防越级约束**：首次引入频度副词，仅限 always/usually/often/sometimes/hardly ever/never 六个核心词，不引入其他频度表达（如 frequently, occasionally 等留至后续综合）

### 语法②：How often 引导的特殊疑问句与回答（G44）

**口诀**：
> How often 问频率，回答用 times 加周期；once 一次 twice 两次，three times 三次记心间

**How often 问句与回答配对表**：

| 问句句型 | 回答句型 | 示例 |
|---|---|---|
| How often + do/does + 主语 + V? | once/twice/three times a week | How often do you exercise? — Twice a week. |
| How often + do/does + 主语 + V? | every day / every week | How often does she read? — Every day. |
| How often + do/does + 主语 + V? | 频度副词 | How often do you eat out? — Sometimes. |

**频次表达对照表**：

| 频次表达 | 含义 | 示例 |
|---|---|---|
| once a week | 每周一次 | I jog once a week. |
| twice a week | 每周两次 | She jogs twice a week. |
| three times a week | 每周三次 | He exercises three times a week. |
| four times a month | 每月四次 | We meet four times a month. |
| every day | 每天 | I read English every day. |
| every other day | 每隔一天 | He jogs every other day. |

> **规律记忆法（times 表达三步法）**：
> - 第一步：基数词 + times（once 特殊，twice 特殊，three times 及以上用 基数词+times）
> - 第二步：+ a/an + 时间单位（a week / a day / a month）
> - 第三步：once = one time（特殊变形），twice = two times（特殊变形）

**特殊变形记忆**：
| 基数词 | 频次词 | 示例 |
|---|---|---|
| one | once | once a day（每天一次） |
| two | twice | twice a week（每周两次） |
| three | three times | three times a week（每周三次） |
| four | four times | four times a month（每月四次） |

**例句**：
1. How often do you exercise? — I exercise twice a week.（问频率 + times 回答）
2. How often does she watch television? — She hardly ever watches TV.（问频率 + 频度副词回答）
3. How often do they go jogging? — They jog every day.（问频率 + every day 回答）
4. How often does he read online? — He reads online three times a week.（问频率 + times 回答）

**中考辨析**：
- How often 问频率（多久一次），How long 问时长（多长时间），How far 问距离（多远）
- 回答 How often 可用频度副词或 times 表达
- once/twice 是特殊变形，不可说 one time/two time（但在口语中 one time 偶尔可接受，中考以 once/twice 为标准）

**易错点**：
- ❌ How often do you exercise? — **Two times** a week. → ✅ **Twice** a week.（两次用 twice）
- ❌ How **long** do you exercise? — Twice a week. → ✅ How **often** do you exercise?（问频率用 how often）
- ❌ one times a week → ✅ **once** a week（一次用 once）
- ❌ three time a week → ✅ three **times** a week（三次用 times，加 s）

**防越级约束**：螺旋自 G35（how much/many），补齐 how often；不引入 how soon（多久以后）和 how long（多长时间）的辨析（留至后续综合）

### 语法③：百分比与生活习惯动词短语（G45）

**口诀**：
> 百分比加 of 名词，主谓一致看名词；生活习惯动词配，exercise sleep read 记牢

**百分比表达结构表**：

| 结构 | 含义 | 示例 | 谓语动词 |
|---|---|---|---|
| 数字 + percent of + 不可数名词 | …的百分之… | Eighty percent of water is clean. | 单数 |
| 数字 + percent of + 单数名词 | …的百分之… | Eighty percent of the class is here. | 单数 |
| 数字 + percent of + 复数名词 | …的百分之… | Eighty percent of students like sports. | 复数 |

> **规律记忆法（百分比主谓一致三步法）**：
> - 第一步：识别 "数字 percent of + 名词" 结构
> - 第二步：看 of 后面的名词是可数还是不可数
> - 第三步：可数复数→谓语复数；不可数/可数单数→谓语单数

**生活习惯动词短语搭配表**：

| 动词短语 | 含义 | 频度搭配 | 示例 |
|---|---|---|---|
| exercise | 锻炼 | usually / often / regularly | I usually exercise in the morning. |
| sleep 8 hours | 睡 8 小时 | always / usually | She always sleeps 8 hours. |
| read online | 在线阅读 | often / sometimes | He often reads news online. |
| watch television | 看电视 | sometimes / hardly ever | They sometimes watch television. |
| eat junk food | 吃垃圾食品 | hardly ever / never | We hardly ever eat junk food. |
| drink water | 喝水 | always / usually | I always drink water in the morning. |
| go jogging | 去慢跑 | often / regularly | He goes jogging regularly. |

**例句**：
1. Eighty percent of students exercise every day.（百分比 + 复数名词 + 复数谓语）
2. Sixty percent of the water is clean.（百分比 + 不可数名词 + 单数谓语）
3. Ninety percent of the class likes English.（百分比 + 单数集合名词 + 单数谓语）
4. She usually sleeps 8 hours and often reads online.（频度副词 + 生活习惯短语）

**中考辨析**：
- percent 后不加 s：eighty percent ✅ / eighty percents ❌
- percent of + 复数名词 → 谓语用复数；percent of + 不可数名词 → 谓语用单数
- "percent of + 集合名词(the class)"在中考中谓语可用单数或复数，以单数为标准

**易错点**：
- ❌ eighty **percents** → ✅ eighty **percent**（percent 不加 s）
- ❌ Eighty percent of students **is** here. → ✅ Eighty percent of students **are** here.（复数名词→复数谓语）
- ❌ Sixty percent of water **are** clean. → ✅ Sixty percent of water **is** clean.（不可数名词→单数谓语）
- ❌ He **usually sleep** 8 hours. → ✅ He **usually sleeps** 8 hours.（三单加 s）

**防越级约束**：仅限健康习惯搭配（exercise, sleep, read, watch TV, eat, drink, jog），不引入其他场景的百分比表达

## 四、拼读详案

### 4.1 本课规则（Stage 3 拼读 L17）

**规则名称**：辅音连缀规则
**核心口诀**："两个辅音连一起，各发各的音不停"

| 拼读模式 | 示例 | 发音要点 |
|---|---|---|
| bl /cl /fl /gl /pl /sl | blue, class, fly, glass, play, slow | b/k/f/g/p/s + l 连缀，各发各音 |
| br /cr /dr /fr /gr /tr | brown, cry, draw, friend, green, tree | b/k/d/f/g/t + r 连缀，各发各音 |
| st /sp /sk /sm /sn | stop, speak, sky, small, snow | s + t/p/k/m/n 连缀，各发各音 |

> **规则要点**：辅音连缀是两个辅音字母连在一起，各自发音，不合并为一个音素。与二合字母（th/sh/ch）不同——二合字母两个字母发一个音，连缀两个字母发两个音。对比：bl（两个音 /b/+/l/）vs th（一个音 /θ/）。
