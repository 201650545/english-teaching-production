### 2.2 各主题详细对比表

#### T1 代词系统（G01 + G02 + G04，3 考点）

**包含考点**：
- G01：人称代词主格与宾格（I/me, he/him, she/her）
- G02：形容词性与名词性物主代词（my/mine, your/yours）
- G04：指示代词（this/that/these/those）单复数及问答

**核心对比表格——代词全系统一览**：

| 人称 | 主格（作主语） | 宾格（作宾语） | 形容词性物主（+名词） | 名词性物主（独立用） | 指示代词（单数） | 指示代词（复数） |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 第一人称单数 | **I** | **me** | **my** | **mine** | this / that | these / those |
| 第二人称单数 | you | you | your | yours | this / that | these / those |
| 第三人称单数（男） | he | him | his | his | this / that | these / those |
| 第三人称单数（女） | she | her | her | hers | this / that | these / those |
| 第三人称单数（物） | it | it | its | its | this / that | these / those |
| 第一人称复数 | we | us | our | ours | these / those | these / those |
| 第二人称复数 | you | you | your | yours | these / those | these / those |
| 第三人称复数 | they | them | their | theirs | these / those | these / those |

**用法对比**：

| 代词类型 | 位置 | 功能 | 示例 |
|---|---|---|---|
| 主格 | 句首/谓语前 | 作主语 | **I** am a student. **He** is my friend. |
| 宾格 | 动词后/介词后 | 作宾语 | Help **me**. Look at **him**. Give it to **her**. |
| 形容词性物主 | 名词前 | 修饰名词 | This is **my** book. **Her** name is Lily. |
| 名词性物主 | 独立使用 | = 形容词性物主 + 名词 | This book is **mine**. = This is **my** book. |
| 指示代词（单数） | 句首/句中 | 指代近处/远处单数 | **This** is a pen. **That** is a ruler. |
| 指示代词（复数） | 句首/句中 | 指代近处/远处复数 | **These** are books. **Those** are rulers. |

**记忆口诀**：
> 主格当主语，宾格跟介动；形物接名词，名物独当家；
> this 近来 that 远，these 多来 those 远多；
> my book 是形物，mine 等于 my book 省名词。

**典型例句**：
1. **I** like **him**.（主格 + 宾格）
2. **She** is **my** sister. **Her** name is Anna.（主格 + 形容词性物主）
3. This pen is **mine**. That is **yours**.（名词性物主）
4. **This** is my friend. **These** are my friends.（指示代词单复数）
5. Look at **them**. **They** are happy.（宾格 + 主格）

**高频易错点**：
| 易错点 | 错误示例 | 正确示例 | 辨析 |
|---|---|---|---|
| 主宾格混用 | Help I. | Help **me**. | 动词后用宾格 |
| 形物/名物混用 | This is mine book. | This is **my** book. | 形容词性物主后必须接名词 |
| 名物后多接名词 | This is her book, that is yours book. | This is her book, that is **yours**. | 名词性物主后不能再接名词 |
| its/it's 混淆 | Its a cat. | **It's** a cat. / **Its** tail is long. | it's = it is；its = 物主代词 |
| 指示代词单复数错 | This are books. | **These** are books. | this/these 单复数配对 |

#### T2 be 动词系统（G03 + G05，2 考点）

**包含考点**：
- G03：be 动词（am/is/are）与人称代词主格搭配口诀
- G05：be 动词否定句与一般疑问句提前法则

**核心对比表格——be 动词搭配与变形全系统**：

| 人称主格 | be 动词（肯定） | 否定式（完整） | 否定式（缩写） | 一般疑问句 | 肯定回答 | 否定回答 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| I | am | am not | —（am not 无缩写） | Am I...? | Yes, I am. | No, I am not. |
| You | are | are not | aren't | Are you...? | Yes, I am. | No, I'm not. |
| He | is | is not | isn't | Is he...? | Yes, he is. | No, he isn't. |
| She | is | is not | isn't | Is she...? | Yes, she is. | No, she isn't. |
| It | is | is not | isn't | Is it...? | Yes, it is. | No, it isn't. |
| We | are | are not | aren't | Are we...? | Yes, we are. | No, we aren't. |
| They | are | are not | aren't | Are they...? | Yes, they are. | No, they aren't. |

**句型转换对比**：

| 转换类型 | 规则 | 示例 |
|---|---|---|
| 肯定句 → 否定句 | be 后加 not | He **is** a teacher. → He **is not / isn't** a teacher. |
| 肯定句 → 一般疑问句 | be 提前到句首，首字母大写 | She **is** happy. → **Is** she happy? |
| 一般疑问句 → 回答 | Yes/No + 主语代词 + be | Is he a student? → Yes, he **is**. / No, he **isn't**. |
| 肯定句 → 否定疑问句 | be + not 提前（或缩写提前） | Aren't you a student? |

**记忆口诀**：
> 我用 am 你用 are，is 连着他她它；
> 单数 is 复数 are，am 只跟 I 搭配；
> 否定 be 后加 not，缩写 isn't 和 aren't；
> 疑问 be 词往前跑，句首大写别忘掉。

**典型例句**：
1. I **am** a student. You **are** my teacher.（肯定句）
2. He **isn't** at home. They **aren't** here.（否定句）
3. **Is** she your sister? — Yes, she **is**.（一般疑问句 + 回答）
4. **Are** these your books? — No, they **aren't**.（复数疑问句）
5. We **are** in the same class.（复数主语）

**高频易错点**：
| 易错点 | 错误示例 | 正确示例 | 辨析 |
|---|---|---|---|
| be 动词选错 | I is a student. | I **am** a student. | I 搭配 am，不搭配 is |
| 否定句用 don't | He don't be here. | He **isn't** here. | be 否定直接加 not，不用 don't |
| 疑问句不提前 | She is happy? | **Is** she happy? | 疑问句 be 必须提前 |
| 回答用错代词 | Are you Tom? — Yes, he is. | Yes, **I** am. | 问 you 答 I |
| am not 缩写错误 | I amn't a teacher. | I **am not** a teacher. | am not 没有缩写形式 |

#### T3 疑问句系统（G06 + G09 + G14，3 考点）

**包含考点**：
- G06：Who 引导的特殊疑问句与回答句型
- G09：Where 引导疑问句与方位介词 in/on/under + There is/are 存在句
- G14：What 引导的特殊疑问句（What sports do you like?）

**核心对比表格——三大疑问词结构与回答**：

| 疑问词 | 考点 | 提问对象 | 句型结构 | 典型问句 | 回答方式 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Who** | G06 | 人（身份/关系） | Who + is/are + 主语? | Who is that girl? Who are they? | She is my sister. / They are my friends. |
| **Where** | G09 | 地点/位置 | Where + is/are + 主语? | Where is my book? Where are they? | It's on the desk. / They are in the room. |
| **What** | G14 | 事物/爱好/运动 | What + 名词 + do you + 动词? | What sports do you like? What do you have? | I like basketball. / I have a pen. |

**There be 存在句对比（G09 核心结构）**：

| 结构 | 用法 | 示例 |
|---|---|---|
| There is + 单数名词/不可数名词 | 表示"某处有"（单数） | There is a book on the desk. There is milk in the cup. |
| There are + 复数名词 | 表示"某处有"（复数） | There are two chairs in the room. |
| There is/are 否定 | is/are 后加 not | There isn't a pen here. There aren't any books. |
| There is/are 疑问 | is/are 提前 | Is there a book on the desk? Are there any chairs? |

**记忆口诀**：
> Who 问人是谁，is/are 跟在后；
> Where 问在哪里，方位介词来回答；
> What 问是什么，事物爱好都能答；
> There be 表存在，is 单 are 复要分清。

**典型例句**：
1. **Who** is that boy? — He is my brother, Tom.（Who 问人）
2. **Where** is the library? — It's next to the classroom.（Where 问地点）
3. **What** sports do you like? — I like soccer and basketball.（What 问爱好）
4. **There is** a clock on the table. **There are** some books under it.（There be 存在句）
5. Is there a pen in the box? — Yes, there is.（There be 疑问句）

**高频易错点**：
| 易错点 | 错误示例 | 正确示例 | 辨析 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Who 后 be 动词错 | Who are that girl? | Who **is** that girl? | 单数主语用 is |
| Where 回答缺介词 | It's the desk. | It's **on** the desk. | Where 回答必须带方位介词 |
| There be 主谓一致错 | There is two books. | There **are** two books. | 复数名词用 are |
| There be 与 have 混淆 | There has a book. | There **is** a book. / I **have** a book. | There be 表存在；have 表拥有 |
| What 后语序错 | What you like? | What **do you** like? | 需借助助动词 do |

#### T4 名词系统（G07 + G10 + G17，3 考点）

**包含考点**：
- G07：名词所有格（'s / of）修饰法则
- G10：可数名词规则复数变化（-s / -es / -ies）及读音
- G17：food/fruit 可数与不可数名词分类

**核心对比表格一——名词所有格（G07）**：

| 所有格类型 | 规则 | 示例 |
|---|---|---|
| 's 所有格（单数名词） | 单数名词 + 's | Tom's book, Mary's desk, my father's car |
| 's 所有格（复数名词以 s 结尾） | 复数名词 + ' | students' books, teachers' room |
| 's 所有格（复数名词不以 s 结尾） | 复数名词 + 's | children's books, men's room |
| of 所有格 | the + A + of + the + B | the leg of the table, the door of the room |

**核心对比表格二——名词规则复数变化（G10）**：

| 变化规则 | 适用条件 | 变化方式 | 示例 | 读音 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 一般加 -s | 大多数名词 | +s | book→books, cat→cats, pen→pens | /s/ 或 /z/ |
| 加 -es | 以 s, x, sh, ch 结尾 | +es | bus→buses, box→boxes, watch→watches | /ɪz/ |
| 变 y 为 i 加 -es | 辅音字母 + y 结尾 | y→i+es | family→families, baby→babies | /z/ |
| 加 -s（元音 + y） | 元音字母 + y 结尾 | +s | boy→boys, day→days | /z/ |

**核心对比表格三——可数与不可数名词分类（G17）**：

| 名词类型 | 特征 | 可否加 -s/-es | 限定词 | 示例 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 可数名词（单数） | 有单数和复数形式 | 可以 | a/an + 名词 | a book, an apple |
| 可数名词（复数） | 有单数和复数形式 | 可以 | 数词 + 名词复数 | two books, three apples |
| 不可数名词 | 只有单数形式，无复数 | 不可以 | 不加 a/an，不用数词直接修饰 | milk, bread, rice, water |

**常见不可数名词速记**（L6 食物主题）：

| 类别 | 不可数名词 | 可数名词 |
|---|---|---|
| 饮品 | milk, water, juice | — |
| 主食 | bread, rice | — |
| 肉类 | chicken, beef | — |
| 水果 | — | apple, banana, orange, strawberry |
| 蔬菜 | — | tomato, potato, carrot |

**记忆口诀**：
> 所有格 's 加名后，复数已有 s 只加撇；
> of 所有格倒着念，the A of the B 是 B 的 A；
> 复数变化三规则，-s/-es/-ies 要分清；
> s/x/sh/ch 加 es，辅音 y 变 i 加 es；
> 可数有复数可数数，不可数无复数不数数；
> milk bread rice water，都是不可数记心上。

**典型例句**：
1. This is **Tom's** book.（'s 所有格）
2. The **students'** books are on the desk.（复数 s 结尾加 '）
3. **Children's** Day is in June.（复数不以 s 结尾加 's）
4. I have two **books** and three **boxes**.（规则复数 -s / -es）
5. There are five **families** in this building.（y 变 i 加 es）
6. I like **milk** and **bread** for breakfast.（不可数名词不加 -s）
7. She eats two **apples** every day.（可数名词复数）

**高频易错点**：
| 易错点 | 错误示例 | 正确示例 | 辨析 |
|---|---|---|---|
| 所有格撇号位置错 | students's books | students'** books | 复数已以 s 结尾只加 ' |
| y 结尾直接加 s | familys | famili**es** | 辅音+y 变 i 加 es |
| 不可数名词加 s | two milks | two **cups of milk** | 不可数名词不能直接加数词 |
| 可数名词单数裸奔 | I have book. | I have **a** book. | 单数可数名词必须有限定词 |
| of 所有格语序错 | the table of leg | the **leg of the** table | of 所有格 = the 部分 of the 整体 |

#### T5 介词系统（G09 + G12，2 考点）

**包含考点**：
- G09：方位介词 in/on/under（基础方位）
- G12：方位介词扩展 behind, next to, in front of, between...and

**核心对比表格——方位介词全景矩阵**：

| 考点 | 介词/短语 | 含义 | 图示意 | 示例 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **G09 基础** | in | 在……里面 | ▢←● | The pen is **in** the box. |
| | on | 在……上面 | ▢↑● | The book is **on** the desk. |
| | under | 在……下面 | ▢↓● | The cat is **under** the chair. |
| **G12 扩展** | behind | 在……后面 | ▢←●(后方) | The ball is **behind** the door. |
| | next to | 在……旁边 | ▢●(并排) | The sofa is **next to** the bed. |
| | in front of | 在……前面 | ▢●(前方) | The TV is **in front of** the sofa. |
| | between...and | 在……和……之间 | A●B | The desk is **between** the bed **and** the door. |

**方位介词搭配 There be 句型综合**：

| 句型 | 示例 |
|---|---|
| There is + 单数名词 + 方位介词短语 | There is a clock **on the wall**. |
| There are + 复数名词 + 方位介词短语 | There are two books **under the desk**. |
| Where is + 单数名词? | Where is my schoolbag? — It's **next to the chair**. |
| Where are + 复数名词? | Where are my keys? — They're **in the box**. |

**记忆口诀**：
> in 里面 on 上面，under 下面记心间；
> behind 后面 next to 旁，in front of 在前方；
> between A and B 夹中间，方位介词要记全；
> There be 句型配方位，is 单 are 复别弄乱。

**典型例句**：
1. The apple is **in** the box. The book is **on** the desk. The cat is **under** the table.（基础方位）
2. The sofa is **behind** the door. The TV is **next to** the window.（扩展方位）
3. There is a chair **in front of** the desk.（in front of）
4. The clock is **between** the picture **and** the lamp.（between...and）
5. Where are my pencils? — They are **in the pencil box**.（Where + 方位介词回答）

**高频易错点**：
| 易错点 | 错误示例 | 正确示例 | 辨析 |
|---|---|---|---|
| in/on 混淆 | The book is in the desk. | The book is **on** the desk. | 桌面上用 on |
| 方位介词后缺名词 | The pen is on. | The pen is on **the desk**. | 介词后必须接名词 |
| between 后缺 and | between A B | between A **and** B | between...and 固定搭配 |
| in front of 多加词 | in the front of the car | in front of the car | in front of 外部前方；in the front of 内部前部（扩展，暂不教） |
| There be 方位语序错 | On the desk there is a book. | There is a book **on the desk**. | 方位介词短语放句末 |

#### T6 形容词系统（G11，1 考点）

**包含考点**：
- G11：形容词作定语（a tidy room）与作表语（The room is tidy）

**核心对比表格——形容词两种用法**：

| 用法 | 位置 | 结构 | 功能 | 示例 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **作定语** | 名词前 | a/an + 形容词 + 名词 | 修饰名词，说明名词特征 | a **tidy** room, a **big** desk, a **happy** family |
| **作表语** | be 动词后 | 主语 + be + 形容词 | 作表语，说明主语状态 | The room **is tidy**. The desk **is big**. The family **is happy**. |

**同一形容词双位置对比**：

| 形容词 | 作定语 | 作表语 | 含义不变 |
|---|---|---|---|
| tidy | a tidy room | The room is tidy. | 整洁的 |
| big | a big desk | The desk is big. | 大的 |
| interesting | an interesting book | The book is interesting. | 有趣的 |
| relaxing | a relaxing sport | The sport is relaxing. | 令人放松的 |
| healthy | a healthy breakfast | The breakfast is healthy. | 健康的 |

> **防越级提醒**：G11 仅限形容词原级，严禁出现比较级（-er/more）和最高级（-est/most）。✅

**记忆口诀**：
> 定语名词前，表语 be 后面；
> 同形两位置，含义不改变；
> a tidy room 是定语，The room is tidy 是表语；
> 形容词原级就足够，比较级最高级以后学。

**典型例句**：
1. She has a **tidy** room.（作定语）→ Her room **is tidy**.（作表语）
2. I want a **big** desk.（作定语）→ The desk **is big**.（作表语）
3. Basketball is an **interesting** sport.（作定语）→ The sport **is interesting**.（作表语）
4. We eat a **healthy** breakfast.（作定语）→ The breakfast **is healthy**.（作表语）
5. He is a **happy** boy.（作定语）→ The boy **is happy**.（作表语）

**高频易错点**：
| 易错点 | 错误示例 | 正确示例 | 辨析 |
|---|---|---|---|
| 定语位置错 | a room tidy | a **tidy** room | 形容词定语在名词前 |
| 表语位置错 | The tidy is room. | The room **is tidy**. | 表语在 be 动词后 |
| 比较级越级 | a bigger room | a **big** room | 本阶段仅原级，不用比较级 |
| 形容词加 -ly | a tidily room | a **tidy** room | 形容词不加 -ly（-ly 是副词后缀） |
| be 动词与形容词搭配错 | The room is tidy room. | The room **is tidy**. | 表语用法不重复名词 |

#### T7 动词与句型系统（G13 + G15 + G16 + G18，4 考点）

**包含考点**：
- G13：Do / Don't 祈使句基础结构
- G15：实义动词 like + 名词复数 / like to do 基础
- G16：一般现在时第一/二人称/复数主语肯定句与 don't 否定句
- G18：want to do sth. 结构

**核心对比表格——四种动词句型结构**：

| 考点 | 句型 | 肯定结构 | 否定结构 | 示例（肯定） | 示例（否定） |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **G13 祈使句** | 命令/请求 | 动词原形开头 | Don't + 动词原形 | Open the door. Sit down. | Don't run. Don't be late. |
| **G15 like 搭配** | 表达喜好 | like + 名词复数 / like to do | don't like + 名词 / don't like to do | I like apples. I like to play soccer. | I don't like milk. I don't like to run. |
| **G16 一般现在时（非三单）** | 日常习惯/事实 | I/You/We/They + 动词原形 | I/You/We/They + don't + 动词原形 | I play basketball. They watch TV. | I don't play soccer. They don't watch TV. |
| **G18 want to do** | 表达意愿 | 主语 + want to + 动词原形 | 主语 + don't want to + 动词原形 | I want to go home. We want to eat. | I don't want to go. |

**like 与 want 搭配对比（G15 vs G18）**：

| 动词 | 搭配结构 | 示例 | 语义 |
|---|---|---|---|
| like + 名词（复数） | like + N(plural) | I like apples. | 喜欢某物 |
| like to do | like to + V | I like to read books. | 喜欢做某事 |
| want to do | want to + V | I want to read a book. | 想要做某事 |
| want + 名词 | want + N | I want an apple. | 想要某物 |

> **螺旋链标注**：G15(like to do) → G18(want to do) → [G34(would like to do) 后续 L13]，L7 展示前两阶。

**记忆口诀**：
> 祈使句，动词原形开头干，Don't 否定跟着办；
> like 接复数或 to do，两种搭配都能用；
> 一般现在时非三单，动词原形就足够，否定 don't 加原形；
> want 后面接 to do，想要做某事记心头；
> like 是喜欢，want 是想要，to do 不定式两处用。

**典型例句**：
1. **Open** your books. **Don't** close the door.（祈使句肯定/否定）
2. I **like** basketball. I **like to play** basketball.（like + 名词 / like to do）
3. They **play** soccer every day. They **don't play** tennis.（一般现在时肯定/否定）
4. I **want to eat** an apple. She **wants to go** home.（want to do，注意 she 用 wants 但属三单扩展）
5. We **don't want to** be late.（want to do 否定）

**高频易错点**：
| 易错点 | 错误示例 | 正确示例 | 辨析 |
|---|---|---|---|
| 祈使句加主语 | You open the door.（作为祈使句） | **Open** the door. | 祈使句省略主语 You |
| 祈使句否定用 not | Not open the door. | **Don't** open the door. | 祈使句否定用 Don't |
| like 后接动词原形 | I like play soccer. | I like **to play** soccer. / I like **playing** soccer. | like + to do 或 like + doing |
| 一般现在时加 to | I to play soccer. | I **play** soccer. | 一般现在时直接用动词原形 |
| 否定句用 don't + to | I don't want to go → I don't want go. | I don't want **to go**. | want to do 中 to 不可省 |
| want 后接动词原形无 to | I want go home. | I want **to go** home. | want to do 固定搭配 |

#### T8 数词系统（G08，1 考点）

**包含考点**：
- G08：基数词 1-100 拼写与数词修饰名词

**核心对比表格——基数词构成规则**：

| 数字范围 | 构成规则 | 示例 | 注意事项 |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1–12 | 独立单词，需逐个记忆 | one, two, three, four, five, six, seven, eight, nine, ten, eleven, twelve | twelve 拼写特殊（不是 twelv） |
| 13–19 | 基数词 + teen | thirteen, fourteen, fifteen, sixteen, seventeen, eighteen, nineteen | fifteen/f eighteen 拼写变化 |
| 20–90（整十） | 基数词 + ty | twenty, thirty, forty, fifty, sixty, seventy, eighty, ninety | forty 拼写特殊（不是 fourty） |
| 21–99（非整十） | 整十 + 连字符 + 个位 | twenty-one, thirty-five, forty-eight, ninety-nine | 必须用连字符"-"连接 |
| 100 | 固定词 | one hundred / a hundred | hundred 前用 one 或 a |

**基数词修饰名词对比**：

| 结构 | 示例 | 说明 |
|---|---|---|
| 数词 + 可数名词复数 | two books, three apples, five students | 数词 ≥ 2 时名词用复数 |
| 数词 + 不可数名词 | one milk（错误） | 不可数名词不能直接用数词修饰 |
| a/an + 单数可数名词 | a book, an apple | 数词 1 用 a/an |

**特殊拼写速记**：

| 易拼错词 | 正确拼写 | 常见错误 | 记忆提示 |
|---|---|---|---|
| 12 | twelve | twelth | ve 结尾 |
| 15 | fifteen | fiveteen | five → fif + teen |
| 18 | eighteen | eightteen | 只有一个 t |
| 40 | forty | fourty | 没有 u |
| 80 | eighty | eightty | 只有一个 t |

**记忆口诀**：
> 1 到 12 各自记， thirteen 到 19 加 teen；
> 整二十到九十加 ty， fifteen 和 forty 要注意；
> 几十几用连字符连， twenty-one 是范例；
> 数词修饰名词时，两个以上用复数。

**典型例句**：
1. I am **thirteen** years old.（年龄表达）
2. There are **twenty-five** students in our class.（数量 + 连字符）
3. My phone number is **one-three-eight-five-five-two-zero**.（电话号码逐位读）
4. She has **two** apples and **one** banana.（数词修饰可数名词）
5. **Forty** and **sixty** is **one hundred**.（整十运算）

**高频易错点**：
| 易错点 | 错误示例 | 正确示例 | 辨析 |
|---|---|---|---|
| 拼写错误 | fourty | **forty** | forty 没有 u |
| 拼写错误 | fiveteen | **fifteen** | five → fif + teen |
| 缺连字符 | twenty one | twenty**-**one | 21–99 必须用连字符 |
| 数词后名词不变 | two book | two book**s** | 数词 ≥ 2 名词用复数 |
| hundred 加 s | two hundreds | two **hundred** | 前有数词时 hundred 不加 s |
