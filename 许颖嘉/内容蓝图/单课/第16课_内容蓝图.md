### 2.2 各主题详细对比表

#### T1 代词系统（扩展）—— G01, G02, G04, G33, G42

**包含考点**：
- G01 人称代词主格与宾格（I/me, he/him, she/her）
- G02 形容词性与名词性物主代词（my/mine, your/yours）
- G04 指示代词（this/that/these/those）单复数及问答
- G33 不定代词 some（肯定句/邀请）vs. any（否定句/疑问句）
- G42 非人称代词 It 指代天气、时间、距离

**核心对比表格**：

| 代词类型 | 功能 | 单数 | 复数 | 用法示例 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **人称代词主格** | 做主语 | I, you, he, she, it | we, you, they | **He** is my friend. |
| **人称代词宾格** | 做宾语（动宾/介宾） | me, you, him, her, it | us, you, them | Please help **me**. |
| **形容词性物主代词** | 修饰名词（后面必须加名词） | my, your, his, her, its | our, your, their | This is **my** book. |
| **名词性物主代词** | 独立使用（后面不加名词） | mine, yours, his, hers, its | ours, yours, theirs | This book is **mine**. |
| **指示代词** | 指代近处/远处 | this（近）/ that（远） | these（近）/ those（远） | **This** is a pen. **Those** are books. |
| **不定代词 some** | 肯定句/邀请/请求 | some | — | I have **some** apples. Would you like **some** tea? |
| **不定代词 any** | 否定句/疑问句 | any | — | I don't have **any** money. Do you have **any** questions? |
| **非人称 It** | 指代天气/时间/距离 | it | — | **It** is sunny today. **It** is 8 o'clock. **It** is 2 kilometers. |

**记忆口诀**：
> 主格做主语，宾格做宾语；my/your/his/her 后加名词，mine/yours/his/hers 独立行；
> this/these 指近处，that/those 指远处；some 肯定 any 疑否；
> It 不只是"它"，天气时间距离都用它。

**典型例句**：
1. ___（I/my/me）mother is a teacher. → **My** mother is a teacher.（形容词性物主代词修饰名词）
2. This pen is ___（your/yours）. → This pen is **yours**.（名词性物主代词独立使用）
3. Would you like ___（some/any）coffee? → Would you like **some** coffee?（邀请用 some）
4. ___ is raining now. → **It** is raining now.（非人称 It 指天气）
5. Are ___（this/these）your books? → Are **these** your books?（复数用 these）

**高频易错点**：
- ❌ This is **mine** book. → ✅ This is **my** book.（形容词性物主代词后必须加名词）
- ❌ I don't have **some** money. → ✅ I don't have **any** money.（否定句用 any）
- ❌ **It** is 8 o'clock. 误译为"它8点了" → ✅ It 指时间，"现在8点了"
- ❌ Would you like **any** tea? → ✅ Would you like **some** tea?（邀请/请求用 some 表礼貌）

#### T2 be 动词系统（扩展）—— G03, G05, G19

**包含考点**：
- G03 be 动词（am/is/are）与人称代词主格搭配口诀
- G05 be 动词否定句与一般疑问句提前法则
- G19 一般过去时 be 动词（was/were）肯定/否定与主语匹配

**核心对比表格**：

| 时态 | I | He/She/It | 单数名词 | We/You/They | 复数名词 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **现在时 be** | am | is | is | are | are |
| **过去时 be** | was | was | was | were | were |

| 句型转换 | 肯定句 | 否定句 | 一般疑问句 |
|---|---|---|---|
| **现在时** | She **is** a student. | She **is not (isn't)** a student. | **Is** she a student? |
| **过去时** | She **was** a student. | She **was not (wasn't)** a student. | **Was** she a student? |
| **现在时复数** | They **are** happy. | They **are not (aren't)** happy. | **Are** they happy? |
| **过去时复数** | They **were** happy. | They **were not (weren't)** happy. | **Were** they happy? |

**记忆口诀**：
> 我用 am 你用 are，is 连着他她它；单数 is 复数 are；
> 过去时也不难：单数 was 复数 were；
> 否定 not 跟在后，疑问 be 动词往前走。

**典型例句**：
1. I ___（am/is/are）twelve years old. → I **am** twelve years old.
2. He ___（was/were）at home yesterday. → He **was** at home yesterday.（he 单数用 was）
3. They ___（was/were）happy last week. → They **were** happy last week.（they 复数用 were）
4. ___（Is/Was）she your teacher? — Yes, she ___. → **Was** she your teacher? — Yes, she **was**.（有 yesterday，用过去时）
5. We ___（aren't/weren't）at school yesterday. → We **weren't** at school yesterday.

**高频易错点**：
- ❌ I **was** a student now. → ✅ I **am** a student now.（now 用现在时，yesterday 才用过去时）
- ❌ They **was** late. → ✅ They **were** late.（复数用 were）
- ❌ **Does** she a teacher? → ✅ **Is** she a teacher?（be 动词疑问直接提前，不用 do/does）
- ❌ She **don't** be happy. → ✅ She **isn't** happy.（be 动词否定用 isn't/aren't，不用 don't/doesn't）

#### T3 疑问句系统（扩展）—— G06, G09, G14, G22, G23, G35

**包含考点**：
- G06 Who 引导的特殊疑问句
- G09 Where 引导疑问句 + There is/are 存在句
- G14 What 引导的特殊疑问句
- G22 特殊疑问句"六宝"（What/Where/When/Why/Who/How）结构
- G23 选择疑问句（or 结构）回答禁用 Yes/No
- G35 how much（问不可数/价格）vs. how many（问可数复数）

**核心对比表格**：

| 疑问词 | 提问对象 | 句型结构 | 典型问句 | 回答方式 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **What** | 事物/动作 | What + is/are + 主语？ | What is this? | It's a book. |
| **Where** | 地点 | Where + is/are + 主语？ | Where is the library? | It's next to the classroom. |
| **When** | 时间 | When + do/does/did + 主语 + 动词？ | When do you get up? | At 6:30. |
| **Why** | 原因 | Why + do/does/did + 主语 + 动词？ | Why do you like math? | Because it's interesting. |
| **Who** | 人物 | Who + is/are + 主语？ / Who + 动词？ | Who is that boy? | He is my brother. |
| **How** | 方式/程度 | How + be + 主语？ / How + do/does + 主语 + 动词？ | How are you? | I'm fine. |

| 疑问句类型 | 结构 | 回答规则 | 例句 |
|---|---|---|---|
| **特殊疑问句** | 疑问词 + 一般疑问句语序 | 根据疑问词具体回答 | What do you like? — I like basketball. |
| **一般疑问句** | be/Do/Does/Did + 主语 + ...? | Yes/No 回答 | Do you like basketball? — Yes, I do. |
| **选择疑问句** | 一般疑问句 + A **or** B? | **禁用 Yes/No**，必须选择 | Is your friend tall **or** short? — He is tall. |

| 提问对象 | 疑问词组 | 接可数/不可数 | 例句 |
|---|---|---|---|
| **数量（可数）** | how many | 可数名词复数 | How many **apples** do you have? |
| **数量（不可数）** | how much | 不可数名词 | How much **water** do you need? |
| **价格** | how much | — | How much **is** this pen? — It's 5 yuan. |

**记忆口诀**：
> 疑问六宝 What/Where/When/Why/Who/How；
> What 问事物 Where 问地点 When 问时间；
> Why 问原因用 because 回答 Who 问人物 How 问方式；
> 选择疑问 or 连接，回答不用 Yes 或 No；
> how many 数可数，how much 不可数还问价。

**典型例句**：
1. ___ is your English teacher? — Mr. Wang. → **Who** is your English teacher?
2. ___ is the weather today? — It's sunny. → **How** is the weather today?
3. Do you like tea ___ coffee? — I like coffee. → Do you like tea **or** coffee?（选择疑问，不用 Yes/No）
4. ___ apples do you have? — Five. → **How many** apples do you have?（可数复数）
5. ___ is this book? — 15 yuan. → **How much** is this book?（问价格）

**高频易错点**：
- ❌ Is your brother tall or short? — **Yes, he is.** → ✅ — **He is tall.**（选择疑问句禁用 Yes/No）
- ❌ How **many** water do you want? → ✅ How **much** water do you want?（water 不可数）
- ❌ How much **apples** do you have? → ✅ How **many** apples do you have?（apple 可数复数）
- ❌ **What** is your name? — **My name is Tom.** 漏掉 What 误用 Who → ✅ What 问姓名

#### T4 名词系统（扩展）—— G07, G10, G17, G29, G32

**包含考点**：
- G07 名词所有格（'s / of）修饰法则
- G10 可数名词规则复数变化（-s / -es / -ies）及读音
- G17 food/fruit 可数与不可数名词分类
- G29 名词复数不规则变化（man→men, child→children, foot→feet, tooth→teeth, sheep→sheep）
- G32 可数与不可数名词区分及量词表达（a cup of, two bowls of）

**核心对比表格**：

| 复数变化类型 | 规则 | 示例 | 读音 |
|---|---|---|---|
| **一般加 -s** | 大多数名词 | book→books, pen→pens | /s/ 清辅音后, /z/ 浊辅音后 |
| **加 -es** | s/x/sh/ch 结尾 | bus→buses, box→boxes, watch→watches | /ɪz/ |
| **变 y 为 i 加 -es** | 辅音字母+y 结尾 | family→families, strawberry→strawberries | /z/ |
| **不规则变化** | 无规则，需记忆 | man→men, child→children, foot→feet, tooth→teeth, sheep→sheep | — |

| 名词类型 | 判断标准 | 量词搭配 | 例句 |
|---|---|---|---|
| **可数名词** | 可用数字计数，有复数形式 | 直接加数词 | two apples, three books |
| **不可数名词** | 不能直接计数，无复数形式 | 需用量词 | a cup of water, two bowls of rice |

| 所有格类型 | 构成 | 适用对象 | 示例 |
|---|---|---|---|
| **'s 所有格** | 名词 + 's | 有生命的人/动物 | Tom's book, my sister's room |
| **of 所有格** | A of B | 无生命的事物 | the door of the room, the capital of China |

**记忆口诀**：
> 规则复数四种加：一般加 s，sxshch 加 es，辅音 y 变 ies；
> 不规则要死记：man→men, child→children, foot→feet, tooth→teeth, sheep 不变；
> 可数直接数，不可数加量词：a cup of, a bowl of, a piece of；
> 有生命加 's，无生命用 of。

**典型例句**：
1. There are three ___（child/children）in the park. → three **children**（不规则复数）
2. I would like two ___（bowl/bowls）of rice. → two **bowls** of **rice**（bowl 可数加 s，rice 不可数用量词）
3. This is ___（Tom's/Tom）schoolbag. → This is **Tom's** schoolbag（有生命 's 所有格）
4. The ___（room's/room）door is open. → The door **of the room** is open（无生命用 of）
5. How many ___（strawberry/strawberries）do you want? → How many **strawberries**（辅音 y 变 ies）

**高频易错点**：
- ❌ three **childs** → ✅ three **children**（child 不规则复数）
- ❌ two **sheeps** → ✅ two **sheep**（sheep 单复数同形）
- ❌ a **water** → ✅ a **cup/glass of water**（water 不可数，需用量词）
- ❌ **Tom book** → ✅ **Tom's book**（所有格必须加 's）
- ❌ **strawberrys** → ✅ **strawberries**（辅音+y 变 ies，不是直接加 s）

#### T5 介词系统（扩展）—— G09, G12, G24, G30

**包含考点**：
- G09 Where 疑问句 + 方位介词 in/on/under + There is/are 存在句
- G12 方位介词短语（behind, next to, in front of, between...and）
- G24 交通与方向介词（by bus, on foot, turn left/right）

**核心对比表格**：

| 介词类别 | 介词 | 含义 | 示例 |
|---|---|---|---|
| **基础方位** | in | 在...里面 | The book is **in** the box. |
| | on | 在...上面 | The cup is **on** the table. |
| | under | 在...下面 | The cat is **under** the chair. |
| **扩展方位** | behind | 在...后面 | The park is **behind** the school. |
| | next to | 在...旁边 | The library is **next to** the classroom. |
| | in front of | 在...前面 | There is a tree **in front of** the house. |
| | between...and | 在...之间 | The bank is **between** the school **and** the hospital. |

| | on foot | 步行 | I go to school **on foot**. |
| | turn left/right | 左转/右转 | **Turn left** at the corner. |

| | at + 具体时刻 | 在...（时刻） | **at** 8 o'clock |

**记忆口诀**：
> 方位介词记位置：in 里面 on 上面 under 下面 behind 后面；
> next to 旁边 in front of 前面 between...and 夹中间；
> 交通方式 by 开头：by bus by car by bike，步行要用 on foot；
> 时间介词三分法：in 大 on 小 at 精确。

**典型例句**：
1. The pen is ___（in/on）the desk. → The pen is **on** the desk.（在桌面上）
2. I go to school ___（by/by the）bus. → I go to school **by bus**.（by + 交通工具不加冠词）
3. The library is ___（between/next）the school and the hospital. → The library is **between** the school **and** the hospital.
4. We have a meeting ___（in/on）Monday. → We have a meeting **on** Monday.（星期用 on）
5. Turn ___（left/the left）at the second crossing. → Turn **left** at the second crossing.

**高频易错点**：
- ❌ I go to school **by a bus**. → ✅ I go to school **by bus**.（by + 交通工具不加冠词/物主代词）
- ❌ We meet **in** Sunday. → ✅ We meet **on** Sunday.（星期用 on 不用 in）
- ❌ **By** foot → ✅ **On** foot（步行是 on foot 不是 by foot）
- ❌ The book is **between** the desk. → ✅ The book is **between** the desk **and** the chair.（between...and 必须配对）

#### T6 形容词系统（扩展）—— G11, G39

**包含考点**：
- G11 形容词作定语（a tidy room）与作表语（The room is tidy）
- G39 描述性形容词修饰名词与作表语用法

> **防越级约束**：G11/G39 严禁比较级（-er/more）和最高级（-est/most），仅限原级。

**核心对比表格**：

| 形容词位置 | 功能 | 结构 | 示例 |
|---|---|---|---|
| **作定语** | 修饰名词，放在名词前 | 冠词 + 形容词 + 名词 | a **tidy** room, a **tall** boy, **short** hair |
| **作表语** | 描述主语状态，放在 be 动词后 | 主语 + be + 形容词 | The room is **tidy**. The boy is **tall**. |

| 描述性形容词类别 | 示例 | 描述对象 |
|---|---|---|
| **外貌形容词** | tall, short, thin, heavy, medium | 身高体型 |
| **头发形容词** | straight, curly, long, short, black, blonde | 头发特征 |
| **性格形容词** | friendly, kind, funny, smart, shy | 性格特征 |
| **感受形容词** | interesting, boring, relaxing, exciting | 感受评价 |

**记忆口诀**：
> 形容词两位置：名词前面做定语，be 动后面做表语；
> a tidy room 是定语，The room is tidy 是表语；
> 描述外貌和性格，只用原级不升级。

**典型例句**：
1. She has ___（straight/straightly）hair. → She has **straight** hair.（形容词作定语修饰名词）
2. The movie is ___（interest/interesting）. → The movie is **interesting**.（形容词作表语）
3. He is a ___（friend/friendly）boy. → He is a **friendly** boy.（形容词作定语）
4. My room is very ___（tidy/tidily）. → My room is very **tidy**.（形容词作表语）

**高频易错点**：
- ❌ She is a **beautifully** girl. → ✅ She is a **beautiful** girl.（形容词而非副词修饰名词）
- ❌ The room is **very tidily**. → ✅ The room is **very tidy**.（be 动词后用形容词不用副词）
- ❌ He is **taller** than me. → ⚠️ 本课范围严禁比较级，仅限原级：He is **tall**.
- ❌ The movie is **interest**. → ✅ The movie is **interesting**.（形容词形式不能直接用动词原形）

#### T7 动词与句型（扩展）—— G13, G15, G16, G18, G34, G37

**包含考点**：
- G13 Do / Don't 祈使句基础结构
- G15 like + 名词复数 / like to do 基础
- G16 一般现在时第一/二人称/复数主语肯定句与 don't 否定句
- G18 want to do sth. 结构
- G34 would like to do sth. 与 would like sth.
- G37 外貌描述 be 动词（身高/体型）vs. have/has（五官/头发）

**核心对比表格**：

| 动词搭配 | 结构 | 语气/功能 | 示例 |
|---|---|---|---|
| **like + 名词** | like + n. | 表达喜好 | I like **apples**. |
| **like to do** | like + to + V | 喜欢做某事 | I like **to read** books. |
| **want to do** | want + to + V | 想要做某事 | I want **to go** home. |
| **would like to do** | would like + to + V | 礼貌地想要做某事 | I would like **to order** a pizza. |
| **would like sth.** | would like + n. | 礼貌地想要某物 | I would like **a cup of tea**. |

> **S1 螺旋链**：like to do → want to do → would like to do（三阶完成）

| 外貌描述 | be 动词描述 | have/has 描述 |
|---|---|---|
| **身高体型** | is tall / is short / is thin / is heavy / is of medium height | — |
| **五官/头发** | — | has short hair / has big eyes / has a small nose |
| **综合描述** | He **is** tall and thin. | He **has** short black hair. |

| 祈使句 | 肯定 | 否定 |
|---|---|---|
| **结构** | 动词原形开头 | Don't + 动词原形 |
| **示例** | **Open** the door. **Please** sit down. | **Don't** be late. **Don't** run in the hallway. |

**记忆口诀**：
> like 喜欢 want 想，would like 最礼貌；
> to do 搭配三兄弟：like to do, want to do, would like to do；
> 外貌描述分两家：be 动词管高矮胖瘦，have/has 管头发五官；
> 祈使句最简单：动词原形开头，否定 Don't 加前面。

**典型例句**：
1. I ___（like/like to）basketball. → I **like** basketball.（like + 名词）
2. She would like ___（drink/to drink）some juice. → She would like **to drink** some juice.（would like to do）
3. He ___（is/has）tall and ___（is/has）short hair. → He **is** tall and **has** short hair.（身高用 is，头发用 has）
4. ___（Don't/Not）run in the hallway. → **Don't** run in the hallway.（否定祈使句）
5. I want ___（go/to go）to the library. → I want **to go** to the library.（want to do）

**高频易错点**：
- ❌ I **want go** home. → ✅ I want **to go** home.（want 后必须加 to do）
- ❌ He **is** short hair. → ✅ He **has** short hair.（头发用 have/has 不用 be）
- ❌ He **has** tall. → ✅ He **is** tall.（身高用 be 不用 have/has）
- ❌ **Not** open the door. → ✅ **Don't** open the door.（否定祈使句用 Don't）
- ❌ I **would like drinking** tea. → ✅ I would like **to drink** tea.（would like + to do，不是 doing）

#### T8 数词系统（扩展）—— G08, G36

**包含考点**：
- G08 基数词 1-100 拼写与数词修饰名词
- G36 基数词与货币/价格表达（yuan/dollar/total/pay/cost/spend）

**核心对比表格**：

| 数词范围 | 拼写规则 | 示例 |
|---|---|---|
| **1-12** | 独立单词 | one, two, three... twelve |
| **13-19** | -teen 结尾 | thirteen, fourteen... nineteen |
| **20-90 整十** | -ty 结尾 | twenty, thirty... ninety |
| **21-99** | 十位 + 连字符 + 个位 | twenty-one, thirty-five, ninety-nine |

| 货币表达 | 句型 | 示例 |
|---|---|---|
| **问价格** | How much is/are...? | How much **is** this pen? — It's 5 yuan. |
| **货币单位** | yuan（人民币）, dollar（美元） | 5 yuan, 10 dollars |
| **total（总计）** | The total is... | The total **is** 25 yuan. |
| **pay（支付）** | pay for... | I **pay** 10 yuan **for** the book. |
| **cost（花费）** | sth. costs... | The book **costs** 15 yuan. |
| **spend（花费）** | sb. spend... on sth. | I **spend** 20 yuan **on** food. |

**记忆口诀**：
> 基数词要会拼：1-12 独立记，13-19 加 teen，整十加 ty 不忘 e；
> 21 写作 twenty-one，连字符不能忘；
> 问价格用 how much，回答用 yuan 或 dollar；
> pay 是人付钱，cost 是物花钱，spend 是人花钱买东西。

**典型例句**：
1. There are ___（twenty-two/twenty two）students in our class. → There are **twenty-two** students.（连字符）
2. How much ___（is/are）these apples? — They ___（is/are）15 yuan. → How much **are** these apples? — They **are** 15 yuan.（复数用 are）
3. The book ___（costs/pays）20 yuan. → The book **costs** 20 yuan.（物作主语用 cost）
4. I ___（spend/pay）30 yuan on this shirt. → I **spend** 30 yuan on this shirt.（人作主语用 spend...on）
5. The ___（total/all）is 50 yuan. → The **total** is 50 yuan.

**高频易错点**：
- ❌ **twenty two** → ✅ **twenty-two**（21-99 之间必须加连字符）
- ❌ How much **is** these apples? → ✅ How much **are** these apples?（复数名词用 are）
- ❌ I **cost** 20 yuan on the book. → ✅ I **spend** 20 yuan on the book.（人作主语用 spend 不用 cost）
- ❌ The book **pays** 15 yuan. → ✅ The book **costs** 15 yuan.（物作主语用 cost 不用 pay）

#### T9 时态系统（新增）—— G16, G25, G26, G19, G20, G21

**包含考点**：
- G16 一般现在时第一/二人称/复数主语肯定句与 don't 否定句（非三单）
- G25 一般现在时三单 -s/-es 变化规则
- G26 三单否定句与疑问句（does/doesn't & 动词还原）
- G19 一般过去时 be 动词（was/were）
- G20 一般过去时实义动词（did/didn't & 规则 -ed 变化）
- G21 过去时间状语（yesterday, ago, last week/year）

> **本主题为 L16 新增核心主题**，做跨主题综合时态对比。G16/G19 在 T2/T7 中仅做功能引用，此处做系统对比。

**核心对比表格 1：一般现在时 vs 一般过去时**

| 时态 | 时间标志词 | be 动词 | 实义动词（非三单） | 实义动词（三单） | 否定句 | 疑问句 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **一般现在时（非三单）** | always, usually, every day | am/is/are | 动词原形 | — | don't + V | Do + 主语 + V? |
| **一般现在时（三单）** | always, usually, every day | is | — | 动词加 -s/-es | doesn't + V(还原) | Does + 主语 + V(还原)? |
| **一般过去时（be）** | yesterday, ago, last... | was/were | — | — | wasn't/weren't | Was/Were + 主语? |
| **一般过去时（实义）** | yesterday, ago, last... | — | did（即动词过去式） | did | didn't + V(还原) | Did + 主语 + V(还原)? |

**核心对比表格 2：三单 -s/-es 变化规则**

| 变化类型 | 规则 | 示例 |
|---|---|---|
| **一般加 -s** | 大多数动词 | like→likes, play→plays, get→gets |
| **加 -es** | s/x/sh/ch/o 结尾 | go→goes, watch→watches, wash→washes |
| **变 y 为 i 加 -es** | 辅音字母+y 结尾 | study→studies, fly→flies |
| **特殊** | have → has | have→has |

**核心对比表格 3：过去时间状语识别**

| 时间状语 | 含义 | 搭配时态 |
|---|---|---|
| yesterday | 昨天 | 一般过去时 |
| ... ago | ...以前 | 一般过去时 |
| last week/month/year | 上周/上个月/去年 | 一般过去时 |
| this morning | 今天早上（已过去） | 一般过去时 |

**记忆口诀**：
> 一般现在时：非三单用原形，三单加 s 或 es；
> 三单否定疑问用 does/doesn't，动词一定要还原；
> 一般过去时：be 用 was/were，实义动词变 -ed；
> 过去否定疑问用 did/didn't，动词同样要还原；
> 看到 yesterday/ago/last 就用过去时。

**典型例句**：
1. She ___（like/likes）math. → She **likes** math.（三单加 s）
2. He ___（don't/doesn't）play basketball. → He **doesn't** play basketball.（三单否定用 doesn't，动词还原）
3. ___（Do/Does）your sister ___（watch/watches）TV every day? → **Does** your sister **watch** TV every day?（三单疑问用 Does，动词还原）
4. I ___（go/went）to the park yesterday. → I **went** to the park yesterday.（有 yesterday 用过去时）
5. They ___（don't/didn't）go to school last Monday. → They **didn't** go to school last Monday.（有 last Monday 用过去时否定，动词还原）

**高频易错点**：
- ❌ He **don't** like math. → ✅ He **doesn't** like math.（三单否定用 doesn't）
- ❌ Does he **likes** math? → ✅ Does he **like** math?（Does 后动词还原）
- ❌ He **goes** to school yesterday. → ✅ He **went** to school yesterday.（有 yesterday 用过去时）
- ❌ Did he **went** to school? → ✅ Did he **go** to school?（Did 后动词还原）
- ❌ He **watchs** TV. → ✅ He **watches** TV.（ch 结尾加 -es 不是 -s）

#### T10 情态与冠词（新增）—— G28, G31, G41

**包含考点**：
- G28 can/can't（能力与许可）及 must/have to（义务与必须）
- G31 冠词 a / an / the 区别与单数可数名词防"裸奔"规则
- G41 Could / Would you please do sth.? 礼貌请求/邀请与动词还原

**核心对比表格 1：情态动词梯队**

| 情态动词 | 功能 | 语气 | 句型 | 示例 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **can** | 能力/许可 | 一般 | can + V(原形) | I **can** swim. You **can** go now. |
| **can't** | 不能/不许 | 一般 | can't + V(原形) | I **can't** speak French. |
| **must** | 必须（主观） | 强 | must + V(原形) | You **must** finish your homework. |
| **have to** | 必须（客观） | 较强 | have/has to + V(原形) | I **have to** wear a uniform. |
| **Could you...?** | 礼貌请求 | 委婉 | Could + 主语 + please + V(原形)? | **Could you** please **help** me? |
| **Would you...?** | 礼貌邀请 | 委婉 | Would + 主语 + please + V(原形)? | **Would you** please **open** the window? |

**核心对比表格 2：冠词 a / an / the**

| 冠词 | 用法 | 判断依据 | 示例 |
|---|---|---|---|
| **a** | 泛指，辅音音素开头 | 看发音不看字母 | a book, a university（/ju:/ 辅音开头）, a pen |
| **an** | 泛指，元音音素开头 | 看发音不看字母 | an apple, an hour（/h/ 不发音）, an egg |
| **the** | 特指 | 双方都知道/上文提过 | **the** sun, **the** boy in red, Pass me **the** book. |
| **零冠词（裸奔）** | ❌ 单数可数名词不能裸奔 | — | ❌ I have **book**. → ✅ I have **a** book. |

**记忆口诀**：
> 情态动词 can 表能力，must 强调主观必须，have to 客观不得不；
> can → Could/Would 越来越礼貌，动词永远用原形；
> a 和 an 看发音：辅音用 a 元音用 an；
> the 是特指双方知，单数可数不裸奔。

**典型例句**：
1. I ___（can/cans）play the piano. → I **can** play the piano.（情态动词后用动词原形，不加 s）
2. You ___（must/must to）wear a uniform at school. → You **must** wear a uniform at school.（must 后直接加原形，无 to）
3. ___（Could/Can）you please ___（helping/help）me? → **Could** you please **help** me?（礼貌请求，动词原形）
4. I have ___（a/an）apple and ___（a/an）orange. → I have **an** apple and **an** orange.（apple/orange 元音开头用 an）
5. He is ___（a/the）boy in red. → He is **the** boy in red.（特指穿红衣服的男孩用 the）

**高频易错点**：
- ❌ He **cans** swim. → ✅ He **can** swim.（情态动词后用原形，且 can 不加 s）
- ❌ **Must** to wear → ✅ **Must** wear（must 后不加 to）
- ❌ Could you please **helping** me? → ✅ Could you please **help** me?（情态动词后用原形）
- ❌ I have **a** apple. → ✅ I have **an** apple.（apple 元音开头用 an）
- ❌ I have **book**. → ✅ I have **a** book.（单数可数名词不能裸奔）
- ❌ a **hour** → ✅ an **hour**（h 不发音，元音开头用 an）

#### T11 连词与句型（新增）—— G38, G40

**包含考点**：
- G38 原因状语 why 提问与 because 回答
- G40 天气表达句型（How's the weather...? / It's sunny/rainy）

**核心对比表格**：

| 因果句型 | 功能 | 结构 | 示例 |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Why...?** | 提问原因 | Why + do/does/did + 主语 + V? | Why do you like English? |
| **Because...** | 回答原因 | Because + 句子 | Because it's interesting. |

| 天气句型 | 结构 | 示例 |
|---|---|---|
| **问天气** | How's the weather + 地点/时间? | How's the weather **in Beijing**? |
| **问天气（变体）** | What's the weather like + 地点/时间? | What's the weather like **today**? |
| **答天气** | It's + 天气形容词. | It's **sunny**. / It's **rainy**. / It's **cloudy**. |

| 天气形容词 | 名词→形容词 | 含义 |
|---|---|---|
| sunny | sun → sunny | 晴天 |
| rainy | rain → rainy | 雨天 |
| cloudy | cloud → cloudy | 多云 |
| windy | wind → windy | 刮风 |
| snowy | snow → snowy | 下雪 |

**记忆口诀**：
> why 提问 because 答，因果配对不分家；
> 问天气 How's the weather，回答 It's 加形容词；
> sun→sunny, rain→rainy, cloud→cloudy，名词变 y 记心间。

**典型例句**：
1. ___ do you like math? — ___ it's interesting. → **Why** do you like math? — **Because** it's interesting.
2. How's the weather ___（in/on）Shanghai? — It's ___（sun/sunny）. → How's the weather **in** Shanghai? — It's **sunny**.
3. ___（Why/Because）are you late? — Because I missed the bus. → **Why** are you late? — Because I missed the bus.
4. It's ___（rain/rainy）today. We can't go out. → It's **rainy** today.

**高频易错点**：
- ❌ **Why** do you like math? — **So** it's interesting. → ✅ — **Because** it's interesting.（why 用 because 回答，不用 so）
- ❌ How's the weather? — It's **sun**. → ✅ It's **sunny**.（需用形容词形式）
- ❌ **What** do you like math? → ✅ **Why** do you like math?（问原因用 why 不用 what）
- ❌ How's the weather **on** Beijing? → ✅ How's the weather **in** Beijing?（城市用 in）

#### T12 交通与规则介词（新增）—— G24, G30

**包含考点**：
- G24 交通与方向介词（by bus, on foot, turn left/right）
- G30 规则与时间介词（in the hallway, on time, in quiet）

> **与 T5 的区别**：T5 侧重方位介词（in/on/under/behind/next to），T12 侧重交通方式介词和时间介词的独立对比。

**核心对比表格 1：交通方式介词**

| 表达方式 | 结构 | 注意事项 | 示例 |
|---|---|---|---|
| **by + 交通工具** | by bus/car/bike/train/subway | 不加 a/the/my | I go to school **by bus**. |
| **on foot** | on foot | 固定搭配，不用 by foot | I go to school **on foot**. |
| **take + a/the + 交通工具** | take a bus/taxi | 需要加冠词 | I **take a bus** to school. |
| **方向表达** | turn left/right | 不加 the | **Turn left** at the second crossing. |
| **问路句型** | How can I get to...? | — | How can I get to the library? |

**核心对比表格 2：时间介词 in/on/at**

| 介词 | 适用范围 | 记忆口诀 | 示例 |
|---|---|---|---|
| **in** | 月、年、季节、上午/下午/晚上 | 大时间用 in | in summer, in 2024, in the morning |
| **on** | 星期、日期、具体某天 | 具体日期用 on | on Monday, on July 1st, on weekends |
| **at** | 具体时刻、中午/午夜 | 精确时刻用 at | at 8:00, at noon, at night |

**核心对比表格 3：规则场景介词搭配**

| 介词搭配 | 含义 | 示例 |
|---|---|---|
| **in the hallway** | 在走廊里 | Don't run **in the hallway**. |
| **on time** | 准时 | Be **on time** for class. |
| **in quiet** | 安静地 | Please read **in quiet**. |
| **in the library** | 在图书馆 | We read **in the library**. |

**记忆口诀**：
> 交通方式 by 开头：by bus by car by bike，步行要用 on foot；
> take a bus 需加冠词，by bus 不能加；
> 方向 turn left/right，不加 the 记心间；
> 时间介词三分法：in 大（月年季）on 小（星期日）at 精确（时刻点）。

**典型例句**：
1. I go to school ___（by/by the）bike every day. → I go to school **by bike**.（by + 交通工具不加冠词）
2. She goes to work ___（on/by）foot. → She goes to work **on foot**.（步行用 on foot）
3. Turn ___（left/the left）at the first crossing. → Turn **left** at the first crossing.（不加 the）
4. We have English class ___（in/on）Monday morning. → We have English class **on** Monday morning.（具体某天的上午用 on）
5. Don't eat ___（in/on）the classroom. → Don't eat **in** the classroom.（在教室里用 in）

**高频易错点**：
- ❌ I go to school **by a bus**. → ✅ I go to school **by bus**.（by + 交通工具不加冠词）
- ❌ I go to school **by foot**. → ✅ I go to school **on foot**.（步行固定用 on foot）
- ❌ Turn **the left** at the corner. → ✅ Turn **left** at the corner.（turn left/right 不加 the）
- ❌ We meet **in** Sunday. → ✅ We meet **on** Sunday.（星期用 on）
- ❌ The class starts **in** 8:00. → ✅ The class starts **at** 8:00.（具体时刻用 at）
