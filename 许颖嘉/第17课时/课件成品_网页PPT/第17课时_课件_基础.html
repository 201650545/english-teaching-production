词汇辨析

避免混淆

EXTEND · 时间充足做 (4 min)
词近义 / 形近辨析
hardly vs hard
hardly 意为“几乎不”（否定期）；hard 意为“努力地/硬的”。
once / twice vs times
一次用 once，两次用 twice；三次及以上用 基数词+times。
health vs healthy
health 为名词（good health）；healthy 为形容词（healthy food）。
sometimes vs some times
sometimes 意为“有时”；some times 意为“几次”。

How often 疑问句

CORE · 课堂必做 (5 min)
法考点② · How often 句型与回答
六色卡规则矩阵
疑问词组★ 重点记忆
How often 用来对动作的发生频率进行提问。
基本结构★ 重点记忆
How oftendo/does主语动词原形 ... ?
第一人称问▲ 难点
How often do you exercise regularly?
第三人称问○ 理解即可
How often does he watch television?
答语搭配○ 理解即可
回答可用频度副词或 once/twice/three times a week。
易错辨析★ 重点记忆
✕How long 问时长；How often 问频率！
💬 How often 问答对话 · 频率与次数表达
How often do you exercise?
I sometimes exercise — three times a week.
How often does she read online?
She reads online every day — seven times a week.
How often do they play basketball?
They play basketball twice a week.
