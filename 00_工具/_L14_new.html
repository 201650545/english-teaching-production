📚 L13 语法三考点快闪

G34

would like 结构

would like 表意愿，想要某物直接接；想要做事加 to do，礼貌提出最得体
I'd like a pancake. / She'd like to buy some snacks.
G35

how much vs. how many

how much 问多少，不可数和价格；how many 问几个，可数复数才用它
How much sugar? / How many dumplings?
G36

基数词与货币/价格表达

基数词表数量，dollar 美元 yuan 人民币；price is + 价格，cost/spend 各有法
The pancake is five yuan.
It costs three dollars.
I spend 50 yuan on snacks.

🔑 G37 · 外貌描述 be动词 vs. have/has

be 动词描写特征，tall/short/thin/heavy；
have/has 拥有部位，hair/eyes/face 配上它
句型公式示例
be + 形容词主语 + is/are + tall/short/thin/heavyHe is tall. / She is thin.
be + of medium...主语 + is + of medium height/buildHe is of medium height.
have/has + 名词短语主语 + have/has + hair/eyes/faceShe has long hair. / He has big eyes.
have/has + adj + 名词主语 + have/has + adj + hair/eyesShe has straight black hair.
wear + 名词主语 + wear/wears + glasses/clothesShe wears glasses.

⚡ be vs. have/has 核心区分

be + 形容词：描述整体特征（高矮胖瘦）— He is tall. ✅ / He has tall. ❌
have/has + 名词：描述拥有部位（头发眼睛）— She has long hair. ✅ / She is long hair. ❌
wear + 名词：描述穿戴物品 — He wears glasses. ✅

📋 主语与 have/has 搭配（螺旋复现 G03/G25）

主语动词示例
I/you/we/they/复数名词haveI have short hair. / They have big eyes.
he/she/it/单数名词hasHe has curly hair. / She has a round face.
G03 (L3) be动词基础 → G16 (L6) 一般现在时非三单 → G25 (L10) 三单-s变化 → G37 (L14) be vs. have

🕹️ G37 · 外貌描述器 — 点击切换人物

点击按钮，查看不同人物的外貌描述：

⚠️ 易错点与中考辨析

❌ He is long hair. → ✅ He has long hair.（hair是名词，用has）
❌ She has tall. → ✅ She is tall.（tall是形容词，用is）
❌ He have curly hair. → ✅ He has curly hair.（三单用has，螺旋G25）
❌ She is wear glasses. → ✅ She wears glasses.（wear是实义动词，不用be）
🚫 防越级约束：仅限 be+形容词 / have/has+名词 基础结构。with+名词后置定语仅提及不深入；严禁比较级（taller/the tallest 等留至 L22 G55-G57）

🔑 G38 · why/because 因果状语

why 问原因，because 来回答；
前因后果要分清，because 句子跟在后
句型公式示例
why 疑问句Why + do/does + 主语 + 动词原形 + ...?Why do you like the actor?
why 疑问句(be)Why + is/are + 主语 + ...?Why is she thin?
because 回答Because + 主语 + 谓语 + ...Because he is a good actor.
主句 + because + 从句主句 + because + 从句I like him because he is funny.
Because + 从句, + 主句Because + 从句, + 主句Because he is tall, he can play basketball.

⚡ why/because 配对规则

why 提问原因 → because 回答原因
because 从句在后（常用）：I like him because he is kind.
because 从句在前（强调原因）：Because he is kind, I like him.

⚠️ 中考辨析与易错点

because vs. so：不能同时出现 — ❌ Because he is tall, so I like him. → ✅ Because he is tall, I like him.
why 疑问句需助动词 — ❌ Why you like the actor? → ✅ Why do you like the actor?
because 接句子不接名词 — because + 句子 ✅ / because of + 名词（留至后续课）
G38 (L14) why/because 基础因果 → G60 (L24) because/since/as → G61 (L24) so/so that/in order that

▶

🔑 G39 · 描述性形容词修饰名词与作表语

形容词有两用，修饰名词放前面，作表语放 be 后面；
不修饰动词不不加 very
用法公式示例
形容词作定语adj. + 名词She has long hair. / He is a tall boy.
形容词作表语主语 + be + adj.He is tall. / Her hair is straight.
多个形容词并列数量 + 大小/形状 + 颜色 + 名词She has long black hair. / He has big blue eyes.
look + 形容词主语 + look/looks + adj.She looks beautiful. / He looks thin.

📋 本课形容词分类表

类别形容词描写对象
身高tall, short人的高矮
体型thin, heavy, medium人的胖瘦
发型straight, curly, long, short头发特征
五官big, small, round眼睛/脸型

⚠️ 易错点

❌ She has hair long. → ✅ She has long hair.（形容词放名词前）
❌ black long hair → ✅ long black hair（长短在颜色前）
❌ He is very tall boy. → ✅ He is a very tall boy.（需冠词a）
❌ She is beautifully. → ✅ She is beautiful.（be后接形容词不接副词）
G12 (L5) 形容词作定语基础 → G37 (L14) be vs. have → G39 (L14) 形容词修饰与作表语 → G55 (L22) 比较级与最高级

💡 ow 两种发音记忆法

ow 发 /əʊ/：通常在字母n前面（know, grow, blow）或在词尾（show, yellow）——记忆：n前面发/əʊ/
ow 发 /aʊ/：通常在字母n后面（now, brown, cow, how）或词尾（cow, how）——记忆：n后面发/aʊ/
特殊情况：有些词需单独记，如 blow/slow 发 /əʊ/，而 bow/town 发 /aʊ/

🔑 G37 · be动词 vs. have/has 外貌描述
口诀：be 动词描写特征，tall/short/thin/heavy；have/has 拥有部位，hair/eyes/face 配上它
核心：be+形容词 / have/has+名词 / wear+穿戴
🔑 G38 · why/because 因果状语
口诀：why 问原因，because 来回答；前因后果要分清，because 句子跟在后
核心：because 和 so 不能连用
🔑 G39 · 描述性形容词
口诀：形容词有两用，修饰名词放前面，作表语放 be 后面
核心：长短→颜色→名词（long black hair ✅）
