语法3考点

语法 3 考点 · Grammar
G40

天气表达句型

How's the weather · What's the weather like · It's + adj.

口诀
"How's the weather 问天气，What's the weather like 也可以；
It's sunny/rainy 来回答，温度用 degrees"
句型公式示例
问天气①How's the weather + in + 地点/时间?How's the weather in Beijing?
问天气②What's the weather like + in + 地点/时间?What's the weather like in summer?
回答It's + 天气形容词It's sunny. / It's rainy. / It's cloudy.
回答(名词)It's + a + 天气形容词 + dayIt's a sunny day. / It's a rainy day.
温度表达The temperature is + 数字 + degrees.The temperature is 25 degrees.
两种问法完全等价
How's the weather? = What's the weather like?（两种问法意思相同）
⚠️ What's the weather like? 中的 like 不可省略——What's the weather? ❌
易错1 ❌ What's the weather? → ✅ What's the weather like?（like不可省）
易错2 ❌ It is sun. → ✅ It is sunny.（回答用形容词不用名词）
易错3 ❌ How is weather? → ✅ How is the weather?（weather前需the）
易错4 ❌ The weather is 25 degrees. → ✅ The temperature is 25 degrees.
防越级约束 仅限基础天气句型，不引入天气进行时与一般现在时的辨析，不引入天气预报高级词汇

语法3考点

语法 3 考点 · Grammar
G41

Could/Would you please 礼貌请求

please 后接动词原形 · 回答 Sure/Certainly

口诀
"Could you please 礼貌求，Would you please 委婉问；
please 后接动词原形，回答 Sure/Certainly 最相称"
句型公式示例
Could you pleaseCould + 主语 + please + 动词原形 + ...?Could you please open the window?
Would you pleaseWould + 主语 + please + 动词原形 + ...?Would you please help me?
Could ICould + I + 动词原形 + ...?Could I use your phone?
Would you likeWould + 主语 + like + 名词?Would you like some tea?
肯定/否定回答
✅ 肯定：Sure. / Certainly. / Of course. / No problem.
❌ 否定：Sorry, I can't. / I'm afraid not.
易错1 ❌ Could you please to open the window? → ✅ Could you please open the window?（please后接原形）
易错2 ❌ Would you please opening the door? → ✅ Would you please open the door?（用原形不用-ing）
易错3 ❌ Could you please helps me? → ✅ Could you please help me?（please后动词原形，不加-s）
防越级约束 仅限 Could/Would you please do sth.? 基础礼貌请求，不引入 Would rather do，不引入 Might/May I...?

语法3考点

语法 3 考点 · Grammar
G42

非人称代词 It 指天气、时间、距离

It's sunny 说天气 · It's seven 说时间 · It's far 说距离

口诀
"It 不只是代词，天气时间距离它；
It's sunny 说天气，It's seven 说时间；It's far 说距离"
用法公式示例
It 指天气It's + 天气形容词It's sunny today. / It's cold in winter.
It 指时间It's + 时间It's seven o'clock. / It's Monday.
It 指距离It's + 距离 (from A to B)It's far from here. / It's two kilometers.
It's time to doIt's time + to + 动词原形It's time to go on a trip.
It's time forIt's time + for + 名词It's time for vacation.
中考辨析 · It's vs. Its
✅ It's = It is（缩写）—— It's sunny.（It is sunny）
✅ Its = 它的（物主代词）—— Its weather is nice.
中考高频陷阱！
易错1 ❌ Its sunny today. → ✅ It's sunny today.（It's = It is）
易错2 ❌ It's time to lunch. → ✅ It's time for lunch. / It's time to have lunch.
易错3 ❌ The weather is sunny. → ✅ It's sunny.（天气句型用 It）
防越级约束 仅限非人称 It 指天气/时间/距离基础用法，不引入 It 作形式主语的高级用法（It's said that... / It seems that...）

Weather Report and Vacation Plans
Welcome to the weather forecast! Today is Monday. Let's look at the weather this week.

On Monday and Tuesday, it's sunny and warm. The temperature is 22 degrees. It's a good time for a trip. On Wednesday, it's cloudy and cool. On Thursday, it's rainy and wet. Please bring an umbrella!

On Friday, it's windy and cold. The temperature is 10 degrees. On the weekend, it's snowy in some places. It's dry and cold in the north, but warm and pleasant in the south.

If you want to go on vacation, summer is the best season. It's hot but perfect for swimming. Have a wonderful time!

阅读理解

上下文推断："Welcome to the weather forecast!" 后面讲了一周天气，forecast是"预报"。

阅读理解

Planning a Summer Trip
Tom and Lily are good friends. They want to go on a trip this summer vacation. The weather is sunny and hot in summer. The temperature is about 35 degrees. It's a perfect time for a trip.

"How's the weather in Sanya?" Tom asks. "It's sunny and warm," Lily says. "The temperature is about 28 degrees. It's wonderful for swimming!"

"Could you please help me plan the trip?" Tom asks. "Certainly!" Lily says. "Would you like to go on Monday?" "Sure!"

They decide to go to Sanya. It's a season of fun. They hope the weather is cool and dry. They want to enjoy the trip. It's exciting!

原文："It's sunny and warm."

阅读理解

原文："Would you like to go on Monday?" "Sure!"

阅读理解

原文："It's exciting!" 说明他们很兴奋。

The Four Seasons Around the World
There are four seasons in a year: spring, summer, autumn, and winter. Different places have different weather.

In spring, it's warm and sunny. ① ______ The temperature is about 15 degrees. It's a beautiful season.

In summer, it's hot and sometimes rainy. ② ______ Near the equator, it's very hot all year. The humidity is high.

In autumn, it's cool and dry. ③ ______ It's a good time for a trip.

In winter, it's cold and snowy in many places. ④ ______ But in the southern hemisphere, it's warm! Climate is different around the world.
📋 五选四（1个干扰项）
A. People need warm clothes.
B. Flowers bloom and trees are green.
C. We can see many games on TV.
D. The leaves turn yellow and red.
E. It's good for swimming.
正确答案：①B ②E ③D ④A（C为干扰项）

✅ What's the weather like in summer?（like不可省略）
第2句 · G41 易错
❌ Could you please to close the window?
✅ Could you please close the window?（please后接原形，不加to）
第3句 · G42 易错
❌ Its sunny today.
✅ It's sunny today.（It's = It is，不是Its）
第4句 · G42 易错
❌ It's time to lunch.
✅ It's time for lunch. / It's time to have lunch.（for+名词，to+动词）
第5句 · G40 易错
❌ The weather is 25 degrees.
✅ The temperature is 25 degrees.（温度用temperature不用weather）

发音要点 oo 有两种发音：/uː/（长元音，如food）和 /ʊ/（短元音，如book）。ew 也有两种发音：/juː/（如new）和 /uː/（如flew）。其中 cool 为本课新词，foot 为 L11 不规则复数词（foot→feet），可形成跨课联动记忆。

练习3 — 看词归音（判断含 oo 还是 ew）

1"food" 含哪个字母组合？
food = f-oo-d，含 oo。
2"new" 含哪个字母组合？
new = n-ew，含 ew。
3"book" 含哪个字母组合？
book = b-oo-k，含 oo。
4"flew" 含哪个字母组合？
flew = fl-ew，含 ew。

练习4 — 最小对立对辨析

5选出含 /uː/ 的词：food / good
food /fuːd/ 含 /uː/（长元音）。good /ɡʊd/ 含 /ʊ/（短元音）。同一组合oo，两种发音！
6选出含 /juː/ 的词：new / now
new /njuː/ 含 /juː/。now /naʊ/ 含 /aʊ/，不含 /juː/。
7选出含 /uː/ 的词：flew / flow
flew /fluː/ 含 /uː/。flow /fləʊ/ 含 /əʊ/，不含 /uː/。
8选出含 /uː/ 的词：cool / call
cool /kuːl/ 含 /uː/。call /kɔːl/ 含 /ɔː/，不含 /uː/。cool是本课新词！

G40
天气表达句型
How's the weather? = What's the weather like? / It's sunny. / The temperature is 25 degrees.
G41
Could/Would you please 礼貌请求
Could you please + 原形 / Would you please + 原形 / Could I + 原形 / Would you like + 名词
G42
非人称代词 It
It's sunny（天气）/ It's seven（时间）/ It's far（距离）/ It's time to do / It's time for sth.
三大口诀汇总
G40"How's the weather 问天气，What's the weather like 也可以；It's sunny/rainy 来回答，温度用 degrees"
G41"Could you please 礼貌求，Would you please 委婉问；please 后接动词原形，回答 Sure/Certainly 最相称"
G42"It 不只是代词，天气时间距离它；It's sunny 说天气，It's seven 说时间；It's far 说距离"
