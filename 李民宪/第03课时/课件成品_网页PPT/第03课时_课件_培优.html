文化旅行 · 本课背景
历史语篇（historical discourse）：讲述过去的人与事，多用一般过去时。本课以"老市场"和"名桥"为例，介绍它们的历史与意义。
旅行语篇（travel discourse）：记录一次旅行，按时间顺序（first→then→next→last）叙述，常用 went / visited / had / saw 等过去式动词。
长句理解：一个长句往往 = 主干（who + did what）+ 附加信息（when / where / how）。阅读时先抓主干，再定位细节，就不怕长句。
时间顺序：历史与旅行语篇常依靠时间词（ago / last year / yesterday / first / then / finally）展开，把握时间词即可理清事件先后。
五步学习路径
步骤1：认识新词与复习词（家具、服饰、旅行场景）。
步骤2：观察一般过去时（was/were、规则 +ed、不规则动词）。
步骤3：训练长句主干提取与细节定位、时间顺序。

步骤5：输出旅行日记或导游介绍，用过去式完整表达。

新词用法速记
building 可数名词：an old building；复数 buildings。如：The traders came to the old building.
trader 可数名词"商人"：trade（贸易）+ -er（人）。如：Many traders sold food here.
wonder 动词"想知道" / 名词"奇观"：I wonder about the past. / The bridge is a great wonder.
difference 名词"不同"，同根词 different（不同的）：the difference between life then and now。
top 名词"顶部"，反义 below：站在 the top of the tower 俯瞰 below（下方）。
wait 动词"等待"：过去式 waited；wait for + 人/物。如：We waited for the bus.
umbrella 名词"雨伞"：take an umbrella；在雨中撑伞 under the umbrella。
wet 形容词"湿的"，反义 dry：My shoes got wet in the rain.
below 介词/副词"在下方"，反义 above：The town looked small below.
hungry 形容词"饥饿的"，反义 full：After the walk, I felt hungry.
场景例句：We waited at the top of the building. It was wet below, so I took my umbrella. I felt hungry after the visit.

语语法 · 一般过去时（历史/旅行语篇）

一般过去时

描述过去发生的事 · was / were

核心规则：讲述历史或旅行经历时，用一般过去时。 be 动词过去式：am/is → was；are → were。如：The town was small. / The traders were busy.
【句型填空】Yesterday I __ at the museum with my friends. （填 was / were）
语篇观察：历史语篇常用 was/were 介绍过去的地点与状态；旅行语篇常用 went / visited / had / saw 记录行程。

过去式规则大全
一、be 动词：am/is → was；are → were。例：The town was small. / The traders were busy.
二、规则动词（+ed）：wait→waited，walk→walked，visit→visited，look→looked，play→played。
三、e 结尾加 d：live→lived，love→loved，believe→believed。
四、辅字母+y 变 ies/ied：study→studied，carry→carried，hurry→hurried。
五、重读闭音节双写：shop→shopped，stop→stopped，plan→planned。
六、不规则动词（须记忆）：go→went，come→came，have→had，see→saw，write→wrote，buy→bought，teach→taught，eat→ate，drink→drank，take→took，sell→sold。
七、否定与疑问：过去式否定用 didn't + 原形（如 didn't go）；疑问用 Did + 主语 + 原形（如 Did you go?）。be 动词例外：wasn't / weren't。

语语法 · 长句主干与细节

读懂长句

先找主干，再定位细节

策略：长句 = 主干（主语+谓语+宾语）+ 附加信息（时间、地点、修饰语）。 例：The young trader / waited / for a long time. → 主干：trader waited。
【长句主干】本题中句子的主语（Who）是？"The young trader with a blue hat waited for a long time at the market."
解析：去掉修饰语 with a blue hat 后，主干是 The young trader waited，所以主语是 The young trader。
【细节定位】上句中的地点（where）是 the ____。（填一个词）
语语法 · 时间顺序

用时间词讲故事

first / then / next / last

时间链：First（先）→ Then（然后）→ Next（接着）→ Last（最后）。 历史/旅行语篇常按时间顺序展开，把握时间词即可抓住事件先后。
把旅行经过按"先→后"正确排序。
Then we had lunchLast we went homeFirst we went to the museum
【句型输出】用一般过去时写一句你的旅行经历（至少用 1 个时间词，如 first/Last）。
提示：past 过去；Last summer 去年夏天；ago 在……之前。均由时间词引出过去事件。
时间词详解
ago"……之前"：放在时间数量后，如 two years ago（两年前）、a long time ago（很久以前）。句中用过去式。
yesterday"昨天"：yesterday morning / yesterday afternoon / yesterday evening。句中用过去式。
last"上一个"：last week（上周）、last month（上月）、last summer（去年夏天）、last night（昨晚）。句中用过去式。
first / then / next / last（顺序）：用于讲故事的第一步、然后、接着、最后。如：First we got up early. Then we had breakfast. Last we went home.
过去时微语篇范例
Yesterday I went to the old market. Many traders sold food and clothes. I waited for my friend, but he did not come. I felt hungry, so I bought a warm cake. Last, I walked home happily.
这段落用 went / sold / waited / did not come / felt / bought / walked 等过去式，配合时间词 yesterday / Last，把一个完整事件讲清楚。

历史与旅行语篇阅读策略
策略一 · 先抓时间：先圈出 ago / yesterday / last / first / then / finally 等时间词，它们决定事件顺序。
策略二 · 再找主干：对每个长句，先分出主语和谓语动词，忽略修饰语，就能快速理解句意。
策略三 · 定位细节：题目问 who / what / where / when / why，就回原文找对应信息所在句，做"细节定位"。
策略四 · 判断推断：推断题不要去原文找原句，而是根据上下文"推测"作者的感受或意图。
策略五 · 巧用工具词：and / but / so / because 提示并列、转折、因果；把握这些词能读懂语篇逻辑。
常见设问与答题句式
细节题：What did the traders sell? → They sold food and clothes.
推断题：How did Tom feel? → He felt happy.
时间顺序：What did they do first? → First they went to the museum.
任务型：根据表格/问题填词或写完整句，答案要完整、用过去式。

阅读 A 篇

历史语篇 · 一般过去时

📄 The Old Market Building
The old market building is in the middle of our town. It is very old. Many years ago, traders came here every morning. They sold food and clothes. People from nearby towns hurried to buy what they needed. One day, a young trader named Tom came to the top of the building. He looked below. He saw many busy people and colourful shops. He wondered about the difference between life then and life now. Tom waited for a long time, but he was not hungry. He was too excited. He had a small notebook, and he wrote down everything he saw. At the end of the day, he put on his jacket and went home. He loved this old market building very much.
精读辅导 · 生词速查
trader 商人 · difference 不同 · top 顶部 · below 在下面 · wonder 想知道
wait 等待 · hungry 饿的 · building 建筑 · wet 湿的 · umbrella 雨伞
长句拆解 · 找主干
句子：Many years ago, traders came here every morning.
主干：traders（主语） + came（谓语，come的过去式）；"Many years ago"是时间状语。
句子：He wondered about the difference between life then and life now.
主干：He wondered；"about the difference..."补充内容；then=那时，now=现在。
时间顺序线索
本段按时间推进：Many years ago（很久以前）→ One day（有一天）→ At the end of the day（一天结束时）。
这些时间词帮助我们按顺序理解历史故事。
【细节题】What did the traders sell at the old market?
解析：第一段 They sold food and clothes，故选 A。
【推断题】Which word best describes how Tom felt about the old market at the end?
解析：末句 He loved this old market building very much，说明他很喜欢、很开心，故选 happy。
【短答】Tom stood at the ____ of the building to look below.（填一个词）

📄 A Trip to the Sea (source: LMX_L03_READ_B)
Last summer, Emma and her family went to a small town by the sea. They stayed in a hotel near the beach. On the first day, they walked to an old building. They walked slowly and talked about the town. Emma went to the top and looked below. The town looked very small. On the second day, it rained. Emma waited under her umbrella, but she still got wet. She felt a little hungry, so her father bought her a warm cake. They visited a museum about local traders. Emma learned about the difference between life in the past and life now. She took many photos. On the last day, they went home. Emma said the trip was the best time of her summer.
精读辅导 · 生词速查

长句拆解 · 找主干
句子：On the second day, it rained, and Emma waited under her umbrella.
主干：it rained + Emma waited；"under her umbrella"是地点状语。
句子：Emma said the trip was the best time of her summer.
主干：Emma said；"the trip was the best time..."是从句（宾语从句）。

本段按天推进：On the first day（第一天）→ On the second day（第二天）→ On the last day（最后一天）。

任务型阅读策略（B篇示范）
信息表填空：先读表格问题，再回原文找对应词。如"On the first day, they walked to an old ____"→ 原文 First day they walked to an old building，答案 building。

问答/推断：找"原因句"。如爸爸买蛋糕，往下找 because she felt hungry，得出 Because she was hungry。

📄 A Famous Old Bridge (source: LMX_L03_READ_C)
The Great Bridge is a famous old bridge in our country. It was built many years ago. Every year, many people visit it. They come to see the tall towers and the wide river below. Long ago, traders crossed the bridge with their goods. They travelled from one town to another. The bridge was very important for them. It saved them a lot of time. Today, the bridge is still strong. People walk across it and enjoy the view. Some visitors wait at the top to take photos. Children feel happy and hungry after the walk, so their parents buy them ice cream. The old bridge is a wonder of our country. It tells us a story about the past. We should take care of it.

be 动词：am/is→was；are→were。
判断技巧：看到 yesterday / last / ago / in the past 就用过去式。

拼拼读 · 过去式 -ed 发音

-ed 三种读音

/t/ /d/ /ɪd/

读音规则：清辅音后读 /t/（walked）；浊辅音和元音后读 /d/（lived）；t/d 后读 /ɪd/（waited, wanted）。 例：walked→/t/，played→/d/，waited→/ɪd/。

-ed 读音规则详表
读 /t/（清辅音后）：walk→walked，cook→cooked，look→looked，stop→stopped，wash→washed，help→helped。
读 /d/（浊辅音和元音后）：play→played，live→lived，open→opened，study→studied，stay→stayed，call→called。
读 /ɪd/（t 或 d 结尾后）：wait→waited，want→wanted，visit→visited，need→needed，start→started，end→ended。
记忆口诀：清后读 /t/，浊后读 /d/，t/d 后读 /ɪd/。先看动词末尾的声音是清是浊，再决定读音。

导游对话

问过去 · 答过去

🎭 场景：你是小导游，向游客介绍老城
游客会问：Was the building old? / Where did you go? / Did you feel hungry? 你需用过去时回答。
【输出】写一句导游回答游客的话（用过去时，如 "We went to the old market."）。
导游对话范例（Role-play）
游客（Tourist）：Was the building old?　导游（Guide）：Yes, it was. It was built many years ago.
游客：Where did the traders come from?　导游：They came from nearby towns every morning.
游客：Did you feel hungry after the walk?　导游：Yes, I did. So I bought a warm cake.
游客：What did you see at the top?　导游：We saw the whole town below us.
问答句型速成
一般疑问（be 动词）：Was/Were + 主语 + …? → Yes, it was. / No, it wasn't.
一般疑问（动作动词）：Did + 主语 + 原形 + …? → Yes, I did. / No, I didn't.
特殊疑问（时间）：When did you go? → We went last summer.
特殊疑问（地点）：Where did you go? → We went to the old town.
特殊疑问（感受）：How did you feel? → I felt happy.
口语提示：回答过去的问题用过去式：Yes, it was. / No, we didn't. / We went to…
