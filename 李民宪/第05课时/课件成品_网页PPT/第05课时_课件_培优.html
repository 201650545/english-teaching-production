can 请求与帮助

情态动词 can

法语法① · can 表能力与请求
Can you + 动词原形 ...?
用 can 礼貌请求对方帮忙，回答用 Yes, I can. / Sure. / No problem.
🔑 请求帮助
Can you help me clean the room?
Can I use your pen?
Sure. / Of course. / No problem.
can + 动词原形
🔑 表达能力
I can cook dinner.
My father can drive a car.
She can't swim.
can 不随人称变化
❌ 常见错误
Can you helps me?
I can to do it.
Does she can sing?
✅ 正确写法
Can you help me?
I can do it.
Can she sing?
口诀
can 后接动词原形，不管主谓第三人称；疑问把 can 提前，回答 Yes/No 加主 + can。

could 更礼貌的请求

情态动词 could

法语法② · could 表委婉请求
Could you please + 动词原形 ...?
could 比 can 更礼貌、更委婉，适合请求长辈或陌生人帮助。
🔑 could 请求
Could you please open the door?
Could I have some water?
Certainly. / Of course.
please 放在 could 后
🔑 can vs could
Can you help me?（熟悉朋友）
Could you help me?（更礼貌）
could 语气更委婉
📋 礼貌请求三步进阶
① 开场：Excuse me.
② 请求：Could you please ...?
③ 道谢：Thank you very much.
⚠️ 不要用 could can 连用，如 Could you can help me?（错误）
要点
Could you please + 动词原形；回答 Certainly / Of course / Sure / No problem。反义否定用 could not = couldn't。

邀请与回应

Would you like...?

法语法③ · 邀请与回应
Would you like + 名词 / to + 动词原形?
邀请别人，肯定回应 Yes, I'd love to. / That sounds great. 拒绝 Would love to, but...
🔑 发出邀请
Would you like some tea?
Would you like to join us?
Do you want to...?
🔑 肯定回应
Yes, I'd love to.
Sure, that sounds great.
Of course, thank you.
🔑 委婉拒绝
I'd love to, but I'm busy.
Thanks, but I can't.
Maybe next time.
❌ 易混
Would you like go?（漏 to）
I like to go.（混淆）
✅ 正确
Would you like to go?
I'd love to.
要点
Would you like + to + 原形；回应 I'd love to.；拒绝时先感谢再说明理由。

方位与家庭场景

方位介词

法语法④ · 方位与家庭场景表达
Where is the book? It's on / in / under / next to the table.
用方位介词描述物品和房间的位置。
🔑 常用方位词
on 在…上
in 在…里
under 在…下
next to 紧挨着
🔑 方位表达
in front of 在…前面
behind 在…后面
between A and B 在两者之间
opposite 对面
🔑 家庭场景
The sofa is in front of the TV.
The lamp is on the desk.
The cat is under the table.
📌 场景表达：The milk is in the fridge. The fruit is on the table. Your coat is behind the door. Can you put it in the wardrobe?
要点
Where is/are 问位置，用方位介词回答；单数用 is，复数用 are。

对话 ①

客人到家前的准备

话对话 · Prepare for a guest

Jack: Sure, Mom. Can I use the sofa cloth?
Mom: Of course. And could you put the fruit on the table?

易错点总结

高频错因

错易错点 · 高频纠错

❌ 常见错误

Would you like go?
The cat is in the table.（应为 on/under）
✅ 正确写法
Can you help me?
Would you like to go?
The cat is under the table.
纠错策略
