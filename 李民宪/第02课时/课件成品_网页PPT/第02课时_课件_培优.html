语法 ① was / were

be 动词的过去式

法一般过去时 · was / were
am/is → was ；are → were
I/He/She/It + was ；You/We/They + were
🔑 肯定
I was at the beach yesterday.
My parents were happy.
was 配单数，were 配复数
🔑 否定与疑问
否定：was not (wasn't) / were not (weren't)
疑问：Was it...? Yes, it was.
be 过去式不借 did
❌ 常见错误
I were bored.
My sister were happy.
Did you happy?
✅ 正确写法
I was bored.（I 配 was）
My sister was happy.
Were you happy?
要点
主语单数（I/He/She/It/单数名词）用 was；主语复数（You/We/They/复数名词）用 were。问句把 was/were 提前，答句用 主格＋was/were。

语法 ① 训练 was / were

语境填空

练was/were 填空
【语法·填空】用 was 或 were 填空：Yesterday I ___ on the beach. My parents ___ there too. The weather ___ fine.
【语法·选择】___ you at home last night? Yes, I ___.
解析：you 疑问用 Were；答句 I 用 was，故选 C。
【语法·填空】用 was / were 填空：My father and mother ___ at home. My sister ___ in the garden. I ___ on the beach.
精讲
判断主语单复数 → 选 was / were；问句 was/were 提前，答句主语用主格。

语法 ② did 问答

一般过去时的行为动词

法一般过去时 · did 疑问与否定
Did + 主语 + 动词原形…？ Yes, 主格 + did.
否定：did not (didn't) + 动词原形
🔑 一般疑问
Did you go there yesterday?
Yes, I did. / No, I didn't.
did 后用原形
🔑 特殊疑问
Where did you go?
What did you see?
Who did you meet?
疑问词＋did＋主语＋原形
⚠️ 高频易错错误率最高
did 后动词用原形：❌ Did you went? → ✅ Did you go?
否定用 didn't：❌ I didn't went → ✅ I didn't go.
be 动词不借 did：Were you happy?（不是 Did you be happy?）
口诀
"过去问句用 did，did 后动词回原形；否定 didn't 加原形，一般疑问主格答。"

语法 ③ 规则过去式

-ed 变化规则

法规则动词过去式 · 四规则
+ed ；+d ；变 y 为 i + ed ；双写 + ed
规则动词在过去时中的书面变化
规则动词原形过去式
一般直接加 -edplay / visit / watchplayed / visited / watched
以 e 结尾加 -dlike / decide / liveliked / decided / lived
辅音+y → 变 iedtry / study / carrytried / studied / carried
重度闭音节双写stop / planstopped / planned
❌ 易错拼写
try → tryed ✗
decide → decided ✓ 但有人写 decidedd ✗
✅ 正确拼写
try → tried
decide → decided
play → played
口诀
"一般加 ed，e 尾加 d，辅音 y 变 ies（过去式 ied），重度闭音双写 ed！"

语法 ③ 训练 规则过去式

拖拽 ＋ 拼写

语法 ⑤ 不定表达

somewhere · anywhere · nothing

法不定表达
somewhere 某地 · anywhere 任何地方 · nothing 没什么
some- 系用于肯定，any- 系用于否定/疑问，no- 系表否定
🔑 somewhere
肯定句：某地
Let's go somewhere warm.
我们去个暖和的地方
🔑 anywhere
否定/疑问：任何地方
I didn't go anywhere.
我哪儿也没去
🔑 nothing
没有什么
I saw nothing interesting.
我没看到有趣的东西
🔄 对应关系重点
somewhere ↔ anywhere：肯定句用 somewhere；否定句和疑问句用 anywhere。
nothing = not anything：I saw nothing. = I didn't see anything.
✗ 双否定： I didn't see nothing. → ✓ I didn't see anything. / I saw nothing.
someone = somebody：某人（肯定句）。
口诀
"some 用于肯定句，any 用于否定疑问，no 直接表没有；nothing 就是 not anything！"

语法综合运用

was/were · did · 过去式 · 不定表达

合一般过去时综合
【综合·选择】Last weekend I ___ to the beach with my family. We ___ very happy.
解析：go→went；We 复数用 were，故选 B。
【综合·填空】补全对话：A: Where did you go? B: I ___ (go) to the park. A: What did you see? B: I ___ (see) a cute girl on a bicycle.
【综合·填空】用所给词的正确形式：Yesterday he ___ (go) to school and ___ (be) late. He ___ (not, have) breakfast.
综合提醒
先判断用 be（was/were）还是行为动词（＋过去式）；be 不借 did；行为动词过去式记得规则或背不规则。

阅读 A 篇 · 正文

A Summer Trip to Qingdao

读阅读 A · 假期对话
A Summer Trip to Qingdao

I went to Qingdao with my family last summer. We stayed in a small hotel near the sea. My parents were happy because the weather was fine. My sister and I were not bored. We walked on the beach and watched the sea every day. We saw seagulls flying over the water and many children playing in the sand.

One morning, we decided to rent two bicycles and ride along the coast. The ride was really enjoyable. My brother took many photos of the sea and the mountains. At noon, we tried some fresh seafood in a famous restaurant. Someone at the next table told us a good place to watch the sunset.

In the evening, we went there and sat on the rocks. The sky turned orange and pink. We felt calm and happy. My grandmother said it was the best holiday she had had. I wrote about it in my diary to remember the trip.

阅读 B 篇 · 正文

A Walk in the Countryside

读阅读 B · 地点感受
A Walk in the Countryside

Last Sunday, my uncle and aunt took me to the countryside. We left home early and walked along a quiet narrow road. My cousin came with us too. The morning air was fresh and clean. We could hear birds singing in the trees, and we saw many green fields and small farms.

At noon, we stopped under a big tree and had a picnic. My aunt made sandwiches, and my uncle brought some fruit. We ate slowly and talked about our favorite activities. I felt relaxed because there was no noise from the city. Everything seemed peaceful and beautiful.

In the afternoon, my cousin and I looked for wildflowers by the river. Someone pointed to a bird flying high above us. We watched it until it disappeared into the clouds. On the way home, I told my parents how much I enjoyed the day. It was a simple but truly enjoyable day away from the city.

My Holiday Diary

读阅读 C · 假期日记（信息表格）
My Holiday Diary

Dear Diary,

Today my family went to a small mountain village. We left home at eight in the morning and took a bus. The village was quiet, and there were many green trees and colorful flowers. My father and mother loved the fresh air. My sister and I were excited, not bored at all.

At ten, we decided to climb the mountain. The climb was hard but enjoyable. At the top, we saw a beautiful lake under the blue sky. Someone took a photo of our whole family. We sat on the grass and had our lunch. We tried some local food, and it was delicious.

In the afternoon, it began to rain. We did not feel sad. We found a small cave and waited under it. My grandmother told us old stories. When the rain stopped, we walked down happily. I wrote everything in my diary that night. It was a wonderful day.
