## 二、语法/能力详案（3 考点）

### 能力①：形容词/副词比较级 -er / more + than

**口诀**：
> "短词加 er，长词加 more；than 一出口，两人比高下"

**公式矩阵**：

| 句型 | 公式 | 示例 |
|---|---|---|
| 短形容词比较级 | 主语 + be + adj.-er + than + 比较对象 | Tom is taller than me. |
| 长形容词比较级 | 主语 + be + more + adj. + than + 比较对象 | Lucy is more outgoing than her sister. |
| 副词比较级 | 主语 + V + adv.-er / more + adv. + than | She sings better than I do. |
| 比较对象人称 | 比较对象用宾格（than me / than him） | My friend runs faster than me. |

**易错点**：
- ❌ Tom is more tall than me. → ✅ Tom is **taller** than me.（单音节短词用 -er，不用 more）
- ❌ Lucy is outgoinger than her sister. → ✅ Lucy is **more outgoing** than her sister.（多音节长词用 more）
- ❌ He is lazy than his brother. → ✅ He is **lazier** than his brother.（辅音+y 结尾：变 y 为 i 加 er）
- ❌ than I 的误用：口语中 than me 即可，书面可保留 than I (am)，本课统一教 than + 宾格。

**防越级约束**：只讲 -er 与 more 两种基本形式，不引入最高级、不规则双音节（better/worse 已作七下复习带过）；名词比较（more books）留至 L08。

---

### 能力②：as...as 同级比较与否定 not as...as

**口诀**：
> "as...as 一样强，not as...as 不如人；中间形容词用原级"

**公式矩阵**：

| 句型 | 公式 | 示例 |
|---|---|---|
| 同级肯定 | 主语 + be + as + adj. 原级 + as + 比较对象 | I am as tall as my friend. |
| 同级否定 | 主语 + be + not as + adj. 原级 + as + 比较对象 | He is not as loud as his brother. |
| 副词同级 | 主语 + V + as + adv. 原级 + as | She works as hard as I do. |

**易错点**：
- ❌ I am as taller as my friend. → ✅ I am as **tall** as my friend.（as...as 中间用原级，不加 -er）
- ❌ He is not so loud as his brother. → ✅ He is **not as** loud as his brother.（否定式固定 not as...as；so...as 仅用于否定旧式表达，本课统一 not as...as）
- ❌ not as...as 与比较级的混淆：He is not as tall as Tom = Tom 更高（与 He is shorter than Tom 同义）。

**防越级约束**：不引入 as...as 修饰名词/数量（as many as）；不引入倍数 + as...as（twice as...as，留至 L09）。

---

### 能力③：人物对比描述句式（Both... / ...but... / ...is more...than）

**口诀**：
> "Both 相同点，but 转折讲不同；is more...than 一比高下，人物对比三件套"

**公式矩阵**：

| 句式 | 公式 | 示例 |
|---|---|---|
| 相同点 | Both + A and B + V（复数） | Both Lily and Lucy are outgoing. |
| 不同点 | A + V + but + B + V | I am shy, but my sister is outgoing. |
| 比较句 | A is more + adj. + than B / A is adj.-er than B | Tom is more hard-working than Jack. |
| 综述 | A and B have sth. in common, but they are different in ... | We are both hard-working, but we are different in personality. |

**易错点**：
- ❌ Both Lily and Lucy is outgoing. → ✅ Both Lily and Lucy **are** outgoing.（both...and 接复数谓语）
- ❌ I am shy but my sister. → ✅ I am shy, **but my sister is outgoing**.（but 后须补全主谓）
- ❌ He is more tall than his friend. → ✅ He is **taller** than his friend.（短词用 -er）

**防越级约束**：不引入最高级句（She is the most outgoing in our class 留至 L09）；both...and 仅限主语位置。

## 五、自然拼读（本课规则）

**规则**：字母组合 `ea` / `ee` / `ie` 发长元音 /iː/，与 `ea` 发短音 /e/ 的对比辨音

| 组合 | 音素 | 示例词 |
|---|---|---|
| ea | /iː/ | eat, tea, read, speak, team, clean |
| ee | /iː/ | see, three, green, sleep, meet, keep |
| ie | /iː/ | piece, field, chief, believe |
| ea | /e/ | head, bread, weather, heavy, ready, health |

**练习**：朗读 18 词 → 辨音归类（/iː/ 与 /e/ 分两组）→ 听音选词 → 拼写。

## 六、课堂游戏

1. **谁更…？比较级擂台**：两名同学上台，全班轮流用 taller / more outgoing 等比较级说出句子抢分。
2. **as...as 对对碰**：把 as tall as / not as loud as 等短语与人物图片配对连线。
3. **人物对比卡**：抽取两张人物卡，用 Both... / ...but... / ...is more...than 各说出 3 句对比。
