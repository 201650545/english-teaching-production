## 二、语法/能力详案（3 考点）

### 能力①：家庭/社区活动表达（borrow/lend 辨析 + give sb a lift + until）

**口诀**：
> "borrow 借入 lend 借出，give a lift 搭便车；not...until 直到才，家庭社区记心头"

**公式矩阵**：

| 句型 | 公式 | 示例 |
|---|---|---|
| borrow sth. from sb. | 主语 + borrow + 物 + from + 人 | I borrow a book from my friend. |
| lend sb. sth. | 主语 + lend + 人 + 物 | Can you lend me your pen? |
| give sb. a lift | 主语 + give + 人 + a lift | Dad gives me a lift to school. |
| not...until | 主语 + not + 动词 + until + 时间 | We don't leave until 8:00. |

**易错点**：
- ❌ I lend a book from the library. → ✅ I **borrow** a book from the library.（借入用 borrow）
- ❌ Can you borrow me your pen? → ✅ Can you **lend** me your pen?（借出用 lend）
- ❌ We leave until the rain stops. → ✅ We **don't** leave **until** the rain stops.（not...until 直到才）

**防越级约束**：不引入 lend sb. sth. 的被动语态；borrow/lend 仅限本结构。

---

### 能力②：祈使句与提建议（Let's / Can you help...? / Don't forget to...）

**口诀**：
> "Let's 一起做，Can you 请帮忙；Don't forget 别忘了，社区服务大家上"

**公式矩阵**：

| 句型 | 公式 | 示例 |
|---|---|---|
| Let's + 动词原形 | Let's + V + ... | Let's clean up the park. |
| 请求帮助 | Can you help me + V + ...? | Can you help me lift the box? |
| 提醒 | Don't forget to + V | Don't forget to take notes. |
| 建议 | Why not + V + ...? | Why not share your toys? |

**易错点**：
- ❌ Let's to clean up. → ✅ Let's **clean up**.（Let's 后接动词原形）
- ❌ Don't forget taking notes. → ✅ Don't forget **to take** notes.（forget to do）
- ❌ Why not to share? → ✅ Why not **share**?（Why not + 原形）

**防越级约束**：不引入 Why don't you...? 与动名词做主语（留至后续）。

---

### 能力③：社区活动信息表述（when/where/who + 活动 + 目的）

**口诀**：
> "时间地点和人物，活动目的讲清楚；when where who what，社区信息全交代"

**公式矩阵**：

| 要素 | 问句 | 示例 |
|---|---|---|
| 时间 | When is the activity? | The clean-up is on Saturday. |
| 地点 | Where is it? | It's at the community center. |
| 人物 | Who can join? | All families can join. |
| 活动 | What do we do? | We pick up rubbish and plant trees. |
| 目的 | Why is it? | We do it to keep our community clean. |

**易错点**：
- ❌ We do it for keep clean. → ✅ We do it **to keep** our community clean.（目的用 to do）
- ❌ The activity in Saturday. → ✅ The activity is **on** Saturday.（星期前用 on）

**防越级约束**：不引入定语从句（who/which 引导的复合句留至阅读专项）。
